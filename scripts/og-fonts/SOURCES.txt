Fonts used only by scripts/generate-og.js to render Open Graph cards.

Source: Google Fonts repository, https://github.com/google/fonts
  ofl/philosopher/Philosopher-Regular.ttf
  ofl/philosopher/Philosopher-Bold.ttf
  ofl/crimsontext/CrimsonText-Regular.ttf
  ofl/crimsontext/CrimsonText-SemiBold.ttf

Both families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The license
texts are kept beside the fonts as OFL-Philosopher.txt and OFL-CrimsonText.txt.

These files are layout inputs, not webfonts. The site's self-hosted webfonts
(roadmap Item 5) are a separate concern.
