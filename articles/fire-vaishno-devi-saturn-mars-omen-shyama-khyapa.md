<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>Guru Shyama Khyapa interprets the <strong>fire at Vaishno Devi Shaktipeeth</strong> as an <strong>ominous sign (ashani sanket)</strong>—a grave warning from the divine realm. The Guru explains that this is part of a larger cosmic pattern: the alignment of <strong>Saturn (King) and Mars (Minister)</strong> creates a terrifying combination that manifests as a "game of fire" across the world. This astrological configuration causes fires to break out anywhere and everywhere, sparing not even the sacred divine lands (<strong>debbhumi</strong>).</p>
<p>The fire occurred in the cave area of Vaishno Devi, and while the main Goddess's idol remained unharmed (a significant protective sign), the smoke and fear caused by the incident were enough to terrify devotees. Guru Baba connects this to similar incidents at other holy sites, including the <strong>Jagannath temple fire</strong>, indicating that everything is interconnected. He references the Puranic legend that <strong>Saturn is currently meditating in the Himalayas</strong> and will eventually return to Vaishno Devi to bow his head. Some legends say Saturn will marry Vaishno Devi, and only then will <strong>Kali Yuga</strong> end.</p>
<p>The Guru makes a profound observation: the <strong>Puranas and Quran align</strong> in their predictions about these end-times. Both scriptures speak of diseases that doctors cannot recognize and other ominous signs. He recalls his prediction about lightning strikes killing many people—27 died in a single day—demonstrating that his warnings about 2021 being worse than 2020 are coming true. The key message is that divine lands are no longer spared from calamities, which is a frightening indicator of the times. However, the fact that the Mother Goddess remains unharmed offers some consolation. This conversation serves as a wake-up call: humanity must stay composed and prepared for what comes next, as the cosmic forces of Saturn and Mars continue their destructive play through fire.</p>
