<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>Guru Shyama Khyapa reveals the profound truth about why seekers cannot see the Mother—not because she is absent, but because her power is so immense that witnessing her directly causes death. Like Ramprasad building his fence, devotees may feel her presence supplying the rope, yet never see the hand that holds it. The third eye (the eye of wisdom), which alone can perceive divine reality, must be opened by the seeker through dedicated sadhana—no Guru can open it for them. During Sandhi Puja, when 48 sacred minutes bridge Ashtami and Navami, the Mother descends to Earth for the brief time it takes a mustard seed to fall from a cow's horn. The terrifying story of the drummer who saw a girl playing hopscotch at midnight and died the next day illustrates the fatal consequences of encountering divine forms unprepared. The Anjali mantras, passed down through fifty-five years of the Guru's Durga Puja experience, offer the only safe way to approach the Mother—through devotion rather than direct vision.</p>
