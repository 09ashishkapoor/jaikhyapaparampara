<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>In this profound discussion on Dharma, Gurudev addresses why the moral teachings of great souls like Krishna, Buddha, Jesus Christ, Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, and Prophet Muhammad fail to take root in modern society. The primary obstacle is the lack of true faith and patience—people have become restless and fickle, running from one guru to another without finding peace. Gurudev cites NASA's satellite imagery of Mount Kailash as scientific proof that "belief often follows vision." While Sanatan Dharma is actually awakening worldwide—even Vishnu temples are being built in Arab countries—the quality of spiritual leadership has declined. Modern gurus often become inaccessible after giving initiation, and the true essence of the teachings is lost. The cycle continues: desperate people take initiation seeking solutions, but without proper guidance and personal transformation, confusion persists. The path forward requires not just taking initiation, but finding teachers who remain accessible and embody the living truth of their teachings.</p>
