<p class="transcript-context">For the first time in more than a year, Guru Shyama Khyapa comes to the Shantikunj temple at Rajpur crematorium, travelling with an oxygen cylinder, for the Annapurna Puja and a Kumari Puja he says he had long wanted to see again. He describes the doctor's reassurance, his fear of going out, seeing Sujit Kar perform the homa, and his tears at returning to the Mother after a year and a few months.</p>

<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>There is no teaching in this episode, only a return. Gurudev comes to <a href="/articles/topics/sacred-places-pilgrimage/">Shantikunj at Rajpur Mahashmashan</a> after a year and a few months, travelling by auto with the oxygen cylinder beside him, and the moment he speaks about is not his health but the <a href="/articles/annapurna-puja-at-the-cremation-ground-service-and-devotion/">Annapurna Puja</a> and the Kumari Puja he had wanted to see once more since Kamakhya and Durga Puja. His fear is documented plainly - of the cylinder running out, of being stuck away from home - and so is the reason he came anyway: "as long as I live, I must come to the Mother." Read it as a devotee's record of <a href="/articles/topics/guru-stories-devotee-lives/">guru bhakti</a> under physical limit, and note the Nar-Narayan Seva and the homa led by Sujit Kar that frame the visit.</p>
