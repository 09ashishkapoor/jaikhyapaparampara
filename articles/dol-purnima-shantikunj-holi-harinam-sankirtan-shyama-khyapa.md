<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>The celebration of <strong>Dol Purnima</strong> at Shantikunj demonstrates that spiritual practice transcends physical location. Even within a crematorium setting, the presence of Lord Narayan and the continuous <strong>Harinam Sankirtan</strong> transform the space into something equivalent to Vrindavan itself. Gurudev's powerful message about the Maha-mantra—<em>"Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare / Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare"</em>—reminds us that in Kali Yuga, the simple yet profound practice of chanting God's names is the most effective path to spiritual purification and protection from the negative influences of the age.</p>
