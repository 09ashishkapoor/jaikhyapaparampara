<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>This profound discourse reveals the deep connection between inner psychology and outer social collapse. The five impulses—<strong>lust, anger, greed, attachment, and pride</strong>—create a cycle of enmity that extends from individual relationships to societal structures. Gurudev's prophecy about the food crisis, echoing Nobel laureate Amartya Sen's thesis, demonstrates that <strong>scarcity is often artificial</strong>; the real crisis is one of distribution and human greed. When food exists but doesn't reach those who need it, we witness not a natural disaster but a moral failure. The humorous story of the <em>khana-kata</em> at the end reminds us that even in dire prophecies, a spiritual master maintains perspective—recognizing that those who spread negativity often do so from their own unresolved struggles.</p>
