<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>In this urgent message, Gurudev highlights the devastating social impact of the COVID-19 pandemic beyond its physical toll. The breakdown of <strong>family bonds</strong>—where a son may not care for an infected father—reflects a deeper spiritual crisis. The warning about the <strong>third wave</strong> being potentially airborne and the description of the pandemic as a "biological war" remind us that human actions have unleashed forces beyond our control. Yet, the core teaching remains: <strong>we must help each other</strong> while taking precautions, rather than abandoning those in need. The rituals at Shantipur demonstrate that spiritual response must accompany practical action in times of crisis.</p>
