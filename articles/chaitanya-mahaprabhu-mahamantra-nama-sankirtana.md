All existing sins will be washed away by this one mantra.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">He said that in the age of Kali, elaborate ritualistic worship is not strictly necessary. Do not worry about complex rituals; they won't be as effective. Just chant this one mantra loudly—perform Nama Sankirtana. If possible, perform Nagar Sankirtana—chanting in the streets. This holy name is the only path to liberation. If you can, perform Nama-Yagya, a sacrificial chanting. This is the reason for your legacy and your peace.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">The Mahamantra is: Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare; Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare. This 16-word, 32-letter mantra is the essence of all spiritual practice in Kali Yuga. Chaitanya did not leave behind material things, but he distributed love. He taught people to respect, devote, and love one another.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Chaitanya is called the Premavatar—the Incarnation of Love. He is the union of Radha and Krishna's sentiment and complexion in one body. NASA has even released an animation showing the 14 worlds, and at the very top, they placed Radha and Krishna. Love is the essence of everything.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">In Kaliyuga, Nama Sankirtana is supreme. Whoever worships the Lord, even if he is a cobbler or someone of low social standing, or even of another faith—I would be compelled to bow at his feet. Because in Kaliyuga, this is the only path. There is no other way. Worshipping various deities without the holy name won't lead to much. One must chant the holy name loudly so no sin can enter.</span>
</div>

If you go to ISKCON today, you will see everyone with chanting beads in their hands, constantly chanting—even while eating or talking. Even now, this practice stands as Chaitanya's immortal gift to humanity.</span>
