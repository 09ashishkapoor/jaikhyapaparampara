<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Kacha obeyed his father and went to Shukracharya's house. Shukracharya was away at the time, but Devayani welcomed him and asked him to wait. Kacha stayed there, serving Shukracharya for a long time.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">He prayed to Mahadev, expressing his desire to establish a sacred place in Mecca. Those who have gone on Hajj or spent time with Dervishes understand that Mecca is a place of immense spiritual power.</span>
