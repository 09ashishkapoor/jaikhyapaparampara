<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">During the time of cosmic dissolution (<em>Pralaya</em>), there was a demon named Hiranyaksha. Brahma granted them a boon so that they could lead a family life and populate the earth with humans.</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">We find references to this in <em>Tantra Sadhana</em>. As a result, a fierce battle took place with the demon. Narayana, in the form of Lord Vishnu, was forced to fight and slay the demon Hiranyaksha. You might have heard of this story.</span>

So, we blended Vedic practices with Tantric practices. We integrated this mantra into Tantra alongside the recitation of the Vedas.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Bhudevi is the original mother. After killing the demon, He brought her to Lord Brahma.
