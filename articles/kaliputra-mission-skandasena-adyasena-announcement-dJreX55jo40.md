On the auspicious day of **Thaipusam**, taking the command of **Deva Senadhipati Kanda** — the divine commander of celestial armies — Shri Praveen Radhakrishna officially declares the **Kaliputra Mission** and its active arm, the **Adya Sena**. This is not an announcement of a new spiritual group. It is the formal declaration of a civilizational mission: to bring **Ma Adya Kali** home, to spread Her name through every algorithm and every platform, and to ultimately seat Her in a temple where no one — regardless of wealth or status — can buy their way into Her *Garbhagriha* (sanctum sanctorum).

### Sri Anand Thakur and the Hidden Vigraham

The story begins approximately one hundred and fifty years ago in Bengal with a largely obscure saint named **Sri Anand Thakur**. He was a *Manasic Shishya* (mental disciple) of **Sri Ramakrishna Paramahamsa** — they never physically met, but Sri Ramakrishna appeared repeatedly in Anand Thakur's visions, guiding him.

Anand Thakur was considered, by most around him, to be mad. In the British-era Bengal where intellectual respectability meant following the path of the Indian Civil Services, a man who sat in corners, had spontaneous visions of Devi, and spoke incoherently about Ma Kali was simply called a social reject. Yet he was, in truth, a deeply empowered *Kali Putra* being prepared for a specific mission.

In a dream, Sri Ramakrishna gave him precise instructions: go to a small water body under a specific tree in the Eden Gardens area, and there you will find a *Vigraham*. In absolute secrecy, Anand Thakur and three companions went, retrieved a small one-foot-tall *Vigraham* of **Ma Adya Kali**, and brought Her home. She appeared immediately *Jagrat* (awakened) — vibrant with presence.

This vigraham was not the fierce, terrifying form people expect. She displayed:

*   **Abhaya Mudra** — the gesture of absolute protection and freedom from fear
*   **Varada Mudra** — the gesture of bestowing boons
*   **Khadga** (sword) raised high in one hand
*   The severed Asura head — which represents the Sadhaka's ego — in another

She was *Saumya* (gentle, graceful) in appearance, even while displaying the most *Ugra* (ferocious) iconography. Her message: *Do not fear Me. I am still the Mother.*

### The Progression of Devi's Forms

To understand why this Vigraham matters, it helps to understand the cosmic arc of **how Devi reveals Herself** as a Sadhaka advances:

*   **Ma Lalita Tripurasundari** — adorned in magnificent jewels and the finest silks, celebrating the heights of physicality. She says: *Pursue it. Gain wealth, property, beauty, abundance. I bless all of this.* She is the Devi of Sri — of prosperity, progress, and fully engaged worldly life.
*   **Ma Tara** — She arrives wearing the garland of skulls, carrying a blue lotus (*Neela Kamala*) and a **pair of scissors**. Do not be afraid; the scissors cut away your attachment to *Rajoguna* (passionate striving). She is beginning to detach you. *"You are not the car. You are not the job. You are beyond that."* She is *Smashana Tara* — not to terrify but to educate.
*   **Kali as Head of the Dasa Mahavidyas** — standing without the flower, without the fine garments. *Digambara.* She is still blessing, still holding Abhaya and Varada Mudra, but the external softness is gone. She is the doorway. She is the Guru of the **Dasa Mahavidyas**.
*   **Ma Adya Kali** — above even the Kali who heads the Dasa Mahavidyas. She is the creator of Kala Bhairava and of the Dasa Mahavidyas themselves. She holds *all Devalokas under Her toes*. She is not restricted to any religion, any race, any Karma Bhumi. Every deity's *Para Brahma Rupa* (highest cosmic form) is Her. She is the Divine Mother of the entire cosmos, from the ant to the president of any nation anywhere on Earth.

### Why She Was Sent Back

Anand Thakur was hit with visions, chaos, and phenomena that he could not digest. He saw in Dhyana that his sacred thread was burning. He received message after message from Ma and from Sri Ramakrishna: *break out from the four heads of Brahma — worship Adya as She wishes, not as the Shastras prescribe.* But the social conditions of that time, the dominance of the four-headed Brahmic norms, made it impossible. Sri Ramakrishna had left the body. Swami Vivekananda was being placed in *Tamas* to prepare for his own role.

Finally, Ma came Herself in a vision to Anand Thakur and gave the most definitive message: *"It is not yet time. Return Me to the Ganga."*

The Vigraham was **immersed** — Her *Visarjan* happened in the sacred river. She was not ready to stay because the world was not yet ready to hold Her in the form She truly wished to be held: not with Abhaya Mudra and Varada Mudra, but with **Khadga and Trishula** — moving forward, not going back.

Sri Ramakrishna's command to Anand Thakur also included a vision of **three temples**:
1. A swan-shaped temple with a *Jagrat Murti* adorned with gold and ornaments
2. A wooden Radha-Krishna mounted upon Garuda
3. **The central one: Ma Adya Kali standing upon the corpse of Mahadeva — Ashta Dhatu (eight metals) Vigraham**

This third temple — and this specific form — is the goal of the Kaliputra Mission.

### The Vigraham That Found Its Way

Four to five years before this announcement, a *Sthapati* (sculptor) near Shri Praveen's home made a Kalika Vigraham. A small crack appeared at the corner of its pedestal. He could not sell it. One of a pair of marble lions he made alongside it also broke — the single remaining lion could not be sold either. Both were left in front of the shop. When Corona arrived and his business stopped, they simply stayed there.

Five years they waited.

One day, without any particular plan, Shri Praveen felt an impulse: *go and get a larger Vigraham.* He went from shop to shop — every one of them wanted advance orders. Ma does not operate by order. She places Herself, and then She calls you to Her. He found Her at the sculptor's shop — intact under that small crack, lion beside Her, both unclaimed for five years, waiting.

He brought Her home. **This is the Vigraham that now stands as the Moola Vigraham** — no Abhaya Mudra, no Varada Mudra. Khadga in one hand. Trishula in the other. The Sadhaka's head held upright. **She is not going back this time.**

**Kanda** (Murugan) arrived on his own terms as well. A Muruga Bhakta in the United States donated His Vigraham. The delivery was supposed to come on a different day — but the delivery person could not find the house. He returned on a **Tuesday**, Murugar's day. He came when the Divine Mother called — not when Shri Praveen called. When the *Abhishekam* (sacred bath) is poured on Ma, the water naturally flows from Her hand onto Kanda placed beside Her. He is drenched by Her grace without a single separate ritual for Him. This is their relationship: the Deva Senadhipati who protects the Kshetra, drenched by His mother's own sacred water.

### The Mission Structure

The **Kaliputra Mission** is open to everyone. Its core purpose:

*   Spread Ma Adya Kali's name through every platform, every algorithm, for **11 months** — posting, sharing, propagating the message without selfish motive.
*   After 11 months of this, by the twelfth month, Ma makes you what you are meant to be: a **Kali Putra**, an embodiment of Her within.
*   The goal is not to build a roster of *shishyas* (disciples). The goal is to build **future Gurus** — people who will talk Her name without fear, without restriction, without an ounce of performance.

The path forward has a clear timeline:

*   **2024** — Sum total eleven: the year of preparation
*   **2026** — The Dasa Mahavidyas, and specifically Guru Kali, the head of the Mahavidyas
*   **2027** — Adya Kali's number (eleven again): the year of Her formation into the Ashta Dhatu Vigraham, placed in the central *Garbhagriha*

When the Ashta Dhatu Vigraham is placed in the public *Garbhagriha*, it will be open to all who wish to come. Later, when the time is right, the original **Moola Vigraham** will be placed in a separate, inner *Garbhagriha* within the same complex — accessible only to those who have done the proper Sadhana, who have completed the Adya Kali *Upasana* Vidhi that takes eleven months of *Deva Prashna* to establish.

### No Money Buys Entry

The message is absolute and final: **no amount of money, status, fame, or power will ever buy access to Her inner Garbhagriha.** 

When the body is placed on the *Smashana* and the *Sahasrara* cracks open — ***Phat!*** — there is no identity left. No powerful houses. No powerful cars. Nothing. At that moment, the first name of Kalika — **Smashana Kali** — smiles at you and asks: *"Do you realize? Do you realize that all of that was nothing?"*

The inner sanctum is only for those who realize this before they reach the Smashana. For those who walk in through Sadhana, not through wealth.

### Conclusion

The Kaliputra Mission is not a five-year plan. It is a **two-thousand-year legacy** — a Sampradaya built root by root, algorithm by algorithm, from the ground up, the way Shanishwara demands: from scratch, with blood, with sincerity, with no shortcuts. Not from a studio. Not from borrowed fame.

A community of *Kali Sadhakas* so bound to each other that the first call in a moment of crisis is not to a relative, but to a sister or brother of the Mission. That is what is being built.

*Jaya Kanda. Kandha Guhane Shanmukha. Bhairava Kalike Namostute.*
