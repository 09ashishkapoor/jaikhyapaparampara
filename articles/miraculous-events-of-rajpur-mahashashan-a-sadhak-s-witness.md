I also met **Nabaneeta Dev Sen**, who sought spiritual guidance regarding her lifespan. I even crossed paths with **Jyoti Basu** while working near his house for 15 years.

More recently, I have been visited by figures like the RSS chief **Keshavji Maharaj** and high-ranking officials from the Home Department. I tell them all: "I work for the Mother."
