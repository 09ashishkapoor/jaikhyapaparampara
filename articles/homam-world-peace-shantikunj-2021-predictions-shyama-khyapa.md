<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>This recording captures a profound moment when Guru Shyama Khyapa's warnings about the year 2021 led to the initiation of a continuous <strong>homam for world peace</strong> (Biswasanti) at Shantikunj. The presence of medical professionals (doctors) participating in the ritual symbolizes the convergence of science and spirituality in times of crisis. Gurudev emphasizes that while his predictions are based on spiritual vision and have historically proven accurate, he offers them not to create fear but to inspire people to return to faith and take refuge in the Divine. The mention of <strong>Gyanganj</strong> and the Himalayas reminds us that spiritual efforts to protect humanity extend far beyond any single temple or individual.</p>
