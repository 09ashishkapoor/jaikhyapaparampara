<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">[01:22 - 02:29] Using Mount Mainak as the churning rod and the serpent king Vasuki as the rope, they began the process. All of these were gathered in one place, with the intention of being divided later.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">[02:30 - 03:30] However, then came the Garal (poison). The question arose: who would consume this poison? Everyone was terrified. Lord Shiva (Mahadev) realized that if this poison fell onto the earth, it would destroy everything—all living beings, Devas, and Asuras alike. He stepped forward and declared that he would drink it.</span>

She intended for only the Devas to have it.</span>
