<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>Ambubachi is not mysticism but applied science: the first rains of Ashar represent Mother Earth's menstrual cycle, triggering creation and abundance. The 51 Shakti Peethas honor this sacred principle, with Kamakhya—"Kam-Akhya"—teaching the conquest of desire. The festival demonstrates how ancient Tantra integrated practical wisdom with spiritual understanding, using rituals to harness natural cycles and purify human consciousness.</p>
