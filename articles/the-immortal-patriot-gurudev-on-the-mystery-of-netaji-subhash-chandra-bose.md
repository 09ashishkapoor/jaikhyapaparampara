It is a matter of great sadness that to this day, so many lies have been spread about the mystery of his death. Falsehoods have been propagated from both government and private sources. We were told his ashes are in Renkoji Temple, but those who truly understand know that truth has been suppressed by those who never wanted India's true independence.

<h2>A Personal Encounter in the Shadows</h2>

I must share something that happened about 35 or 36 years ago. There was a powerful tantric named Dulal Bhattacharya in Malancha. One night, around 11:00 PM, I went to his house with an elder we called 'Kaka Babu.' 

As we entered, Dulal-da’s behavior was strange. He tried to chase us away, which was uncharacteristic of him. In the lawn, a man was sitting with his back to us, holding a transistor radio to his ear, listening to *Voice of America*. He had a lit cigarette. 

When Kaka Babu mentioned his name, the man spoke with an incredibly grave voice: "Why have you come? Go home from here. I am having some difficulty." Frightened by the intensity, we left. Later, Dulal-da told us: **"Do you know who that was? It was Netaji. He was with me in secret for three days."** I saw a surgical scar on his back that night, matching reports of a surgery he had in his youth.

<h2>The Secret of Gyanganj</h2>

Netaji was a master of disguise and a linguist who knew many languages. He had many body doubles. It is said he was seen by Lal Bahadur Shastri during the Tashkent Agreement. 

My firm belief is that Netaji is still alive. He is currently in **Gyanganj**, a mystical place where great saints and sages reside and where age is not a factor. In Gyanganj, there are beings who are hundreds or even thousands of years old, like Babaji Maharaj. Netaji is being nurtured there with great care. 

It is said that when India faces its greatest external threats, he will reappear in the same physical state as when he entered. Age means nothing in that realm.

<h2>The Devotee and the Hero</h2>

Netaji was a great devotee of Goddess Durga. He even performed Durga Puja inside a submarine while escaping. If Swami Vivekananda were alive during his time, the fate of Bengal and the mystery of Netaji might have been resolved differently. 

The suppression of truth regarding Netaji is a shame for our nation, especially for us Bengalis. While he is respected worldwide—even by his enemies—his own land has often failed to honor his legacy correctly. 

Lies do not last forever. One day the truth will be revealed, and those who played games with his legacy will have nowhere to hide. I pray for his health and his timely reappearance to straighten the spine of this nation, which has become bent.
