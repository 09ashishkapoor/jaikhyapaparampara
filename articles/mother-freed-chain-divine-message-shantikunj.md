<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>In this extraordinary account from Rajpur Mahashasman Shantikunj, Guru Shyama Khyapa reveals how the Divine Mother herself intervened to deliver a profound lesson about freedom and attachment. After years of keeping Mother's feet chained—an act born from his devotion and fear of losing her—a mysterious woman appeared wearing the exact same lock and chain on her own feet. Through this supernatural encounter, Gurudev realized that the Divine cannot be bound by human attachments. The message "Unchain me; I am someone who cuts chains" speaks to the fundamental spiritual truth that true devotion requires letting go, not holding on. This experience transformed Gurudev's understanding, leaving him feeling "very light" as both he and the Mother were finally freed from the chains of attachment.</p>
