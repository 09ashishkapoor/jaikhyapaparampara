<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Yes. I had a long-standing desire to see where the Lord of Lords, Mahadev, lives. My mind was incredibly restless with this wish. One day, I reached a place-you could compare it to a coal mine. Imagine the shaft from which coal is extracted; I somehow ended up in a place like that. This happened in a state of deep spiritual intensity, but my desire was very firm.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">I went there and saw a massive hole, and from it, a brilliant, terrifying light was emitting. As I went closer, a gentleman-a huge figure holding a kamandalu (water pot) and wearing rudraksha beads-was guarding that portal. He would not let me pass, no matter how many times I tried. He would just gesture with his hand for me to leave.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">But I didn't give up hope. I thought, "I must go." I stood there for a long time. Eventually, the gentleman called me and told me I could proceed. I was so happy! Once he gave me permission, I entered the hole and went inside.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">I found another world, much like ours, but it was filled with many tall trees. Every tree was glowing with light. There were bulb-like objects hanging from every tree, and they were falling to the ground. As I started walking under the trees, those "bulbs" fell on my body. The light... how can I describe it? It was exquisite. I have never seen such light in my life. The radiance was so beautiful; it felt as if it increased my lifespan by a hundred years. It was so serene.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">I was alone; there was no one else there. I was wearing just my pajama and punjabi. My goal was noble: to find Mahadev. I kept walking and walking. The light there was like the radiance of a hundred suns combined-pure, milky white. It didn't make me tired at all. There was no hunger, no thirst, and no need for sleep. I just kept walking.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">There were small mountains, a few trees, and water bodies. The scenery was so beautiful that my heart was filled with joy. I felt I had reached heaven. I heard a sound-I can't quite describe what kind of sound-and I followed it. It took a long time, maybe a day or two.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">When I arrived at the source, I saw a massive crowd of people. They were singing the name of Mahadev. I saw the great saints there-Ramakrishna Paramahamsa, Bamdev Baba, Kathiya Baba, Goraknath Baba-all the great spiritual masters. They were surrounding a high place where Mahadev was, and they were guarding Him. Their forms were like tigers, and they were all wearing tiger skins. They wouldn't let anyone get close.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">I watched from a distance. The beauty was incomparable. I didn't see Mahadev as a person; I saw a Pillar of Light (Jyoti). A terrifyingly beautiful light that you cannot even imagine. You know the ice lingam at Amarnath? It was like that, but its radiance was beyond anything possible in this world. It was unexpected. I felt I had truly reached heaven.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Devotee:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Gurudev, let me ask you-the description you are giving us is quite chilling. We never expected to hear such a story. These matters are usually very secret. Was this a dream you had?</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">No, this was not a dream. This is the truth. It was not a dream. In a dream, you see many things, but I saw this with my own eyes. I lost myself in it. I didn't see His physical form, but I saw that Pillar of Light.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

They wouldn't let me enter, so I watched from a distance and then eventually came back. It was the greatest sight of my life.</span>
</div>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">No, I request everyone not to think this is a made-up story. This is the absolute truth. I saw this with my own eyes. Such things happen in the lives of great souls.</span>
