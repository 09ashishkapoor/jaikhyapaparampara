The story of Sati's sacrifice and the scattering of her 51 body parts across India established the sacred <strong>Shakti Peethas</strong>, while Shiva's planting of his trident in Kashi created a city that never experiences famine or pandemic. The revelation that Gyanganj—home to immortal sages and the mysterious Jutsu Lama—is accessed through Benares Hindu University, reveals how the spiritual and academic worlds intertwine. The mysterious elderly lady who provides funds for the annual Annapurna Puja reminds us that divine grace often arrives in unexpected forms.</p>
