<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>This beautiful encounter reveals the essential unity underlying all spiritual traditions. The Aghori practitioner Arnibana and Gurudev recognize that whether through Sufi formless meditation or Nathpanthi Aghora practice, both paths lead to the same <strong>formless Brahma</strong>—the radiant, immortal Being in the sky. The technical detail about proper <strong>Sashtanga prostration</strong> (keeping the deity on one's right side) reminds us that spiritual practice has precise traditions for a reason—to properly receive grace and wash away the sins of seven births. Most profoundly, the meeting itself, occurring only after seven attempts, demonstrates that <strong>past life connections</strong> and divine timing guide our spiritual journeys more than we realize.</p>
