Considering my age and the current weather, it is becoming very difficult. Furthermore, people come with serious illnesses—some with Thalassemia, others with tumors, and various other "sinful" diseases. They come to me for help, but I am simply unable to sit for long periods.</span>

It is rising again, as you can see and hear. People don't just come from West Bengal; they come from all over India and even from abroad—America, Canada, France. I do not want this to continue because my health is fragile. As I predicted last year, Corona is taking a dangerous turn again. This "third wave" is going to be far more lethal than the previous ones.</span>
