Most seekers living in *Samsara* are full of desires — the wish to prove themselves to neighbors, to fulfill a parent's unfulfilled dream, to build wealth and legacy. These desires are not wrong; they are the designs of the body, the *Karma* encoded in the blood. But the critical question is: to whom does one pray for wealth? To the intermediary, or to the **Source**?

### Kubera's Borrowed Authority

**Kubera** is worshipped across Jainism, Buddhism, and Hinduism as the deity of wealth. He has been granted the ability to bestow riches. But Kubera did not attain this power on his own. He spent **10,000 years** in the deepest forests meditating upon **Swarnakarshana Bhairava** before being given the boon of *Dhanada* — the one who can give the gift of wealth.

Kubera is a distributor, not the originator. Wealth attained through Kubera is always **borrowed** — it carries *Karma*. When you rise through Kubera's path, you step on others' toes. There are hundreds who wished for your spot, and their jealousy and *Nazar* (evil eye) create negative *Karma* that must eventually be burned.

**Mahalakshmi**, by her nature, is never stable in one place. She arrives and then moves. This is the nature of *Shakti* itself.

### The Source of All Wealth

**Vatuka Swarnakarshana Bhairava** is seated on the **Golden Throne**. He is not depicted as an ascetic in a forest — he is the one who has taken control of the entire wealth of *Mrityuloka* and provides it to Mahalakshmi and Kubera. When you go to Him, He gives you wealth that is **earned in this realm and burned in this realm** — across all realms simultaneously. He is the only one who grants wealth without requiring karmic payback across lifetimes.

He tells Brahma: *"Whatever path you have shown for living in grandeur — building wealth, properties, bloodlines, and exceptional sons and daughters — I take control of this."*

### The Three Representations

*   **Standing with the White Dog**: Swarnakarshana Bhairava stands adorned in jewels and gold, his *Vahana* (vehicle) the white dog elevated to the status of the Grand Vahana — the glorious divine white light. He has taken the status of wealth from Brahma's design. Pray to Him as if you are already Him.
*   **Giving Wealth to Kubera and Mahalakshmi**: He stands distributing wealth — to Kubera on the right, to Mahalakshmi on the left. This representation is ideal for women who lack the external Bhairava Tattva or are distancing from their father. Seeking grandeur directly from Bhairava provides the *Adhara* (support) that is otherwise missing.
*   **Seated on the Golden Throne with Bhairavi**: The ultimate form of the *Grihastha* (householder). Bhairavi sits comfortably on his lap. He is not in the *Smashana* — he is in the malls, corporate offices, political battlegrounds, parliament, and cricket stadiums. He is the married man who holds the inclination to further his bloodline, pursue promotions, and rise higher in career. This representation is excellent for the recently married, those seeking properties, or a long married life.

### Going to the Source

Do not pray to the agents. Do not get caught by intermediaries who will distract and throw you off the path. Kubera gives wealth, but he is not the originator. When you take from someone who is not the source, what you hold will always be borrowed — always bound by *Karma*.

There is a man sitting with leprosy in front of a shut-down shop, covered in flies, his body stinking of decaying flesh. He has money in his bowl, but nobody will touch it. He cannot walk to a shop even with money in hand. This is what wealth beyond one's *Karma* looks like when the bill comes due. Every toe you step on while climbing through borrowed wealth will demand payment.

**Go to the Source.** Swarnakarshana Bhairava is the one who gave the power to Kubera. When you pray to Him, your wealth is earned and burned within the same realm — across all realms. He is aware of who you are across every dimension. There are no middlemen, no borrowed authority, no karmic debt.

Take 5 or 10 *Sankalpas*, bring the Bhairava within, and He will show you the path — whether it leads to a partner, a career, or a granddaughter in 2120. Rise above the limited thought of one lifetime's wealth. Go to the one who gave wealth to Mahalakshmi and Kubera themselves.

**Bhairava Kalike Namostute.**
