<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>Guru Shyama Khyapa offers a somber reflection on Dol Purnima (Holi) coinciding with a Blood Moon eclipse—a phenomenon he calls "Noshto Chand" (Corrupted Moon). While reminiscing about the joyful traditions of the past, he expresses deep concern about the festival's modern degradation through alcohol and chemical colors. The Blood Moon, he warns, portends massive bloodshed and conflict. Drawing from the Bhavishya Malika and scientific predictions, he discusses the possibility of humanity's departure from Earth by 2027, nuclear warfare, and the reversal of natural laws. This conversation serves as both a nostalgic look at lost traditions and a grave warning about humanity's precarious future.</p>
