Wrap that yellow thread around the Peepal tree seven times. Tie it well. Then, worship the tree with flowers and water. Make a vow (manat); the Gita says that the Peepal is the best among trees. Not the Banyan, but the Peepal. Tell the tree, "O Peepal tree, please arrange my marriage, and I will offer puja in return."</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">If someone can do this regularly—though you only tie the thread once—and offer water and prayer every day, it works. Wrap seven strands of white thread dyed with turmeric—making it yellow—around the tree, and offer flowers, water, and sweets just as you would worship God.</span>

Her mother was crying to me, saying, "She is of marriageable age; won't I ever see a grandchild?" But the daughter is adamant; she won't marry. I asked her why, and she said that given the current state of society, it's better not to marry. It's better to be alone than to deal with court cases and legal battles.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Young men are also showing this reluctance. They say women aren't "good" anymore. Men come to me repeatedly saying their wives have left for their father's house and are demanding a lot of money.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Even twenty-five years! Yesterday, a lady from Bombay came to my house. She was crying to me, saying, "I call him, and he tells me to go get admitted to a hospital; he won't come back." I asked her what she did to make him so angry, and she said she did nothing. They are not humans; they fall into the category of animals.</span>

Many have had children this way, I won't deny it. But in some cases, like a girl from Burdwan who tried twice, it didn't work.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">In those cases, we suggest a rare tree called the Jayanti tree. Jayanti is associated with Kartikeya. Kartikeya grants children, as does Brahma. Kartikeya is the alternative to Brahma. We worship Kartikeya for children. That tree is his abode. If someone can find the root of that tree—it's very rare—it usually works.</span>

<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">No, that is a matter of personal mindset. Divorce used to be common among Muslims—the "Triple Talaq." I don't have a "medicine" for that, but to provide some peace, I give them certain objects to wear or keep nearby. It has helped many people settle their disputes.</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Guru-ji, another thing we see is extreme unrest in the household. Sometimes it's financial or due to lack of education, but even in families that have everything, there is no end to the fighting. What is the cause?</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">It's just the household dynamic; people cannot tolerate one another. The shops often sell the red one, but you must find the white one. Its root is very beneficial for domestic peace.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Interviewer:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Parents also come to you because their children don't want to study.</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

I give them a traditional preparation from here to help the children focus on their studies and become more obedient.</span>
