<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>What makes this talk distinctive is where Gurudev places his worry. The disciple brings war, drought, and famine; Gurudev answers them, but keeps returning to one question, what becomes of the <em>Brahma Padartha</em> if <a href="/articles/jagannath-leaves-puri-blood-idol-kalki-avatar-shyama-khyapa/">Jagannath leaves Puri</a> and no <em>Nabakalebara</em> renews the image. His answer to the age is not a strategy but a manner of living: "live honestly and virtuously," keep a simple righteous life, and trust that the great souls who have passed will return. The forecast of <a href="/articles/topics/prophecy-world-events/">Satya Yuga beginning in 2027</a> and of a much smaller population is offered as what the <em>Bhabishya Malika</em> says, not as a claim of his own. Read the article as devotional teaching about cycles of time; it is not prediction, medical, or safety advice. For the wider theme see <a href="/articles/brahma-padartha-jagannath-divine-heart-puri-temple/">the mystery of the Brahma Padartha</a> and <a href="/articles/four-dhams-extinction-prophecy-jagannath-puri-shyama-khyapa/">the four Dhams and the prophecy of extinction</a>.</p>
