<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>This introduction by Subho Bhoumik reveals the essence of Gurudev Shri Shri Shyama Khepa Maharaj's teachings: the philosophy of <strong>Paropkar</strong> (selfless service to others). Through the timeless Sanskrit verse from Bhartṛhari, we understand that just as trees bear fruit, rivers flow, and cows give milk—all for the benefit of others—so too is our human body meant for serving others, not for selfish indulgence. Gurudev embodies this principle by taking upon himself the diseases and sufferings of those who come to him, offering a living example of what true Indian spiritualism represents: <strong>"Aham Brahmasmi"</strong>—the realization that we are one with the Supreme, expressed through compassionate action.</p>
