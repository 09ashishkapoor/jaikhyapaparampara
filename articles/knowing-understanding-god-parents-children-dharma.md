<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>The fundamental truth that "the biggest mistake of your life is not knowing or understanding God" serves as the foundation of this discourse. Gurudev explains that while parents create the body, it is God who places the "packet" of life force (Pran) into the child at birth—an act that would kill the parents if they attempted it themselves. God is established on Truth (Satyameva Jayate), and where there is no truth, God cannot reside. The article powerfully emphasizes that parents' primary duty is to introduce their children to God, not to amulets or tantriks when problems arise. The epidemic of restless, disobedient children stems not from a lack of charms but from parental neglect—mothers and fathers too busy earning money, leaving children with caretakers who pacify them with smartphones. Drawing from the Mahabharata, Gurudev shows how Dhritarashtra's blind love for Duryodhana destroyed the Kuru dynasty. Like produces like: a mango tree produces mangoes, not jackfruit. Parents' characters are inherited by their children. The coming "biological war" and diseases that science cannot cure make it imperative that we teach the next generation to know and understand God—their true controller and creator.</p>
