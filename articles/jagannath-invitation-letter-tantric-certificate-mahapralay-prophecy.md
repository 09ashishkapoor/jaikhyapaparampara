We are conducting a program for 'Jagannath Darshan' all over India, and he has appointed me as the spokesperson. I act as a bridge, like the Howrah Bridge, connecting the devotee and the Lord. That is what I am working toward. Jai Guru.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

This is all by the grace of the Mother (Goddess Tara).</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">I believe Lord Jagannath is the first and greatest Tantric. One cannot practice Tantra without his dictation. There is no greater Tantric in the world than Him. Lord Krishna's heart is within Him. That is why I have visited Puri 45 or 50 times to gain spiritual strength. He gave me the 'certificate' that I can practice Tantra. People from Canada, France, Germany, Japan, and even Russia come to me to learn Tantra.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Recently, people from Russia visited. Anyway, they come and are satisfied. Pavankumarji also came to see me. He wouldn't say anything other than 'Jai Shri Madhav.' He showed me a lot of respect. I accepted that respect.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">However, one thing that saddened me is that his treasury was broken. Now, a 'Mahapralay' (Great Catastrophe) will begin. I don't know how it will be organized or how people will be protected, but a catastrophe is coming because his treasury was violated. I am very worried about how this will unfold. Every day, about a hundred people come to me. They trust me and believe in me. Some even ask for my permission before doing simple daily tasks. This is why I am concerned about how He will bring about this destruction and how He will manage it.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">He is the Lord of the Universe, Jagannath. We all breathe because of Him. He is the Lord of the Supremo. Like our parents, even if they don't give fruit as they age, they still provide shade.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

As I said, He gave me the identity of a Tantric. I have visited many times, read the Gita, and even performed a 'Homa' (Fire ritual) at His temple. Many people in Puri know me.</span>
</div>
