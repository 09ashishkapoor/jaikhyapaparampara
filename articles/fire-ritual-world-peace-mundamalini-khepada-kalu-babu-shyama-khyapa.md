<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>This sacred fire ritual at Mundamalini Tala exemplifies the true spirit of Tantric practice—performed not for personal gain but for world peace and human welfare. The presence of Kalu Babu, the divine dog who was companion to Bamdev Baba, lying peacefully on Khepada's lap during the ritual signifies the harmony between all beings in the presence of sincere spiritual practice. Khepima's testimony of being called by the Great Father to serve at the cremation ground reminds us that the Divine chooses and protects those who dedicate their lives to service. The combination of Vedic and Tantric methods, performed daily with fire as the primary worship, demonstrates the living tradition that Gurudev and his disciples maintain for the welfare of all beings.</p>
