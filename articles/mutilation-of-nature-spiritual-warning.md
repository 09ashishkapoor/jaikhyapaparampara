<h2>The Fatal Decade: 2022 and the Void</h2>

Many thought that once 2021 passed, we would be saved. But 2022 holds events that I cannot even speak of explicitly for fear of spreading panic. We are seeing volcanoes erupting in the ocean, tsunamis threatening coastal cities like Kolkata, and earthquakes of unprecedented magnitude. This is a cycle. No one has the power to control these forces. The Earth is gradually declining, and nature will continue to torment us from all sides.

<h2>Averse to God: The Toll of Frustration</h2>

<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">People come to me asking, "What is the point of calling God when such suffering persists?" I understand their frustration. Because the search for a true human has ended from God's side, the pull is no longer mutual. This averseness is a symptom of a deeper spiritual gap that cannot be easily repaired.</span>

<h2>The Only Path Remaining</h2>

Is there a stone, a ritual, or a Rudraksha that can save the Earth? No. Even when Lord Krishna lived in Dwapara Yuga, his own home went underwater. What is destined to happen will happen. God has already determined the outcome. Our only recourse is constant prayer—to call upon the Divine every single moment, even if it feels like He is not listening.

<h2>A Message to the Devotees</h2>

I am currently confined due to my health and the doctor's orders, and it pains me mentally to see how much people are suffering. I receive hundreds of calls daily, and hearing their troubles without being able to go to the Mother's cremation ground to perform the work for them is a heavy burden. But remember, the Earth's lifespan is not yet over. We must endure this cycle with patience and prayer.
