The Pandavas once asked Lord Krishna about the future of the Kali Yuga—what would it be like?</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Yes, they asked Krishna about the future. Krishna told them, "Go into the forest and spread out in four different directions. </div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">First, Yudhisthira went and saw a strange sight: an elephant with two trunks playing in the water. He returned to Krishna and said, "Lord, I saw something I cannot understand—an elephant with two trunks. What does this mean?"</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Krishna replied, "You have seen correctly. In Kali Yuga, people will be of two kinds—or rather, two-faced. They will say one thing but do another. Their words will not match their actions. This will be the character of the people."</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Next came Bhima. He saw a cow licking its newborn calf with such intensity that the calf began to bleed. Despite the blood, the cow wouldn't stop licking. Bhima asked, "Lord, I saw a cow licking its calf so hard that it bled. What is the meaning of this?"</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Krishna said, "In Kali Yuga, parents will love their children so blindly that they will cause their downfall. </div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Then came Arjuna. He said, "I saw a vulture. It was eager to eat rotting meat, yet on its wings, the entire Vedas were written. It was obsessed with carrion despite carrying such holy wisdom."</span>
</div>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Krishna replied, "This represents the scholars and priests of Kali Yuga. They will speak great words and quote scriptures, but their minds will be fixed on worldly greed and 'rotting' desires. Many who call themselves saints will be hypocrites focused only on material gain."</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Then Nakula returned. He said, "Lord, I saw a massive boulder rolling down a mountain. It crushed trees and everything in its path. But then, it was stopped by a tiny, fragile blade of grass. How is that possible?"</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Krishna explained, "The boulder represents the sins and the immense weight of the Kali Yuga. No wealth or power can stop the downfall. However, that tiny blade of grass represents the Name of God. In this age, only the constant chanting of God's name can stop the momentum of destruction and save a person. Those who remember God will find protection."</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Finally, Sahadeva came. He said, "Lord, I saw many shallow wells that were full of water. But the largest, deepest well in the center was completely bone-dry. Why?"</span>

</div>
