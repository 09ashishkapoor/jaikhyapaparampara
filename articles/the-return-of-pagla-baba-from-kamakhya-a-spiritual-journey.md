On November 26, 2020, Santu Acharya—whom we call 'Pagla'—a secret seeker and disciple of Syamakhyapa of this Shantikunja, started his journey on foot to Kamakhya. Today is March 28, which means we are seeing Pagla after almost four months and four days. He went to Kamakhya on foot, performed his spiritual practices (sadhana) there, and has now returned. Just moments ago, we witnessed our Guru, Sri Sri Syamakhyapa, welcoming him in a way we have almost never seen before. It was a rare and beautiful sight of a Guru honoring his disciple. Gurudev, if you could please speak about the spiritual significance or the rituals behind this.

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Spiritual matters and the relationship between Guru and disciple are profound; it is rare to witness such an encounter. A Guru must always protect his disciple. We can say Pagla has reached a new stage of elevation, and so we welcomed him by washing his feet and offering garlands, lamps, and incense.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">This is an ancient tradition of India. When the Guru sent the disciple there, the Guru's presence reached the Mother through him. It wasn't just for Pagla's own spiritual growth. We have seen from the beginning that the Guru's goal here is world peace and human welfare. It is about the upliftment of the entire human society and delivering them from danger. Gurudev practices and performs rituals for this daily. Despite his age and physical ailments, and even amidst the severity of the pandemic, we couldn't stop him. His only aim was to stand by the helpless and distressed during these times.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Yes, for public welfare. Sacrificial fires (Yajnas) and prayers were held here almost every day. By the Mother's grace, we haven't seen a single case of COVID in this Shantikunja. It couldn't enter here.</span>

Vlogger: Yes, we witnessed the temptations of the road. Gurudev was anxious at every moment. He would constantly ask anyone who came here where Pagla had reached that day. He was always eager to hear from him; talking to him seemed to give Gurudev mental peace. After Pagla reached Kamakhya, Gurudev suggested someone visit him. I went there and met him, and we have brought you videos of our conversations during my stay there. Everything happened under Gurudev's instructions. He told me to go there and arrange his stay and place of practice. 

Then Pagla finished his sadhana. There was a time when news reached Gurudev that Pagla's mother was bedridden at home. We wondered if he would return, but he remained firm in his resolve. He finished his practice and has returned to Shantikunja today. His future life of spiritual practice is a lesson for all of us. He has our best wishes. Now, I'll ask Gurudev to say something to him.
