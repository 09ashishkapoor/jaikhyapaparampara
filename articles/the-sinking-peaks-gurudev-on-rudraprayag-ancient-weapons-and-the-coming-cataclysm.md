<h2>The Hidden Artifacts of Mahabharata</h2>

Many people ask about the weapons and armor of the Ramayana and Mahabharata. They still exist. Karna’s armor and the 'Akshay Kamandalu' are said to be hidden within secret chambers of the **Konark Sun Temple**. These artifacts, crafted by Brihaspati and Vishwakarma, were placed where the first ray of the sun falls on Earth. Scientists today are discovering secret underground chambers in major temples, often fleeing in fear from what they find.

<h2>The Role of Brihaspati and Shukracharya</h2>

Brihaspati, the Guru of the Devas (Jupiter), protects the Earth by absorbing or deflecting negative celestial forces. On the other hand, Shukracharya, the Guru of the Daityas, possessed the *Maha Mrityunjaya Mantra*, a power so great that even the Devas tried to steal it through Kacha. These cosmic teachers still influence the destiny of our world.

<h2>The Non-Aryan Origins of Shiva and Jagannath</h2>

There is a significant point that Mahadev and Lord Jagannath are considered non-Aryan deities. Mahadev is called 'Kirat' (a hunter), and both He and Durga took forms as 'Kirat-Kirati' or 'Sabar-Sabari.' Jagannath, with His large eyes and absence of hands and feet, reflects these ancient, non-Aryan roots.

<h2>Warnings of the Kalki Avatar and Critical Years</h2>

The next six years are critical for humanity. It is written in the *Jagannath Bhagavata* and the *Bhavishya Malika* that Lord Jagannath will not mount His chariot for these six years. The **Kalki Avatar** will appearing for destruction, as the influence of Kali Yuga reaches its peak. 

The seven Immortals (*Chiranjivis*), including Hanuman, Ashwatthama, and Vibhishana, are still alive and will join the final battle. Ashwatthama has been seen worshipping Mahadev in forests, and Vibhishana still visits the Puri temple in secret.

<h2>The Imminent Cataclysm</h2>

Badrinath will not remain, and **Kailash is slowly sinking**. As it sinks, global water levels will rise. We are facing a massive earthquake, potentially 8 or 9 on the Richter scale. The cracks in our roads and the sinking of our lands are the final warnings. There is no escape for humanity from these unfolding events.
