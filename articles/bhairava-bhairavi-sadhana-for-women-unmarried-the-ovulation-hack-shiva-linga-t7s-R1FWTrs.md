Eight out of every ten emails from women carry the same question: "What if my monthly cycle arrives during a *Sankalpa*? Am I impure? Am I disqualified?" This doubt has been so deeply ingrained by **Brahma's** Vedic design that it requires the most fundamental *Procedural Collapse* to dismantle. The answer lies not in theology but in science — and in the **Shiva Linga** that sits on the hump of a bull.

### The Body Is the Yantra

In the path of **Bhairava**, the body itself is the *Yantra* (sacred instrument). Your monthly cycle does not keep you away from the divine — it is the *Shakti* of creation itself. There is nothing impure within you. The cycle that Brahma's design calls "inauspicious" is, in Bhairava's path, the very key to unlocking the *Shakti* within.

### The Shiva Linga and the Bull

To understand the science, one must first understand the **Shiva Linga**. It is not what many assume. The answer lies in the **Kangayam** breed of bull — specifically the *Karampasu*, a fully black specimen found in less than 0.5% of the breed. The entirely black Kangayam bull — black inside the ears, the feet, the underbody — is considered an *Amsha* (aspect) of **Shiva** himself.

When a bull stands and looks at you with reverence, its hump beautifully represents the Shiva Linga. Seen through the two horns of **Nandishwara**, the hump is the *Swayambhu* (self-manifested) Lingam. What is the hump? It is a repository of fat and **testosterone**. The more testosterone-loaded the bull, the more magnificent and perfectly shaped the hump becomes — a natural, self-manifested Lingam.

But the moment a bull is **castrated**, the testosterone is removed. The hump loses its black color and becomes white. It becomes frail, fragile — like a deflated balloon. The shape of the Lingam disappears entirely.

The black Lingam seen in temples, worshipped and revered — that color comes from **testosterone**. Remove the testosterone from the bull, and the Shiva Linga on its back ceases to exist. Testosterone is the Shiva Tattva.

### The Woman's Cycle: Estrogen, Progesterone, and Testosterone

A woman's monthly cycle runs 28 to 32 days, mirroring the Moon's 29.5-day cycle:

*   **First 15 days**: Dominated by **Estrogen**.
*   **Last 15 days**: Dominated by **Progesterone**.
*   **Ovulation (Day 10–14)**: The window where the woman's body naturally produces the **highest testosterone**.

During ovulation, a woman is at her most powerful creative state. She is flying — whatever her field, she is at peak performance. This is the phase where the body naturally generates the male hormone — the **Shiva Tattva** — within the woman herself.

The 10th to 14th day coincides with the Moon becoming full and brightest — the *Pournami* (Full Moon). The Moon is within you. Your own cycle is within you.

### The Internal Bhairava Tattva for Unmarried Women

For women who have decided — by choice, by *Karma*, or by trauma — that they do not need the physical presence of a man in their life, the **Bhairava Tattva** can be unlocked from within.

When a woman is married, her husband becomes the external Bhairava Tattva. Fifty percent of her *Sadhana* goes to him. But for the unmarried woman, the Bhairava Tattva is already present inside her — peaking during ovulation, when testosterone is at its highest.

That feeling of absolute power, creative fire, and invincibility during days 10–14 — **that is the Bhairava Tattva within you.** You are supposed to breed it, expand it, and hold it until it becomes your permanent state of being. That *Adhara* (foundation) is what allows you to seat *Devi* within.

### The Actionable Hack

For unmarried women (and married women who wish to try):

*   Track your cycle and identify your ovulation window (typically days 10–14).
*   If that window coincides with a **Sunday** or **Tuesday**, that is ideal for beginning.
*   Around day 9 or 10, begin **Swarna Akarshana Bhairava Sadhana** for 11 days.
*   Observe whether you can retain the level of positivity, creative *Shakti*, and stability that the internal Bhairava Tattva provides during this peak window.

This is not about being at the mercy of the Moon. It is about using the body's own cycle — the *Yantra* — to unlock what already exists within. *Shiva* and *Shakti* are one and the same. The hormones that create the Shiva Linga on the bull are the same hormones that peak within the woman during ovulation. Those who said the monthly cycle was inauspicious were following Brahma's design. In the path of Bhairava, what was called inauspicious is actually the most auspicious.

### Conclusion

The *Procedural Collapse* that Bhairava embodies breaks every rule Brahma established. A woman's body is not a disqualification — it is the most powerful *Yantra* in creation. The testosterone that makes the bull's hump a Shiva Linga is the same hormone that peaks during ovulation, creating the internal Bhairava Tattva within every woman. Count your days from the 11th day of your cycle, begin your *Sadhana*, and pray to the Bhairava that already lives within you.

**Bhairava Kalike Namostute.**
