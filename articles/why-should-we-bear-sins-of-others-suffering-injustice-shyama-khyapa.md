<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>Gurudev Shyama Khyapa addresses one of humanity's most profound questions: <strong>Why do innocent people suffer when dishonest people prosper?</strong> Drawing upon the wisdom of saints like Ramprasad and the teachings of the Gita, he reveals that God's justice operates on a precise timeline. The dishonest may flourish temporarily, but divine retribution is certain. More importantly, Gurudev emphasizes that spiritual seekers have a duty to protest injustice—not as a political act, but as a sacred obligation. Silence in the face of wrongdoing is itself a crime. By maintaining righteous actions and refusing to be corrupted by bad influences, we protect ourselves and contribute to society's moral fabric.</p>
