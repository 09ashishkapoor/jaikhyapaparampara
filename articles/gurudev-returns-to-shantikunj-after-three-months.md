<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Yes, since the day this temple was founded, that instruction has been written: "Do not give a single rupee to this temple." Just bring some rice or lentils for the Mother (the Goddess). That will provide her meal, and whatever is left over will be shared among the people as `prasad.' That is perfectly fine.</span>
