<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>This article introduces the fifth book about Guru Shyama Khyapa's life, written and edited by Dr. Sujit Kar under the pen name "Saptarshi." The name originated from a profound spiritual experience where the Seven Sages (Saptarshi) constellation appeared to Dr. Kar during meditation, identifying him as one of their own. The book, published in Mumbai by Dr. Kalyan Roy, chronicles Gurudev's biography, supernatural experiences, and spiritual journey. The story of Saptarshi exemplifies how divine recognition comes through sincere practice—Basudev Jagannath Himself provides the ultimate "certificate" of spiritual attainment.</p>
