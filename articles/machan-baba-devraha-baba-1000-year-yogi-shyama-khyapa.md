He was a great devotee of Lord Ram. He would address everyone as "Child" (Baccha). Those who saw him would see the likeness of Hanumanji in him. He had such light and power in his eyes that he could have overturned the world. He passed away 30-32 years ago. It's a loss for us, even though he lived for a thousand years. As far as I know, he shared a loving embrace with Tulsidas.</span>
