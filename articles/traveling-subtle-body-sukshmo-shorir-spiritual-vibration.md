<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>The phenomenon of Gurudev's subtle body (Sukshmo Shorir) traveling to devotees who have never met him physically reveals profound mysteries of yogic science. Like the stone thrown into a pond creating ripples that reach the shore, Gurudev emits a spiritual vibration—an invisible radiation that expands in ever-widening circles. Those who enter this vibrational field experience his presence: "You came, you ate, you blessed me." The mystery of Gyanganj, where great yogis appear dead yet sit preserved for 70-100 years while their souls work elsewhere, demonstrates the same principle—the body remains intact while the soul performs its cosmic duties. Gurudev's firm Sankalpa (resolve) shared with the Divine Mother drives this phenomenon: "Wherever there is human suffering and tears, I will go." Through constant Kriya performed day and night—even while appearing to smoke or look away—his soul travels to those in need. This "spiritual electricity," like the electric power of ocean fish, operates beyond ordinary perception. With nearly 15 million YouTube views and devotees from Canada, Australia, England, France, and Japan coming after seeing him in dreams, the reality of this vibrational presence becomes undeniable. Once caught in this net of divine vibration, the connection becomes permanent—and ultimately, for the devotee's highest good.</p>
