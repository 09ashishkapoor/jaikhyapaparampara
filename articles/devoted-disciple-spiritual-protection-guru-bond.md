<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gargi-di:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">To put it briefly is difficult because it is quite detailed. I took initiation from him. The Guru's salutation mantra says: Gurur Brahma, Gurur Vishnu, Gurur Devo Maheshwara—Guru is Brahma, Vishnu, and the Great Lord Shiva. It is impossible to fully explain the Supreme Brahman. For me, Gurudev resides in both my speech and my heart.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gargi-di:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">After my initiation, I experienced supernatural things from him. No matter how far away I was—whether in the dead of night at home or anywhere else in the world—I always felt his presence and guidance. Through his will, I was able to surrender myself completely. Not everyone can do that; it was my good fortune that he made it happen.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gargi-di:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">I was at home, and I had a thought about something related to Gurudev's teachings. I thought to myself that I should ask him about this specific topic. Believe it or not, two days later, I saw him in a video answering that very question—the exact question I had thought about. I had not told anyone. Isn't this a profound spiritual connection? Can science explain this? He is something far beyond what people view him as.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gargi-di:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">A very close relative of mine caused a lot of trouble at my home. One night, he even raised his hand against me. I was devastated and cried all night. The next morning, Gurudev called a devotee and put me on the phone. It wasn't a time he would usually call. When I spoke to him, he just said, "I know, I know everything." Even without me telling him, Gurudev understood everything.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gargi-di:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">He taught me how to truly call upon God. I am an absolute devotee. By his grace, I live a good life. I have a three-story house in Kolkata, and he has left me wanting for nothing. Whether I am alone at night or in danger, he knows. He is the Supreme Power.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">I have many disciples, but she is special. She always looks after me. She keeps track of my health. If I have a slight cough, she calls the doctor. I always tell my disciples: focus on your karma. Do not worry about results. If someone insults you, it does not matter. Do such work that your name is written in gold letters. She is special and always keeps a check on me, calling many times a day to ask how I am. I deeply respect and love her.</span>
</div>
