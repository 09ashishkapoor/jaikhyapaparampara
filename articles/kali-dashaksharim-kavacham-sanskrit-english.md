I am offering this Kavacha back into the ether, so that others like me may benefit from its knowledge, draw closer to Ma Adya Mahakali, and receive Her motherly protection through the intense and transformative journey of Her path.

I found this Kavacha myself through what appeared to be a great coincidence, at a time when I was calling out deeply to Ma Adya. So if you have arrived here, perhaps your arrival is no coincidence either. Take it as a quiet nod from the unseen realm.

Do not let doubt stop you. Step forward.

Ma is kind. She teaches in Her own way.

Even if today you approach Her words like a stammering fool, struggling with every syllable, continue. With Her grace, the same tongue may one day recite with confidence, and the same mind may begin to discover extraordinary depths of knowledge hidden within even the simplest recitation of a Kavacha.

You do not need to begin as a scholar.

Begin with love.  
Begin with surrender.  
Begin with courage.

Let Ma teach you the rest.

**Be a Veera.**

**Darna Mana Hai.**

The heart and essence of the Kavacha is very simple: “Ma Kali, wherever I am and in whatever condition I am, surround me completely with Your presence and protection.”

<h2>The Prologue</h2>
<h3>Narada Speaks</h3>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">kavacham shrotumichami tam cha vidyam dashaksharim.<br/>

</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
I wish to hear this sacred Kavacha and the ten-syllabled Vidya of the Goddess.<br/>
O Lord, you are all-knowing; therefore, please teach me this sacred knowledge of Bhadrakali.
</div>
</div>

<h3>Narayana Speaks</h3>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">shrunu narada vakshyami mahavidyam dashaksharim.<br/>
gopaniyam cha kavacham trishu lokeshu durlabham॥</div>
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Listen, O Narada. I shall reveal to you the great ten-syllabled Mahavidya.<br/>
This Kavacha is deeply secret and exceedingly rare even throughout the three worlds.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">hrim shrim klim kalikayai svaheti cha dashaksharim.<br/>

</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Hrīṁ Śrīṁ Klīṁ Kālikāyai Svāhā — this is the sacred ten-syllabled mantra of Ma Kalika.<br/>
Sage Durvasa once bestowed this sacred teaching upon a king at Pushkara on an auspicious solar occasion.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">dashalakshajapenaiva mantrasiddhih kruta pura.<br/>
panchalakshajapenaiva pathan kavacham uttamam॥</div>
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
In ancient times, through ten lakh repetitions, the mantra was brought to perfection.<br/>
And through five lakh repetitions together with the recitation of this supreme Kavacha, its protection was perfected.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">babhuva siddhakavacho'py ayodhyam ajagama sah.<br/>

</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Having attained the perfected protection of the Kavacha, that king came to Ayodhya.<br/>
Through the grace of this Kavacha, he became victorious throughout the whole earth.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Listen, O noble sage. I shall reveal this supremely wondrous Kavacha.<br/>
Long ago, Narayana compassionately gave this Kavacha to Shulin — Lord Shiva, the Bearer of the Trident.

<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
It was given for Shiva's victory during the terrible destruction of Tripura.<br/>
Later, Lord Shiva gave this very Kavacha to Sage Durvasa.
</div>
</div>

</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Sage Durvasa then bestowed it upon the great soul Suchandra.<br/>
It is an extremely secret sacred truth, regarded as the very embodiment of the multitude of mantras.
</div>
</div>

<h2>Protection of the Body</h2>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">hrim shrim klim kalikayai svaha me patu mastakam.<br/>
klim kapalam sada patu hrim hrim hrim iti lochane॥</div>
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Hrīṁ Śrīṁ Klīṁ — may Ma Kalika always protect my head.<br/>
May Klīṁ protect my skull and forehead, and may Hrīṁ Hrīṁ Hrīṁ protect my eyes.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>

</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
O Three-Eyed Mother, invoked through Hrīṁ, please always protect my nose.<br/>
O Ma Kalika, protect me, protect me; please always protect my teeth.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">hrim bhadrakalike svaha patu me'dharayugmakam.<br/>
hrim hrim klim kalikayai svaha kantham sadavatu॥</div>
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
O Ma Bhadrakali, invoked through Hrīṁ, please protect both of my lips.<br/>
Hrīṁ Hrīṁ Klīṁ — may Ma Kalika always protect my throat.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>

<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Through Hrīṁ, may Ma Kalika always protect both of my ears.<br/>
Krīṁ Krīṁ Klīṁ — may Ma Kali always protect my shoulders.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">krim bhadrakalyai svaha mam vakshah sadavatu.<br/>
krim kalikayai svaha mam nabhim sadavatu॥</div>
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Through Krīṁ, may Ma Bhadrakali always protect my chest.<br/>
Through Krīṁ, may Ma Kalika always protect my navel.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">hrim kalikayai svaha mam prishtham sadavatu.<br/>
raktabijavinashinyai svaha hastau sadavatu॥</div>
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Through Hrīṁ, may Ma Kalika always protect my back.<br/>
May She who destroyed Raktabija always protect both of my hands.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">

hrim chamundayai svaha sarvangam me sadavatu॥</div>
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Hrīṁ Klīṁ — may Mundamalini, She who wears the garland of severed heads, always protect my feet.<br/>
Through Hrīṁ, may Ma Chamunda always protect my entire body.
</div>
</div>

<h2>Protection in Every Direction</h2>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">prachyam patu mahakali agneyyam raktadantika.<br/>
dakshine patu chamunda nairrutyam patu kalika॥</div>
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
May Mahakali protect me in the East, and may Raktadantika, the Red-Toothed Goddess, protect me in the Southeast.<br/>
May Chamunda protect me in the South, and may Kalika protect me in the Southwest.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">shyama cha varune patu vayavyam patu chandika.<br/>
uttare vikatasya cha aishanyam sattahasini॥</div>
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
May Shyama protect me in the West, and may Chandika protect me in the Northwest.<br/>
May Vikatāsya, the Fierce-Faced Goddess, protect me in the North, and may Sāṭṭahāsinī, She whose laughter resounds powerfully, protect me in the Northeast.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">urdhvam patu lolajihva mayadya patv adhah sada.<br/>
jale sthale chantarikshe patu vishvaprasuh sada॥</div>
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
May Lolajihva, the Goddess with the lolling tongue, protect me above; may Adya Maya, the Primordial Divine Power, always protect me below.<br/>
Whether I am in water, upon the earth, or in the space between, may the Mother and Source of the entire universe always protect me.
</div>
</div>

<h2>Phalashruti — The Fruits and Glory of the Kavacha</h2>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">iti te kathitam vatsa sarvamantraughavigraham.<br/>
sarvesham kavachanam cha sarabhutam paratparam॥</div>
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
My child, I have now revealed to you this Kavacha, which is regarded as the embodiment of the multitude of sacred mantras.<br/>
It is said to contain the very essence of all Kavachas and to be supremely exalted among them.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>

kavachasya prasadena mandhata pruthivipatih॥</div>
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Through its grace, King Suchandra became lord of the seven regions of the earth.<br/>
Through the grace of this Kavacha, Mandhata became a great ruler of the earth.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">pracheta lomashash chaiva yatah siddho babhuva ha.<br/>
yato hi yoginam shreshthah saubharih pippalayanah॥</div>
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Through it, Pracheta and Lomasha are said to have attained spiritual accomplishment.<br/>
And through it, Saubhari and Pippalayana became renowned among the foremost yogis.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">yadi syat siddhakavachah sarvasiddhishvaro bhavet.<br/>

nischitam kavachasyasya kalam narhanti shodashim॥</div>
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
One who attains perfection in this Kavacha is said to become endowed with the highest spiritual accomplishments.<br/>
Even great acts of charity, austerities, and sacred vows—<br/>
—are traditionally praised as not equalling even one-sixteenth of the spiritual merit of this Kavacha.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Transliteration</div>
<div lang="sa-Latn">idam kavacham ajnatva bhajet kalim jagatprasum.<br/>
shatalakshaprajapto'pi na mantrah siddhidayakah॥</div>
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
If one worships Kali, the Mother and Source of the universe, without knowing this Kavacha—<br/>
—the text declares that even an immense number of mantra repetitions may not bestow mantra-siddhi.
</div>
