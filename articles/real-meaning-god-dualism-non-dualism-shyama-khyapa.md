<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>Guru Shyama Khyapa offers a nuanced perspective on the nature of God and faith. Using the story of Trailokya Swami, he illustrates that God's existence is ultimately a matter of belief—atheists and believers alike shape their reality through their convictions. He distinguishes between healthy faith and blind faith, emphasizing that spirituality cannot be demonstrated like a science experiment. The teaching explores the tension between Dualism (Dvaitabad) and Non-Dualism (Advaitabad) through the historical lens of Ramakrishna and Vivekananda, while also highlighting society's hypocrisy in worshipping goddesses while marginalizing women—a theme he passionately decries.</p>
