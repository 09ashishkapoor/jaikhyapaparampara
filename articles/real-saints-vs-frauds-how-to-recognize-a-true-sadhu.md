**Interviewer:** Pranam, Gurudev. Are Mahakali and Param Brahma one and the same?

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">They are definitely one. Param Brahma is within you. Through meditation (*Dhyana*), you will realize this. The real Brahma is asleep within you; awaken Him. Unless you become Param Brahma yourself, you won't understand the Mother.</span>

**Interviewer:** Gurudev, what is the difference between a "Bhondo Sadhu" (fraud) and a real Sadhu?

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">To recognize them, you must test them. Ask Pointed questions. A real one will answer instantly. Sainthood is in the mind, not the clothes. Those who make a business out of God are frauds. Real Sadhus speak through their eyes and use very few words. If your luck is strong, a real Sadhu might appear and just give you a glimpse (*Darshan*)—that is enough.</span>
</div>

**Interviewer:** What are Raj Yog and Bhakti Yog?

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Raj Yog is the Yoga of Kings—performing rituals with grandeur and pomp. But Bhakti Yog is the essential one. God is attained through tears and devotion, not display. As the song says, "How many nights have you stayed awake for God?" Bhakti is the greatest path.</span>
</div>

Today, many people fall into the traps of frauds who target vulnerable families and demand money to "fix" their problems. They destroy families with impossible conditions. Be careful of these businesses made in the name of God.
