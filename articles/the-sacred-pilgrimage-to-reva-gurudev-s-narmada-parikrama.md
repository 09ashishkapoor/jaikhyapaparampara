**Student:** Pronam, Gurudev. Please tell us about the Narmada pilgrimage.

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>

We stayed at the Bharat Sevashram Sangha in Amarkantak. At the Mother's Temple, there are "Narmada Yantras" where seekers meditate. But be careful—there are two monkeys there who will slap away anyone they deem impure!</span>

We visited Barfani Baba, a great devotee of Krishna. Every morning, a monkey comes to him and performs *Pada-Seva* (worshipping his feet). It is truly peculiar and divine. We also saw the Pataleshwar Temple and the house of Saint Kabir.

The Narmada Parikrama (circumambulation) used to be through dense jungles filled with bears and hostile tribes, but now it is safer. I meditated daily at the Mother's temple. It is a place where God truly walks. Narmada is Shiva’s place, and the river is said to be His teardrop.

We visited Kapildhara and Dudhdhara, where water falls over a Shiva idol inside a cave. At the "Mother's Garden," I met a Naga Sadhu who understood Bengali perfectly. He told me to sit and remember "Ma Reva"—there, they don't call her Narmada; they call her Reva.

**Student:** Gurudev, why do seekers perform this Parikrama?

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Because Narmada is Shiva's place. All seekers must set foot there. I sat for meditation at Bhrigu Kamandalu and had a vision of the Mother. The locals believe the Mother keeps a sharp eye on everyone who visits, ensuring they are cared for. The Reva chant is the only one that truly matters there.</span>
</div>
