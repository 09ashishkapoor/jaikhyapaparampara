<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>Guru Shyama Khyapa reveals the profound connection between Mars (Mangal) and Mother Bagalamukhi, the golden-complexioned goddess who paralyzes enemies by pulling their tongues. The Guru emphasizes that Mars represents auspiciousness—hence the term "Mangalik"—yet modern society introduces inauspicious elements (meat at weddings) that disrupt this sacred energy, leading to the high divorce rates observed today. The remedy lies not just in gemstones like red coral, but in understanding that even the planets themselves are engaged in constant Kriya Yoga, worshipping their chosen deities. Mother Bagalamukhi, seated upon Ekavaktra Shiva with her stone mace, represents the power of Stambhan—paralysis of negative forces—while demanding precise ritual observance from her devotees.</p>
