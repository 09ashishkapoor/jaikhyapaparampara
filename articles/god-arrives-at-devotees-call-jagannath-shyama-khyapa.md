<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>The arrival of Lord Jagannath at Guru Shyama Khyapa's temple is a testament to the ancient spiritual law: <strong>"God belongs to the devotee."</strong> When physical limitations prevent a sincere seeker from reaching the Divine, the Divine finds a way to reach the seeker. This episode serves as a reminder that sincerity and a selfless heart are the only requirements for experiencing the presence of God.</p>
