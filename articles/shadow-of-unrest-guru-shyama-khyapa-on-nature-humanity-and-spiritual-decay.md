<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

Now, I am a man of the spiritual line; it’s not possible for me to handle everything. These are medical matters, yet they come to me saying, "We’ve seen many doctors but couldn't find a cure. We’ve come to you as a last resort."</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

Even now, people can’t sit in the sun or go out; they are drenched in sweat. It’s an intolerable state of nature.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">And humans are reacting the same way. Nature is controlling man—there's no doubt about that. Just as nature has become erratic, humans have no stability either. They are wandering around in a frenzy, going here and there.</span>

I saw you on YouTube and came for a blessing."</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">But the frenzy I see in West Bengal... sometimes I get very angry and speak harshly to people. I try to see five or ten. But daily, 50 to 100 people are waiting.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

I tell them clearly: "Don't bring your family's unrest to me or to the crematorium. You can adjust these things yourselves if you want." You don't need amulets or charms for that. But even then, they insist, "No, I can't handle it, this and that..."</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

I told her to come back in the morning, and I would cleanse her. I did that for her. So, there’s this element of ghosts and spirits too.</span>
</div>

Then there are those who can't have children; they also crowd around me.</span>

Many people return them, offer prayers to the Mother [Deity], and leave.</span>

You come every day and sit before the Mother. Do you think giving her fruit will fix everything?"</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

Because people aren't following these boundaries, they are the ones making the environment toxic. Nature is reacting to this.</span>

But now, this same environment is "torturing" you so much that you can't even stand up. It’s all related. Nature and man are deeply connected. If nature doesn't help man, man cannot survive. But humans don't care about that. "I will do whatever I want," they say. And then they come to Tantriks like us. When they come to me, they get scolded. I ask them, "Why have you come to this crematorium to die?" I tell the women and the men: "Why have you come to this crematorium? Go die somewhere else."</span>

I hear stories of people spending 10,000 or 20,000 elsewhere and getting nothing, then coming here. The problem is, those who wear saffron robes don't necessarily know anything. They just wear red clothes, and people get attracted and lose their money.</span>

People are in such a frenzy they don't care about money; they’ll give 10,000 or 20,000 just to get their work done. They want to "buy" peace with money.</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

I don't know how long this will go on, but people have become truly frantic. Peace is gone. I’ve said it before: peace is leaving this world. As days go by, malice and jealousy increase. You wouldn't believe it—neighbor against neighbor, house against house. There is no harmony. Everyone suspects everyone else. "He must have done black magic on me," or "He went to a specialist to ruin me."</span>

If this is human nature, how can they sit before God? And even if they sit, they have a huge mobile phone in their hand. I ask, "What are you doing? You're sitting before the deity and fiddling with your phone?"</span>

The common man has to suffer the consequences. When kings go to war, the commoners get trampled.</span>
