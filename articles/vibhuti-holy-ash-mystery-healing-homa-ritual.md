<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>Vibhuti, the sacred ash from Homa rituals, holds both scientific and spiritual significance that extends far beyond what meets the eye. As Gurudev explains, the combination of Bael wood (rich in phosphorus, calcium, and potassium), Sandalwood, and Bhurja creates a chemical reaction that increases oxygen levels and produces healing carbon. When mixed with white sandalwood paste and applied while chanting the Mahamrityunjaya Mantra, this ash becomes a powerful remedy for skin diseases and chronic pain. The article also reveals Gurudev's profound commitment to his 1,000-day Mahamrityunjaya Homa—a vow that transformed into three years of unwavering dedication, interrupted only once by a mysterious clairvoyant at Jagannath Temple who reminded him never to abandon his spiritual station.</p>
