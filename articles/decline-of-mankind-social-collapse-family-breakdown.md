<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>In this deeply concerning discourse, Guru Shyama Khyapa witnesses firsthand the rapid decline of social values and family structures in modern India. Where devotees once came seeking healing from physical ailments, now 80% of visitors come with divorce cases—wives who have left husbands, husbands who have abandoned children, and families torn apart by infidelity. Gurudev identifies multiple causes: mobile phone addiction enabling secret relationships, women earning independent incomes leading to unstable character, excessive "open" socializing before marriage, Western cultural influences, and the loss of traditional dress and values. He observes with pain how children now bully parents for iPhones, threaten suicide if denied, and speak to their parents as enemies. The mixing of men and women in workplaces has become "like wood and fire." Gurudev declares that conscience is the main thing—a person without conscience is better off dead. The era of Sati-Savitri and Sita has passed, replaced by a society where parents may soon vanish into "Hi, Hello" relationships. His stark warning: "Sin is like water; it binds itself. The more you sin, the more you will be trapped."</p>
