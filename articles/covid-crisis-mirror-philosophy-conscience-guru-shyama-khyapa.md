<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>In this heartfelt conversation during the COVID pandemic, Guru Shyama Khyapa shares the profound <strong>"mirror philosophy"</strong>—the practice of looking into one's own conscience to determine right from wrong.
