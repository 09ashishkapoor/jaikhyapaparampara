<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>Guru Shyama Khyapa delivers a profound discourse on forgiveness (Kshama) as the highest virtue declared in the Bhagavad Gita. Yet he observes with sorrow that modern society, having lost the essential qualities of shame, hatred, and fear, no longer possesses the capacity to forgive. The breakdown of family units, the objectification of women, and the rise of "leftover" lives where relationships are discarded like Ucchista (remnants) reveal a civilization in slow decay. The Guru's message is both a warning and a call to return to the sustaining Dharma of forgiveness—one that requires understanding that ultimately, we own nothing and take nothing with us, making the capacity to forgive the true measure of spiritual maturity.</p>
