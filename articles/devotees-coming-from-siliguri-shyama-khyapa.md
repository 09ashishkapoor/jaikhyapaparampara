<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom: 20px;"><span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight: bold; color: #333;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span> <span class="speech-text">Actually, I was supposed to go there myself. But looking at the condition of the roads and the weather, I didn't feel confident about traveling. I really intended to go there. They held a large puja during Janmashtami and were expecting me. Anyway, I sent Pagla Baba to represent me. However, it seems they aren't fully satisfied unless I go myself—that's the issue. With the current weather and road conditions, how can I travel? Even the trains are uncertain right now. They visit once or twice a year, stay here, eat, offer their prayers to the Mother, and then head back. Among them are Jibon and Rakesh. They want to say something. Jibon, please speak. Tell us why you've come and how you feel after being here for five days.</span></div>
<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom: 20px;"><span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight: bold; color: #333;">Jibon:</span> <span class="speech-text">Greetings to my fellow brothers and Gurudev. Six of us took initiation (diksha) about a year ago during the lockdown. On that day, Gurudev told us that a spiritual center (kshetra) would be established in Siliguri. Today, we can feel that a center has already been formed there. Thousands of people are waiting for Gurudev. In these times, people are in search of a true Guru. Devotees from Bihar, Kishanganj, and even Nepal have come; they were initiated yesterday. I want to say that what we have received from Gurudev in this past year is beyond imagination. I don't have the words to describe it. The spiritual wealth he has given us with both hands is something we can never exhaust. I can say this with a guarantee. The brothers who came yesterday for initiation will soon realize the difference between a true Guru and others. Our lives have been blessed, and so will theirs. That is all I have to say.</span></div>
<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom: 20px;"><span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight: bold; color: #333;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span> <span class="speech-text">Well said. We have a player here, Rakesh Singh. He has played for Mohun Bagan and East Bengal in Kolkata. He has played on many grounds and is still playing. He is a very good boy. He took initiation from me last year during the lockdown. I'd like him to say a few words.</span></div>
<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom: 20px;"><span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight: bold; color: #333;">Rakesh:</span> <span class="speech-text">My respects, Gurudev. Since taking initiation, what we have received from Gurudev and the Mother is something we can never finish.</span></div>
<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom: 20px;"><span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight: bold; color: #333;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span> <span class="speech-text">Tell them where you have played.</span></div>
<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom: 20px;"><span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight: bold; color: #333;">Rakesh:</span> <span class="speech-text">I have played for Mohun Bagan, East Bengal, the Junior National team, the BSF Sporting Club, and in the Premier League. My affection for you grew from that moment. Whenever you call, I talk about you or Jibon. You two are maintaining the Siliguri center. I will definitely visit; there is no doubt about that. Let the weather improve a bit, and I will go with Dulal. I am committed to visiting because so many people call me from there. It makes me very happy.</span></div>
<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom: 20px;"><span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight: bold; color: #333;">Rakesh:</span> <span class="speech-text">Many people are waiting for you, Gurudev. They keep asking when you are coming.</span></div>
<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom: 20px;"><span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight: bold; color: #333;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span> <span class="speech-text">Does anyone else want to say anything?</span></div>

What can you say about that, Dulal-da?</span></div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom: 20px;"><span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight: bold; color: #333;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span> <span class="speech-text">I will go there and give initiation to whoever I can.</span></div>

People don't realize that everything they need is already within them. It's like the musk deer that wanders looking for the scent of musk, not realizing the scent comes from its own body.</span></div>
<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom: 20px;"><span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight: bold; color: #333;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span> <span class="speech-text">Exactly.</span></div>

People come and ask for this or that, but the solution starts from home once you connect with Gurudev. You don't even have to call him.</span></div>
<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom: 20px;"><span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight: bold; color: #333;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span> <span class="speech-text">That's right.</span></div>
<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom: 20px;"><span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight: bold; color: #333;">Disciple (Dulal):</span> <span class="speech-text">Jibon, I've told Gurudev many times that I bow before your devotion. If such devotion exists, why wouldn't things happen? I seek answers from Gurudev, but he doesn't say much. However, I see the results through you. Whatever you pray for happens. I personally respect your devotion deeply.</span></div>
<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom: 20px;"><span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight: bold; color: #333;">Jibon:</span> <span class="speech-text">Where there is devotion, there is victory. That is my belief.</span></div>
<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom: 20px;"><span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight: bold; color: #333;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span> <span class="speech-text">It's true. In the beginning, people don't understand. They only come to me when they want something. They ask for blessings or for specific need to be met. I give what I can in the name of the Mother, and they get better. I don't take any money for this; not a single penny. People think that if I give them something physical, they will get better. But I tell you, the moment you tell me your problem, the process of healing begins. For example, when Puja Singh was admitted to the hospital, Rakesh called me. That was enough. Soon after, she was fine and returned home. People can't always grasp this, but those who come to us and even just touch us will find their purpose fulfilled.</span></div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom: 20px;"><span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight: bold; color: #333;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span> <span class="speech-text">That is also true. Let things proceed as they are. Does anyone else want to speak?</span></div>

I will go in the future. Many people told me not to go right now, but I feel a great need to be there.</span></div>
