<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>The Satyanveshi—the truth seeker—does not speak from imagination or fear, but from direct perception through Kriya Yoga. The water calculation method, using a stone bowl filled with water, allows Gurudev to see future events as images appearing in the water. This is not prophecy based on divine revelation but a scientific-spiritual process that anyone can develop through dedicated practice. As Kali Yuga ends and Satya Yuga begins, the collision between ages creates natural upheaval. The tornadoes, floods, and disasters are the "wind of the changing era," cleansing the old world to make way for Satya Yuga where truth, non-violence, and righteousness will prevail. Those who survive this transition will be the virtuous ones who become the seeds of the new world.</p>
