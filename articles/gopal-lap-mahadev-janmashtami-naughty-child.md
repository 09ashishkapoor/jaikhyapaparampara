<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>This brief Janmashtami glimpse captures the eternal playfulness of the Divine Child. Gopal (Krishna), the cowherd who leads cattle to the fields, sits momentarily in Gurudev's lap after temple rituals—yet his mischievous nature cannot be contained. Even as he rests, he knocks over lamps and creates delightful chaos. The name "Gopal" itself speaks to this identity: protector and leader of cows, yet forever the playful child who steals butter and hearts alike. On this auspicious Janmashtami, Gurudev's lap becomes a throne for the naughty divine, reminding us that the Highest can also be the most playful, and that mischief and divinity often dance together.</p>
