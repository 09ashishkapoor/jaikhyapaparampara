<h2>The Rama and Krishna Avatars</h2>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Disciple:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">The new Ram Mandir in Ayodhya is being inaugurated. Rama came in the *Treta Yuga*, and Krishna came in the *Dvapara Yuga*. What is the difference between them, and what about this temple?</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Krishna said in the Gita that He comes in every age to protect the righteous and destroy the wicked. Regarding the Ram Mandir, there is much discussion about the rituals. In my view, such rituals are traditionally the domain of Brahmins, but we must also look at the larger cycle of avatars.</span>
</div>

Our Lord Narayana has ten incarnations. From the *Matsya* (Fish) to *Varaha* (Boar), and then through *Narasimha* and *Vamana*. *Parashurama* taught the use of weapons, then came the *Rama* avatar. In the list of ten, *Balarama* is often cited as the incarnation after Rama, followed by Buddha and finally Kalki.

<h2>The Emergence of Kalki Avatar</h2>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Kalki is coming with immense power. To succeed, He will need the seven immortals (*Chiranjivi*)—Markandeya Rishi, Hanuman, Parashurama, King Bali, Vibhishana, Kripacharya, and Ashwatthama. Without them, the war cannot be won, as it must be fought on all fronts.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Disciple:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">According to *Bhawishya Malika*, the Kalki avatar has already been born and is staying in a secret place with 80,000 *Narayani* soldiers.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Yes, that has been said. This transition period will continue until around 2050. The *Kalki* era, or the end of it, will take time. In the Ramayana, Rama used the help of Hanuman and others—beings of nature—to kill Ravana. In *Dvapara*, Krishna's play was primarily with humans. But in *Kali Yuga*, sin and virtue are imbalanced—three parts sin and one part virtue. This imbalance will lead to divine intervention.</span>
</div>

<h2>The Great Destruction and Survival</h2>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">The Earth will not be completely destroyed, but many will perish. God will not protect those with bad characters. As *Bhawishya Malika* suggests, only a small portion of the population—around 63 crore worldwide—will survive. New diseases and wars are already entering our world. The war will be terrible but short—a "war of one day" that will be lead by those who were kept away from previous battles, like the sons of the great warriors of old.</span>
</div>

<h2>Comparing Divine Powers</h2>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Rama had the *Gandiva* and broke Shiva's bow, but he did not use the *Sudarshana Chakra*. Krishna had the *Sudarshana Chakra*, but it was ordained that it would not work for unrighteousness. The *Kalki* avatar will have His sword and the collective power of the seven immortals.</span>
</div>

Just as Badrinath was for *Satya Yuga*, Rameshwaram for *Treta*, and Puri for *Dvapara*, the Ram Mandir in Ayodhya is destined to be the significant temple of *Kali Yuga*.
