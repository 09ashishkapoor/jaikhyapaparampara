<h2>A Poetic Tribute</h2>

Swami Jay Bijay Giri recited a moving tribute he composed for Gurudev:

*"The son of the Mother, in the Mother’s lap, sits alone in the bower of peace... 
You are the king of pilgrimages—Kashi, Vrindavan, and the Kaaba. 
A hidden practitioner, remaining secret while laughing and playing in the world. 
One without a second, O Bhairava Shama Khapa."*

He also shared a prayer for forgiveness and guidance, emphasizing Gurudev's role as a "divine tree of desire" (*Kalpataru*) for his disciples.

<h2>The Cycle of Yugas Within the Mind</h2>

In a fascinating insight, Maharaj explained how the four Yugas manifest daily within a person's consciousness:

*   **Satya Yuga (Midnight to Sunrise):** The time for peaceful meditation and pure thoughts.
*   **Treta Yuga (Sunrise to Noon):** The start of active duties and sacrifices for society.
*   **Dwapara Yuga (Noon to Sunset):** The time of intense work, business, and worldly struggle.
*   **Kali Yuga (Sunset to Midnight):** When negative thoughts, temptations, and distractions enter the mind.

To navigate the "Kali Yuga" of the mind, Maharaj emphasized the importance of *Naam Kirtan*—loudly chanting the name of God to drive away dark thoughts.
