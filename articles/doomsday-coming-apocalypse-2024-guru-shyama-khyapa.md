<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>Guru Shyama Khyapa's words on the eve of Guru Purnima 2024 carry the weight of prophecy spoken through a heart heavy with foreknowledge. His identification of 2024 as a "year of death"—where out of 8 billion people only 650 million might remain—echoes the ancient puranic calculations of pralaya (cosmic dissolution) that occur at the end of each cycle. The Guru's revelation that the fall of Lord Balabhadra at Jagannath Dham signals inevitable famine demonstrates how spiritual masters read cosmic signs invisible to ordinary perception. His reference to the "Maran Jajna" (Great Sacrifice) having begun reveals a tantric understanding: the gods themselves have initiated the cleansing, and even a realized master cannot intervene in the Divine Plan once it has been set in motion. Yet within these apocalyptic warnings lies a deeper truth—Krishna's promise to appear age after age to protect the righteous and destroy the wicked. The coming cataclysms are not punishments but the necessary surgery performed by the Cosmic Physician to remove the cancer of adharma from the body of the world.</p>
