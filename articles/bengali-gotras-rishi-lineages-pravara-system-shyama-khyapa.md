If I give Diksha to someone and they become very famous, they become a "Pravara" associated with me.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">The Pravara-mala consists of the names of those who achieved great fame, whether they were disciples or biological descendants. Many modern priests might just recite the names without knowing who these Rishis actually were. That is why I explained the names of the Rishis alongside the Pravara-mala.</span>

Only Rishis live within a family and produce descendants while remaining great spiritual pillars. That is how the Pravara-mala is formed—through their associates, brothers, or sons who also achieved greatness.</span>
