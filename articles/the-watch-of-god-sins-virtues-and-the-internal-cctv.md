Disciple: Greetings, Gurudev. Someone must be watching us and judging our sins and virtues. What do you have to say about this?

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-left:5%;margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Look, you do not have the power to judge sins and virtues. Report your actions to God at night. Whether it is sin or virtue, let Him judge it. We often don't even know which of our actions are truly sins. If a mother steals bread to feed her starving child, is that a sin? That judgment belongs to the Creator.</span>
</div>

No one is born a sinner. But due to circumstances, people might do wrong things. God means forgiveness. As Krishna told Shishupala, He forgives up to a limit. But remember, everything is being recorded. There is a "CCTV camera" inside your soul—the Paramatma—observing everything. You cannot escape it.

If someone commits sins in a previous life, it will be reflected in this life. Consider the story of the two boys and the pigeon. One killed the pigeon out of sight of people, but the other couldn't do it because he felt the pigeon's eye watching him. The second boy realized that the Supreme Soul is always watching.

Disciple: Gurudev, when people say Yamaraj will punish you after death, what does it mean?

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-left:5%;margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Gurudev:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">In our society, when we don't get justice, we say these things. Courts can be corrupted, but God's judgment is final. The "cup of sin" eventually fills up. A person might die suddenly before facing earthly justice, but the sin is carried over. The next life will be terrifying.</span>
</div>

Surrender yourself to Him. Do your work, but do not obsess over results. Surrender your actions to the Divine. There is no other way.
