<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>This conversation, recorded during the <strong>Omicron wave</strong> of early 2022, captures Gurudev's deeply troubling observations about the state of the world. He sees the pandemic not as an isolated medical crisis but as part of a larger <strong>planetary reckoning</strong>—what he calls "nature's revenge" for humanity's mistreatment of the earth. His most haunting statement—<em>"It's as if the Goddess doesn't want to see human faces anymore. God and nature have turned against humanity"</em>—echoes ancient prophecies about the darkening of divine grace in Kali Yuga. The closing of major temples like <strong>Kalighat, Tarapith, and Dakshineswar</strong> represents more than pandemic precautions to Gurudev; it symbolizes a spiritual withdrawal. His prediction that this period could extend until <strong>2030</strong>, potentially leaving "half the world empty," is one of his most dire warnings about the coming cataclysm.</p>
