<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Scholar:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">To understand "Akal Bodhon," we have to look at geography and the solar cycles: Dakshinayan and Uttarayon. When the sun leans toward the south, it is Dakshinayan. When it leans toward the north, it is Uttarayon. Each lasts six months.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Interviewer:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Which months are these?</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Scholar:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Uttarayon begins the day after Pous Sankranti (mid-January), during the Ganga Sagar Mela. It continues until the full moon of the month of Ashar (mid-July). These are the six months of Uttarayon. Then, from the day after the Ashar full moon, Dakshinayan begins.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Scholar:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">In Dakshinayan, the gods sleep while humans are awake. In Uttarayon, the gods are awake while humans sleep. This is why saints and sages choose to pass away during Uttarayon. You see this in the Mahabharata; when Bhishma was on the bed of arrows, it was Dakshinayan. He waited until Uttarayon began before he finally left his body.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Scholar:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Because Ram worshipped the Goddess during Dakshinayan (autumn) to defeat Ravana, he had to wake her up "untimely." That is why it is called "Akal Bodhon" (Untimely Awakening).</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">According to the Puranas, the original Durga Puja is celebrated in the spring. We have chosen the autumn celebration because the weather is pleasant and food is abundant. However, the "original" worship of the Goddess in her form as Annapurna happens during Uttarayon.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Scholar:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Yes, Basanti Puja. It was first performed by King Surath for the sake of increasing his wealth and prosperity. That is the original Durga Puja. The autumn one is the "untimely" one. While it is popular with the public, for spiritual practitioners, the autumn puja doesn't hold the same theological significance because it occurs while the gods are technically in their period of rest.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

It was only with the trident that she could finally defeat him.</span>

There are also the 64 Yoginis who accompanied her on the battlefield.</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

She transformed herself into the fierce, naked form of Kaali to show her power.</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
