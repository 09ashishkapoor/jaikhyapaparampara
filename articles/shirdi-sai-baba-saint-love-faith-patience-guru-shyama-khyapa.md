<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">I stayed with a man named Kalyan Rai. He asked me, "Father, will you come to Shirdi Sai with us?" I said, "Yes, why not? Let's go." We went there by car, which took quite some time. We had his darshan (sacred viewing).</span>

I was surprised and asked, "I have money and belongings with me; what if they are stolen?" They told me, "No one will take anything. Even if you leave millions of rupees here, you will find them exactly where you left them in the morning."</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">So, I followed their word. I left my cameras and other equipment; nothing was lost or disturbed. <span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Once, Ramakrishna became very ill. At that time, Sai Baba left his body for three days. But a strange thing happened at the exact same time—I can't explain why, but in 1886, Sri Ramakrishna also prayed to God to take his life.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">In the meantime, Sai Baba returned from his three-day samadhi. We cannot call them liars; they are world teachers. So, the story is likely true.</span>
