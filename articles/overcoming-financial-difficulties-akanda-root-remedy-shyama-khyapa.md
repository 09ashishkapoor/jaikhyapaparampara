If someone follows it, they might see money coming their way.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">It is nothing else but this: On a Purnima (full moon) day, one must find a White Akanda (Crown Flower/Swallow-wort) tree. </div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Once you have the root, prepare a mixture of cow urine, cow dung, milk, ghee, yogurt, and Kush water (water infused with kusha grass). Submerge the root in this mixture and leave it overnight.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru Shyama Khyapa:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">The next morning, take the root and place it on top of a Shiva Linga (Lord Shiva's head). Men should wear it on their right hand, and women on their left hand. I should mention that it often proves more effective for men than for women.</span>
</div>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">This is a significant remedy that I have shared with many, and many people practice it. </div>

</div>
