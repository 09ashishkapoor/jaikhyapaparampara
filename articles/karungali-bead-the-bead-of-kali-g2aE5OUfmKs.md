The **Karungali Mala** is one of the most powerful and least understood sacred materials in Kali Sadhana — equal in significance to the Rudraksha but far more dangerous when misused. Shri Praveen Radhakrishna opens this teaching with a direct warning: *do not simply buy and wear it.* What follows is a precise transmission of what Karungali is, why it demands respect, and the exact method by which it must be used to become a genuine shield rather than a source of harm.

### What Is Karungali? The Black Kali Wood

The name itself carries the teaching. *Karungali* breaks down directly into its meaning: **Dark Kali** or **Black Kali**. This is not merely a label — it describes the wood's fundamental energetic property. Karungali is an absolute absorber of energy. It does not discriminate. It draws in positive energy and negative energy with equal force and holds both. This is its most important and most dangerous characteristic.

To clarify the danger: giving a gun to a baby does not make the gun evil, but the result is destructive. Karungali placed in the hands of someone without proper initiation and consecration is precisely this scenario. The wood absorbs whatever energy is present — and if there is no consecrated intent directing what it does with that absorbed energy, the results are unpredictable and frequently harmful.

### The Historical Context: Karungali Cots

In earlier eras, *Zamindars* (landlords), businessmen, and wealthy householders would commission entire sleeping cots built from Karungali wood. The purpose was Kavach — protection during the most vulnerable hours.

During sleep, the physical body is switched off. The *Sukshma Sharira* (subtle body) — the emotional and mental complex — is fully active and exposed. *Upasakas* in early stages of Sadhana are especially vulnerable at this time. Any *Prayoga* (intentional black-magic working) directed at them, any concentrated negative emotion from an enemy, any stray astral interference can infiltrate psyche and disrupt the bloodline, business, or intimate relationships. The Karungali cot would absorb all of this before it could reach the sleeping body. The wood was a literal energetic barrier.

### Karungali as a Pause Button

The clearest functional image Shri Praveen offers is that of **Mahadeva consuming cosmic poison and holding it in his throat** (the *Neelakantha* moment). Karungali functions in exactly the same way — it is a *pause button*. It absorbs negative impacts: the evil eye (*Buri Nazar*), targeted *Prayogas*, the compressed hatred of a colleague, the destructive wishes of family members. All of this is paused, held inside the Mala, before it can penetrate the Sadhaka's field.

But a pause button has a catch: **it pauses everything equally**. A Karungali Mala that is not properly consecrated and not in active relationship with Ma Kali will also begin to slow down the positive energy flowing to the wearer. The Mahalakshmi energy, the good fortune, the wealth Sankalpas, the auspicious periods — all of these get absorbed and held as well. This is why practitioners report going from good jobs to the street while still wearing Karungali: the Mala was absorbing indiscriminately.

The danger is not theoretical. It is observed.

### The Safe Method: On, Off, and Nightly Recharge

For the Sadhaka who is not yet fully immersed in Kali Sadhana but wishes to use Karungali for protection, Shri Praveen transmits the following precise technique:

**Nightly Routine (Every Night Without Exception)**
Place the Karungali Mala on the *Yantra* of **Ma Adya Maha Kali** before sleeping. The Yantra should be placed before a consecrated photograph of Ma Kali in the home. Every night, the Mala rests on Her Yantra — discharging all absorbed negative energy into Devi (who consumes it) and recharging with Her grace.

**The On/Off Cycle**
*   Begin wearing the Mala on *Amavasya* (New Moon day).
*   Wear it for **11 days** (or 22, 44 — multiples of 11).
*   After 11 days: remove it and leave it on the Yantra for an equal number of days (11 days off).
*   This prevents the Mala from reaching capacity — after 11 days of absorbing all energies around you, it needs time on the Yantra to discharge and refresh.

**Consecrating the Yantra**
The Yantra does not require elaborate ritual. Place it in front of Ma Kali's image. Offer *Naivedyam* (food), light *Dhoop* (incense), light a lamp, recite a simple *Stava* (hymn). This simple Bhakti is sufficient to consecrate the Yantra and make it a functioning energetic receiver.

### For the Advanced Sadhaka

If a practitioner has found Ma Kali's grace through consistent Sadhana and she has fully entered their life, the Mala may be worn for continuous periods — 365 days — without the on/off cycle. In this state, the consecrated Karungali operates as a living shield synchronized with all 1,008 names of Kali — including her Lakshmi and Mahalakshmi aspects. It becomes not merely a protector but an active generator of auspicious energy.

### The Cow Shelter and the Invisible Sanctuary

A brief story illustrates the broader principle. A bank branch in an ordinary part of a city was performing exceptionally well while similar branches struggled. An elderly woman client explained why. The land the bank was built on had once housed a *Gaushala* — a sanctuary of native cows kept by a devout *Devi Upasaka*, not for commercial milk production but purely as a spiritual offering.

The *Gau Mata* (Mother Cow) is protected by the entirety of the *Devaloka* (the realm of Gods). A family that lives alongside a native cow kept only for seva (service) will not even experience disturbing dreams. That Gaushala had imprinted the land for decades. The bank, built above that ground, was still receiving the residual grace of the cow's presence.

The principle: **every material in Prakriti — every animal, tree, leaf, crystal, and wood — holds a specific energetic property**. *Prakriti* is Devi's body. The Sadhaka who is deep in Kali Sadhana finds this revealing itself naturally. Karungali is one such revelation — a specific, potent property of a specific wood that has been identified, proven, and transmitted across the Khepa tradition.

### Conclusion

Karungali is not jewellery. It is not a trending spiritual accessory. It is a concentrated energetic absorber named after the Black Kali Herself. Used without consecration, it will pause your good fortune alongside your suffering. Used with the on/off method and nightly recharging on the Yantra, it becomes precisely what it is named for — a black hole that consumes all harm and returns you to Devi's care each morning. Approach it as such, and it will serve you well.
