The Divine Mother resides within the Guru, and when the Guru appears, the Goddess enters our lives 100%.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">So, this is my first lesson: never chase after a Guru. When the time comes, he steps out of hiding and reveals himself to you. The day the Guru fully initiates you, the entire Gurumandala (lineage of Gurus) opens up for you, and all the Gurus bless you.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">The Mission of 10,000 Temples: I have experienced this personally—every door simply opens for us. There is no need to worry about anything—where the money will come from, how the transport will be arranged, where the idols will come from, or whom to invite. You just think about it, and it gets done. Whoever tries to stop us fails; nothing works against us, and we just keep moving forward.</span>

We don't even need to ask the Mother for anything; we just sit, and every door opens for us.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">The Hidden Siddha of West Bengal: Speaking of Gurudev's power, I would say it is time for the whole of India to know that there is a great Siddha (perfected master) in West Bengal. He is a great Siddha who never expected a stage or platform for himself, nor did he want anyone to talk about him. He never expected fame because he sacrificed everything, took on the karma of his disciples, and gave them his all. To this day, he has never asked me for a single thing. I think he is even hesitant to ask his disciples, 'Do you need anything?' He only gives. Finding a Guru like this today is rare. So again, I say, do not bother them by chasing them. Practice meditation at home. When your time comes, the Guru will appear.</span>
</div>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">When the Prarabdha Karma (destined karma) for which we were born comes to an end, only then can we fully benefit from the grace of the Goddess and the Guru. Stand up, fight, go out into the world, and do your work flawlessly. When you do this, the Goddess blesses you, and the Guru comes to you as a manifestation of Her grace. Once your karma's cycle is complete and the Guru sets his eyes on you, the entire Gurumandala resides within you in a single day.</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

I thought, since he was sent by Kedarnath Ji, I must show him. He wouldn't believe it initially. So, what did I show him? I grabbed the Guru's feet. By holding his feet, I showed him the vision. I showed him the feet of Maa Kali, the feet of Maa Durga. Yes, I was very happy.</span>
</div>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Concluding Thoughts: So, it is true: when the time comes and your karma is complete, the Guru will come knocking at your door, telling you it is time to start your Sadhana (spiritual practice). I have been doing sadhana for 13 years, met many Gurus, and seen many seekers in our temple, but I never begged for initiation. Do your work. The divine fragment of the Goddess resides within the true Guru—this isn't the case for every Guru, as it takes lifetimes of sadhana.</span>
