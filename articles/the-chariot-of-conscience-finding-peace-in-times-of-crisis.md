People don't watch the full message; they lack patience. The conclusion is always at the end. Regarding those suffering, I am constantly praying. This is a period of *Pralay* (cataclysm), and the Mother doesn't always listen easily, but we must keep asking.

I once heard a politician say everyone should buy a mirror and look at themselves. I realized this is a deep philosophy. The mirror is your **conscience** (*Bibek*). Look in it and ask if you are a sinner or a virtuous soul. Everyone commits sins, and we often pay for them across births.

Buddha’s final words were: "Awaken your conscience, and it will lead you." If people questioned their own conscience, the world wouldn't be in this state. The "chariot of conscience" will lead you through the darkest times. God exists, and everything happens at His signal. If we can satisfy Him through love, this danger will subside. It is temporary; the hands of the clock keep moving.

**Assistant:** Gurudev, it is unfortunate that while we produce vaccines, our people struggle to get them. Politicians seem to have no conscience.
