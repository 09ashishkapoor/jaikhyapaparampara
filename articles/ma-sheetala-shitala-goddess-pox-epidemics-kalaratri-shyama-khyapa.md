<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Ma Shitala resided in the ashram of Sage Katyayana. <span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">We generally know her as Kalaratri, a very fierce form. She is the deity associated with pox, measles, cholera, and smallpox—all the terrible epidemics of the past.</span>

This was the ritual. I've seen this for a long time. In Arambagh, Hooghly, there is a temple dedicated to her. During the month of Phalguna, around the time of the Dol festival, there is a massive crowd. You can't even find a place to stand.</span>
