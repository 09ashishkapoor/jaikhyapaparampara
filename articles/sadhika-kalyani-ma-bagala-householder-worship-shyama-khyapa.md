I felt so small, like an ignorant fool. I don't know anything. By the grace of Ma and Guru, I found Ma Bagala. I see her just like my own little child. She is so sweet and always whispers in my ear.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">When I find time, even while in the bathroom or anywhere, I chant. I have no other time. I guide the people who come to me in that way. We don't know much about elaborate rituals. Whatever Ma teaches me, I do that. Then I see that I am doing the right thing.</span>

</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">We say: "Even if the Guru goes to a brothel, he remains the purifier of the fallen." This is what is said about the Guru.</span>

I start by purifying the seat, remembering the Guru, and worshiping the five deities. That's all I know. Then I leave everything in Her hands.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">I sit down, and my Guru told me to perform Homa (fire ritual). I asked, "How do I do it, Gurudev?" The wood doesn't matter; the fire does.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">I am not bound by many rules. I always tell people, don't worry too much about rituals. A child can do anything in the mother's lap. <div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

I never wanted to show off as a guru. Now, if the Mother wants me to be one, what can I do?</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">I say, if someone benefits from the Mother's mantra, if I give the mantra, I want nothing in return. May they be well and happy. That's all I want. I don't sit around dressed up like a guru. I am just like Shyama Khepa Baba. This is what I love. I felt that God is not far; He is very close. Love Him and cry for Him. No one cries for Him. Everyone cries for "my son," "my daughter." But when did you become His? You didn't bring anything with you, and you won't take anything with you—not even the clothes on your back.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">My karma is everything. Based on my karma, I will be reborn or not. This is what I tell everyone. I don't act like a guru. I know nothing. I am an ignorant fool. But I have suffered a lot because of property disputes. I was devastated. Ma Bagala—Ma Sarada herself—came in my dream and said, "Look, your Guru will come after three months." That's how Ma Bagala gave me Gurudev. Ma is there. I told her I don't know many rules. "You stay like my daughter. I'll scold you, I'll love you, I'll dress you up." That's all.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">What can we householders do? You (God) are eating from my hand. Whatever money my husband gives me, I manage with that. I don't count the money. I wondered if I could manage this month. But Ma feeds me. Ma loves fish and rice; she wants it every day. Sometimes I say, "Gurudev, don't eat now." I tell Ma, "There is no fish today, eat eggs." Then, while I'm serving the food, I see someone has brought a large fish for Ma. Then I realize Ma herself brought it.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">I don't care about caste. Sometimes someone brings food from a hotel. I tell Ma to eat it, and then I eat it. I am a complete householder. This is what I tell everyone. Don't cry and complain. Tell Ma; she can do everything. Your bad times might just pass with a small scratch. You will survive.</span>
