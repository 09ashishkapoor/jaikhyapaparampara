<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">For instance, in the Treta Yuga, there was the Surya Kavach (Sun Talisman). So, talismans have held a great influence since ancient times, and that influence continues today.</span>

Their prices vary as well; for example, a Bagalamukhi Kavach can cost around 50,000 rupees, while a Kali Kavach might be 30,000, and a Tara Kavach 25,000.</span>

<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>This discourse reveals that authentic <strong>Tabeez and Kavach</strong> are not commercial products but sacred spiritual tools requiring years of dedicated practice (12-15 years of Japa, Tapa, and Yajna) to create effectively. The power of an amulet lies not in its material cost but in the <strong>Siddhi</strong> (spiritual attainment) of its maker. The specific requirements—creation in cremation grounds after midnight, proper astrological alignments, and mastery of the <strong>Dasha Mahavidya</strong>—demonstrate that spiritual protection is a serious science, not a quick fix. Gurudev's warning about planetary burdens transferring to unqualified makers reminds us that authentic Tantra requires both knowledge and the spiritual capacity to handle its forces.</p>
