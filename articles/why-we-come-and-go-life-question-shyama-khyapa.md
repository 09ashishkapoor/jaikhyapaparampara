<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Oh, Rahul, my sincere blessings to you. This short poem of yours has deeply moved me. </div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">This coming and going is a continuous play. There is no end to it, and no ledger to balance it. When I try to settle the accounts, I see that everything is an error—yet, we still feel there is nothing. Truly, there is nothing. There is no one to answer to, no one to tell. My words, my behavior, my gestures—why should I give an account to anyone?</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">But in the next moment, I feel that there is something. Someone is watching me. I have to answer to someone. This "being" and "not being" is what keeps us thinking. It is a very high level of thought.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Rahul, I thank you immensely because we are all witnessing and feeling this "play," but we often cannot express it. We keep it buried within our hearts. To whom shall we say it? Mother Earth doesn't speak, doesn't eat, doesn't sleep. Yet, I feel someone is behind me, constantly poking me, asking, "What are you doing? Why are you doing it?" That "someone" keeps track of me every moment. I don't know who it is yet; I haven't fully realized it.</span>

We are just dying inside, wondering why we came and where we are going.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">We come into this world naked, and we leave naked. In between, there is a drama, a "Jatra Pala" (folk theater).
