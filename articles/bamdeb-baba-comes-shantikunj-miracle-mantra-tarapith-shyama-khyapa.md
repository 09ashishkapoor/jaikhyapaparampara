Since I couldn't go to him, it is a miracle that Baba himself has come to me. This is truly amazing—that Bamdeb Baba came on his own. I have requested him many times before, but this time he understood and came himself.</span>

Would you say this is truly Bamdeb Baba's wish?</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Absolutely. Nothing happens outside of his wish. I tried many times, but it wouldn't work. </div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">What can I say? Baba's Leela is impossible to fully grasp. He has the power to turn the world upside down. We know his immense power. I have seen it in my own life—major tasks being completed, debts being cleared—everything.</span>
</div>

That is Baba's power.</span>

</div>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Those days are gone. I don't even feel like going there anymore. It has become full of hypocrisy and cheating.
