<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>This profound discourse on the sacred tradition of Deeksha (initiation) reveals the complex, ancient relationship between Guru and disciple that forms the pillar of Sanatan Dharma. Gurudev explains that surges of initiation seekers come in waves—just as they did during the time of Ramnarayan Ram and Balak Brahmachari, who attracted 300,000 disciples. The connection is often rooted in Prarabdha karma—past life connections that bring devotees to their Guru at the appointed time, even at age 60 or 65. Dada Ghatak eloquently explains that the Guru is the pathfinder, compared in scriptures to Brahma, Vishnu, and Maheshwara combined. While Gurudev maintains strict boundaries to protect his inner peace—telling initiates not to return immediately—the Guru actually resides in the disciple's heart forever. The powerful saying "If Mahadev is angry, the Guru can save you; but if the Guru is angry, even Mahadev cannot" underscores the primacy of this relationship. The fact that youth from around the world (Canada, France, Germany) are seeking initiation through YouTube suggests the spiritual awakening Gurudev has predicted may indeed be beginning from Bengal.</p>
