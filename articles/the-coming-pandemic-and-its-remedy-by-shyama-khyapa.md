<p><em>(Background: Chanting of Sanskrit Mantras and religious hymns praising various forms of the Goddess and deities.)</em></p>

<p><strong>Interviewer:</strong> Friends, you can see... I am now going to Gurudev.</p>

<p><em>(Interviewer approaches Gurudev at the temple)</em></p>

<p><strong>Interviewer:</strong> Gurudev, my respects to you. You have come to the temple after a long time today, and a <em>Homa</em> (fire ritual) is also being performed for the Mother.</p>

<p><strong>Gurudev:</strong> Yes, the <em>Homa</em> is a regular occurrence. We have no shortage of rituals; we perform a <em>Homa</em> every week. And it is a grand <em>Homa</em>. We do this not for any other reason, but to keep the environment and the spiritual connection right. Looking at the current state of the world, I am worried. December hasn't even fully set in yet. If you look at what will happen from 2024 onwards... there will be a mass loss of life. A "cleansing" of humanity will begin in 2024. I am fearful of this, which is why I am trying to find some resolution through these rituals and sacrifices.</p>

<p><strong>Interviewer:</strong> But you are quite unwell yourself. You are supposed to be admitted to a nursing home tomorrow for surgery.</p>

<p><strong>Gurudev:</strong> Yes, I will be admitted tomorrow, and the operation is the day after. That is fine, but this work is more important. People are constantly calling and asking how I am and how the Mother is.</p>

<p><strong>Interviewer:</strong> That is why I showed the Goddess first.</p>

<p><strong>Gurudev:</strong> Yes, look at the fire now. There is no artificial light here; the Goddess is visible only by the light of the ritual fire.</p>

<p><strong>Interviewer:</strong> It looks beautiful.</p>

<p><strong>Gurudev:</strong> Yes, show the Mother clearly. That is all that is needed.</p>

<p><em>(Background: Continued chanting of Sanskrit Mantras dedicated to various planetary deities like Budha, Brihaspati, Rahu, and Ketu.)</em></p>

<p><strong>Interviewer:</strong> Gurudev, I have a question. Based on what we are seeing in the news and around us—is there some disaster or pandemic coming that is even more dangerous than Corona?</p>

<p><strong>Gurudev:</strong> Look, it has already arrived. It is a pneumonia-type illness. Many children are being affected by it. This contagion has spread from China. China will definitely face a pandemic, but other surrounding countries will also be affected. I declared long ago that these diseases were coming. It is a terrifying form of pneumonia that is very difficult to recover from. I don't know what the doctors will do, but it has everyone worried.</p>

<p>Furthermore, 2024 will be a terrifying year. I call it the "Year of Death." People will die in large numbers. I don't know exactly how it will happen, but 2024 is the year of mortality. Everyone must remain cautious; being whimsical or careless won't work. If we don't stay alert, I fear the world as we know it might face an existential crisis.</p>

<p>I am receiving reports from places like Canning and other areas that many young people are dying suddenly. We are performing these rituals to see if we can recover from this. I am praying to the Fire and the Mother so that the severity is lessened.</p>

<p><strong>Interviewer:</strong> What about 2025?</p>

<p><strong>Gurudev:</strong> 2025 will also be difficult. There will be solar effects—solar flares or related phenomena. It will be very hard for people to get through 2024 and 2025.</p>

<p><strong>Interviewer:</strong> Will we see a return to lockdowns?</p>

<p><strong>Gurudev:</strong> It is heading in that direction. Very soon, it might move toward lockdowns again.</p>

<p><strong>Interviewer:</strong> Is there any way to save ourselves?</p>

<p><strong>Gurudev:</strong> We are trying through these rituals. For the common public, there is no other way but the <em>Maha Mrityunjaya Yagna</em> (a ritual dedicated to conquering death). This is the only thing that can save us from this pandemic. I urge everyone to call competent priests and perform the <em>Maha Mrityunjaya Yagna</em> in various places. Use pure ghee and <em>Bel</em> wood for the ritual. I believe this can provide some relief.</p>

<p><strong>Interviewer:</strong> Friends, you heard Gurudev's message. Please spread this message so that people can perform these rituals at home.</p>

<p><strong>Gurudev:</strong> Yes, the <em>Maha Mrityunjaya Yagna</em> and <em>Harinam Sankirtan</em> (chanting the names of God). These two things can give the world some respite. 2024 and 2025 are very dangerous. I am predicting this because I see no other way unless God saves us. As long as I am alive, I will continue these prayers.</p>

<p><strong>Interviewer:</strong> Thank you, Gurudev. We hope everyone stays healthy and safe. Respects to you.</p>

<p><strong>Gurudev:</strong> Blessings to all.</p>
