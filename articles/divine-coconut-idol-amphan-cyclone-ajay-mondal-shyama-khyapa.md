<p>This miraculous story demonstrates how the Divine chooses Her instruments according to Her own will, not human qualifications. Ajay Kumar Mondal, with no background in art or carpentry, was guided by the Mother to create a living idol from a hollow coconut and Neem wood. The hollow coconut—empty of flesh and water—became the perfect vessel for divine manifestation. Gurudev's instruction to worship with simple love rather than complex rituals reminds us that the heart's devotion matters more than external ceremonies. When the Mother calls, She provides all that is needed to fulfill Her purpose.</p>
