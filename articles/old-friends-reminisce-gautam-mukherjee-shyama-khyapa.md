Please.</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GAUTAM MUKHERJEE:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Yes, certainly. About 40 years ago, when Gurudev, Gopal Da, and I used to sit at the Keoratala Cremation Ground, I started forming various ideas about Gurudev. But one thing I witnessed personally in my own life: at one point, due to a financial crisis, I couldn't enroll my daughter in school. I brought my daughter to Gurudev at the temple. Gurudev asked her, "Ma, why have you come?" My daughter told him, "Dadu (Grandfather), I couldn't get into school because we don't have the money." Within two or three minutes—I don’t know exactly what Gurudev did—he told her, "Go, you will be enrolled in school." And it was a miracle to me. The very next day, she was called by the school she wanted to attend, and she started her studies there without us having to pay any money. Today, that girl has passed her Higher Secondary exams and is pursuing her career. This was a huge miracle for me. I later asked Gurudev out of curiosity, "Gurudev, how did this happen? You said it, and the next day the school called her for free enrollment."</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Gurudev told me one thing: "After hearing your daughter’s words, I felt something in my heart. I just told Ma (the Goddess). That’s all I did in that short time. I was confident your daughter would be enrolled because Ma gave me that assurance." This was a major experience for me regarding Gurudev.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">HOST:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">And you mentioned that you, Gopal Da, and Gurudev used to sit together, drink tea, and chat. Could you share some of those experiences?</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GAUTAM MUKHERJEE:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Well, during that period, we would sit at the cremation ground and talk about various things. Many dead bodies would be brought in. We saw some truly strange things with our own eyes. Gurudev saw them too. For instance, I remember seeing an elderly woman crying inconsolably because her grandson had died. She was sitting inside the cremation ground, wailing. Then, as is the custom, "Khoi-Murki" (puffed rice and sweets) was brought for those attending the funeral. As soon as the snacks were being distributed, the woman stopped crying immediately. she spread out the end of her sari to collect as much as possible and started eating. We laughed so much seeing that—just moments ago she was wailing, and now she's happily eating. There are so many experiences like that; if I start telling them, they'll never end. As for Gurudev’s spiritual practices (Sadhana), I won't say much. I know many things, but those are esoteric.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">[Laughing] Let me tell you a funny story. I used to sleep with a "Bed" (a ritual stick or possibly referring to a Veda) under my head at the cremation ground. He came to me and said, "Gurudev, please give me that pillow." I said, "This isn't a pillow; I'm sleeping on this Bed." He said, "That’s exactly what I want. If I sleep on it, all the knowledge of the Bed will enter my head and my stomach!" I said, "Fine, then sleep on it, what’s the problem?" Those were the days... it’s hard to describe them in words. We were very happy then, like friends. Gurudev, Gopal Da, and I were very close for a long time. Gopal Da is no longer in this world; I just heard that from Gurudev. We spent such good times together, and today we are sitting here reminiscing. It’s been a long time since I visited Gurudev. My heart was heavy, wondering how he was. So I came today.</span>
</div>
