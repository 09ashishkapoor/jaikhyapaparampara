<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>This article documents the extraordinary 1400km walking pilgrimage (Padayatra) undertaken by Pagla Baba from Ranaghat to the sacred Kamakhya temple in Assam. The journey, accomplished with remarkable speed (150km in just three days at one point), was supported by numerous organizations including the RSS, Vishva Hindu Parishad, Bajrang Dal, and Bharat Sevashram Sangha. The account reveals both the profound devotion of the pilgrims and the practical challenges they faced, including Gurudev's strict instructions about photography and the need to maintain sanctity during such a public spiritual journey. The pilgrimage exemplifies the living tradition of walking to sacred sites that remains vibrant in the Khyapa Parampara.</p>
