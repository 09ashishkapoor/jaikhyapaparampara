Underground movements grow. People find ways to resist. And most importantly, the universe has a natural law of balance. When too much oppression accumulates in one place, a counterforce emerges. Nature itself seeks equilibrium. The Taliban are small fry in the grand scheme of things. They are insignificant. There are greater forces at play in the world.</span>

<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>Power built on fear and oppression is unsustainable—like ants that grow temporary wings only to fall. The Taliban's persecution of minorities and women represents a regime founded on insecurity, not strength. Though minorities suffer now, the universe operates on spiritual law: dharma prevails, and tyranny eventually crumbles. For those facing persecution, the path is humanitarian refuge and inner spiritual freedom. The perpetrators will face the inevitable consequence of their actions. Neither military intervention nor hatred serves dharma; compassionate wisdom and firm moral stands do.</p>
