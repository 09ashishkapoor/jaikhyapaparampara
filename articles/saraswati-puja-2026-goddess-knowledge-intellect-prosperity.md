A common tradition is to place our books at the feet of the Mother. There is a deep-rooted belief that by doing so, the Goddess blesses the books, leading to better academic performance.</span>

While these traditions have shifted slightly over time, the essence of worshipping the Mother remains.</span>
</div>

It was Maha-Saraswati who defeated powerful demons like Shumbha.</span>
