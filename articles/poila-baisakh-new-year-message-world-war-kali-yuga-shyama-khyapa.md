He reads the Bhabishya Malika on the drying Ganges, a coming variant disease, and the end of Kali Yuga, then says Nature rather than God is what kills, makes people forget, and gives birth again.</p>

<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>Gurudev's New Year message moves between three registers in a single sitting: the <a href="/articles/topics/prophecy-world-events/">prophetic</a> reading of 2026 against the Bengali year 1433, a practical wish that charity be turned into a cow and fodder for every household, and a closing argument that Nature - which he names as Maya, Mahamaya - is what kills, makes people forget, and gives birth again. He is careful to separate himself from market prophecy: he reads the <em>Bhabishya Malika</em> but says plainly, "we don't make prophecies," and dismisses zodiac forecasts as a way to make money. Taken with his warning that Kali Yuga and Satya Yuga now overlap, the talk asks a devotee for two things only, honest livelihood and the divine name, while the age turns. Read it as devotional teaching on cycles of time, not as prediction or preparedness advice. For the wider prophecy series see <a href="/articles/2023-predictions-calamities-earthquakes-bhabishya-malika-prophecies/">the Bhabishya Malika predictions</a>, and for his account of Nature taking revenge see <a href="/articles/mutilation-of-nature-spiritual-warning/">the mutilation of Nature</a>.</p>
