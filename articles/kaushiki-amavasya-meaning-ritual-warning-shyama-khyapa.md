<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>Guru Shyama Khyapa reveals the formidable power of Kaushiki Amavasya, when Mother Durga transformed her Kosh (form) into Kali to satisfy Mahadev's desire while preserving the dignity of universal motherhood. This New Moon commemorates the day Durga became Kaushiki—one of the sixty-four Yoginis. The Guru delivers a stern warning: the Brahma Shila at Tara Peeth, brought by Vashishta and later protected by Bama Dev (the reincarnation of Vashishta), possesses such ferocious energy that touching it can cause madness or ruin. Householders have no place at Tara Peeth on this night, where the Mother's head alone rests upon the stone, and where the union of greed and desire has replaced true spiritual practice.</p>
