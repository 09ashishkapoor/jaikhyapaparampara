<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">I am Sushona Banerjee from Behala. My home has a temple dedicated to the Mother [Goddess]. I also had a question for Gurudev. I’ve heard that at the temple of Ma Moulakhi, which is very powerful, no one can stay after 7:00 PM; people have even died there. But Ma Moulakhi told me, "They don't worship me or feed me properly." I asked, "Mother, what should I do?" She said, "They will leave when they see you." After they left, I bathed the Mother in the evening. When I was applying vermilion (sindoor) to her, she told me to apply it to the big toe of my right foot as well. The devotees and the priest there asked, "What are you doing?" I said, "Whatever happens to me will happen, just let me be." I didn’t even know what was happening to me at that moment. I applied the vermilion to the Mother's feet and my own.</span>
