<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

Just as he performed rituals for world peace, I am undertaking this walking pilgrimage for global peace and for the world to be free from disease.</span>
</div>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Currently, everyone is terrified and distressed by the COVID situation; it has become a nightmare for everyone. I pray to the Mother to cure these diseases and bring the world back to a state of peace. This is my prayer to Ma Kamakhya. As I travel, I am visiting every temple of the Mother along the way, offering the same prayer. I feel deeply fulfilled to dedicate myself to the well-being of the world. Thank you.
