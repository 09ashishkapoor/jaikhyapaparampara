<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>In this solemn moment, Guru Shyama Khyapa provides clear guidance to disciples on their duties following Guruma's passing to Amrit Lok (the eternal realm). The <strong>three-day ashoucha</strong> (mourning period) reflects the traditional respect accorded to a Guru or Guruma, who is considered as one's own parent. The rituals of nail trimming on the third day and the <strong>bhoji</strong> (food offering) on the fourth day help disciples maintain spiritual discipline while honoring the one who nurtured their spiritual journey. This moment reminds us of the impermanent nature of physical existence and the eternal bond between Guru, Guruma, and their disciples.</p>
