Even though we are Tantrics and perform Tantra Sadhana, we haven't set the Gita aside. Sujit Haldar reads it, and we listen to its profound glory. I tell everyone: hold onto the Gita.</span>

Gita, Ganga, Gayatri, and Govinda—these four 'G's, if held in one's heart, ensure there is no rebirth. One who reads the Gita with devotion understands the essence of all Vedas, Puranas, and other scriptures.</span>

Listen with rapt attention.</span>

He who knows in truth this glory and My yogic power becomes established in unwavering devotion; of this, there is no doubt.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">This is Vibhuti Yoga. Vibhuti is nothing other than His actions and expressions for the sake of the world. Everything you see in this world is Him. He works through you. This is what Vibhuti means; He shows you glimpses of His work.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Among horses know Me to be Ucchaishravas; among lordly elephants I am Airavata; and among men, know Me to be the king. Of weapons I am the thunderbolt; among cows I am Kamadhuk (the cow of plenty).</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

For the sake of the gods, he sacrificed his body so that his bones could be used to create the weapon that killed Vritrasura.</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Now, the Gita Mahatmya (Glory of the Gita). Dharadevi said: O Lord Vishnu, O Supreme Master, how can a person who is bound by his past karma develop unwavering devotion?</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">One who is bound by karma but is always engaged in reading the Gita becomes liberated and happy in this world. He is never tainted by his actions.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">He is not bound by karma. Even if he performs actions, they are not with desire. Reading the Gita helps bring the soul under control.</span>
