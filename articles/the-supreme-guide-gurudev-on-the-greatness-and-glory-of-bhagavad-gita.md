<h2>The Guide from Birth to Death</h2>

The Gita is not just a book; it is a guide for the soul from birth to death. Even if we are Tantrics practicing intense *sadhana*, we never set the Gita aside. It is the essence of all Vedas and Puranas. If you hold the 'four G's'—Gita, Ganga, Gayatri, and Govinda—in your heart, there is no rebirth.

<h2>Understanding Vibhuti Yoga (Chapter 10)</h2>

In the tenth chapter, Lord Krishna reveals His **Vibhuti**—His divine opulences. He explains that He is the source of all gods, sages, and the fourteen Manus. He is the Self seated in the hearts of all living entities.

"Vibhuti" refers to His actions and expressions for the sake of the world. Whatever kindness you show or charity you give, He is the ultimate doer. He mentions being the **Sama Veda** among the Vedas, **Shiva** among the Rudras, and the **Himalayas** among immovable things.

<h2>The Original Kapila and the Thunderbolt</h2>

Krishna mentions, "Among perfected beings, I am the sage Kapila." He refers to the original Kapila Muni, who meditated at Ganga Sagar and worshipped Goddess Kali. He also identifies with the thunderbolt weapon, which was made from the bones of Sage Dadhichi to defeat the demon Vritrasura—a symbol of ultimate sacrifice for the greater good.

<h2>The Prophet of Dissolution</h2>

Among the luminaries, Krishna is the radiant Sun. I have mentioned before that at the time of dissolution, twelve suns will rise. This was also spoken of by Shri Ramakrishna. The earth will become uninhabitable as the cycle concludes.

<h2>The Glory of the Gita (Gita Mahatmya)</h2>

The *Gita Mahatmya* states that one who reads the Gita is never tainted by their actions. Just as water does not wet a lotus leaf, sins do not touch a devotee of the Gita. 

- Reading the whole Gita leads to the supreme goal.
- Reading even one chapter daily grants a long human birth or the realm of Shiva.
- Even pronouncing the word "Gita" at the time of death brings a good destination.
