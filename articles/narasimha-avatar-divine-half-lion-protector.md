<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">But Prahlada did not listen. He worshipped and chanted Lord Narayana's name every day. Hiranyakashipu subjected Prahlada to every possible torment—throwing him from high places, tossing him into boiling water and hot oil—leaving nothing out.
