<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Now, King Bali was a fierce and powerful king. If anyone asked him for anything, he would grant it; that was his custom.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Once, King Bali initiated a massive Yajna (fire sacrifice). Narayana saw this as the perfect opportunity. <span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">King Bali laughed it off and said, "Alright, that's not a big deal. It shall be given."</span>
