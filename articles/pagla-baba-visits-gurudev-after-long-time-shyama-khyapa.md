<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">I don't have much to say. But let me share how I found Guru-dev. Initially, I was studying to be a priest. <span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Some people asked for silver sandals, some for clothes, some for a large amount of money, and others said they would perform the abhishek (ritual) for 20,000 or 30,000 rupees. I didn't find him through YouTube or Facebook; I found him through word of mouth. On the first day I came, there were many people around him, and he didn't have much time. He said he wasn't feeling well and asked me to come back later. I came back again, and after some consideration, he gave me initiation.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">That feeling—it cannot be described openly. It was a different kind of experience. I had many visions and feelings. The Guru is the ultimate guide who helps a person take the first step in their spiritual journey. He should never be forgotten. I believe every disciple should maintain contact with their Guru, without any selfish motives. Everyone has some level of self-interest, but at some point, one must remember the Guru selflessly. I am a small person; please forgive me if I've said anything wrong. He is a realized saint, a Mahatman. Words cannot do him justice. That's all I have to say.</span>
