<h2>The Foundation of Spiritual Practice</h2>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Devotee:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Greetings, Gurudev. We have seen that you have been worshiping the Mother for almost 30 years. My question to you is, what have you received from the Mother, or what has the Mother received from you? If you could shed some light on this, it would be wonderful.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Look, there is always a matter of wanting and receiving; it’s not that there isn't. Sadhus (sages) want the Mother’s lotus feet. That is why the saint Ram Prasad said, "Some, O Mother, you give the status of Brahma, while others you cast down." That means Ma gives someone the highest spiritual realization and casts another down according to their karma. This is based on their deeds. 

When sages first begin their spiritual practice, they need a foundation. Without the virtues of past lives, one cannot become a seeker of the Mother. A seeker will be a seeker in every birth. In some lives, they may have a family, and in others, they may not. This is the rule. Depending on their karma, Ma may give them a family in the next life or may not.</span>
</div>

<h2>Transitioning from Form to Essence</h2>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Sadhus start with a foundation when they begin their practice. They need something—usually a form or an image of a deity—to start with. Later, when they understand the Mother’s true essence or the essence of God, they no longer need the physical form. This is a profound realization. Initially, seekers play with God. Later, when the "game" ends, the seeker’s only wish is, "Now, take me into your lap." This is an inner desire of the seeker—to witness the Mother. There is no escape from this longing. 

That is why we say, while practicing, take someone as your foundation and move forward with them. When you truly know them, regardless of which deity they are, you will no longer have a worldly attachment to them. In the process, a person sometimes reaches a state of madness. As the inner spirit (Hansa) awakens, the seeker’s thoughts and outlook change completely, and they enter the world of action. Then, the specific form of the Mother is no longer required.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Devotee:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">So, you are saying that one phase ends and you enter a different phase?</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Exactly, you move into a different world. That is the rule; that is what happens. You enter another realm. Ma is always there, but it’s about how much the seeker trusts Ma and how much Ma trusts the seeker. They are complementary to each other. Mother keeps track of everything the seeker does. You’ve seen this in Ram Prasad’s life as well. 

Ram Prasad once said, "Does a child not survive if the mother dies? I call out to Mother, but she has her eyes and ears closed. Yet, Ma exists within this suffering child. Does a child not survive if the mother dies?" In another instance, when he was very sad, he told Mother, "This time, Kali, I will eat you! A child born in a great moment is one who eats his mother." He expressed this through his songs. Sages sometimes distance themselves from the Mother, and then reconnect again. We see this often in Ram Prasad’s life.</span>
</div>

<h2>The Mystery of Divine Action</h2>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">These are all the Mother’s workings. Ma works invisibly. A seeker knows but doesn't fully know; understands but doesn't fully understand. Even great sages, at some point, stop asking for the Mother. We saw this with Bama Khepa; he wouldn't go into the Mother's temple. He would sit in his own place. If anyone came to him, he would say, "Go, go offer worship to the Mother. Go see the Mother." 

In the end, that is what sages say. "I have nothing; whatever there is, it's with the Mother. Go there and tell her." Now, whether they say this out of frustration or from the depths of their soul, or with what perspective, is hard to tell. It’s a mystery. 

Even I still don't fully understand who is doing the work. But work is happening. People are coming with faith. Who is doing it? This is a gray area. I can’t explain it even today, but things are getting done. People tell me their problems, and two days later they say the work is done. Who did it? How? It’s hard to say. Someone is definitely doing it. I realized I was on the wrong path when I questioned it, and a great Sadhu corrected me. He said, "Why do you need to know who is doing it? Just listen and tell them to go to the Mother. Whatever happens, Ma will do it."</span>
</div>

<h2>Divine Messengers in Human Form</h2>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Guru:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Messengers of Mother do come. Even Totapuri came to Ramakrishna to teach him the Vedas and Vedanta. When we finished the Mahamrityunjaya Yajna, a very tall, beautiful man with long matted hair came and sat at my feet. He looked exactly like Lord Shiva. He blessed me and said, "Everything is within you. Whatever you do will happen." 

Gods appear in human form, but humans don't always realize it because they lack that consciousness or that "eye." We see the outer world with these two eyes, but with the Third Eye, we can see much further—into other worlds and even hear the conversations of Gods. Those who have opened their Third Eye can see and hear the divine. 

I have performed many rituals for 30 or 32 years. But one thing I know—all these people coming, Ma is doing their work. For those who come with devotion and love, their work is done in a day. For those with a clouded mind or duplicity, it takes longer. But those who trust Mother get results immediately.</span>
</div>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Speaking of realizations, a viewer asked: you gave him initiation (Diksha) in a dream. Is that also a realization?</span>

When someone calls out from the heart, Ma doesn't go herself; she sends someone to do the work. I get calls from all over India saying, "You came to my house." It’s through Kriya Yoga that such things happen. Most of these visits happen at night. 

Whether it is a vision of the Mother or a visitation from a Guru, there is no boundary. Just this morning, a Muslim man from Bangladesh called. He had a vision of Mother Kali in his dream. I told him there is no problem—his Allah and my God are one and the same. Devotion knows no religion.</span>
