This proves that good people still exist in this world—people who help others because humanity is for everyone. I am forever grateful to them for helping my disciple.</span>

This hospitality reminds me of the ancient tradition of Atithi Devo Bhava (the guest is God). Also, the temple itself felt very spiritually charged and miraculous.</span>
