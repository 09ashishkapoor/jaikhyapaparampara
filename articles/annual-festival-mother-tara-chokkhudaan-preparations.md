<h3 style="color:var(--accent-bright);margin-top:0;">Spiritual Insight</h3>
<p>The annual festival of Mother Tara at Shantikunj represents a sacred time of renewal and devotion. The Chokkhudaan ritual—painting the deity's eyes—symbolizes awakening the divine consciousness and inviting the Mother's presence to reside fully in her form. Preparations include beautiful temple decorations, devotional music with traditional drums (Dhak), and the gathering of devotees who stay awake through the night in vigil. While Gurudev has scaled back the grandiosity of the celebrations, the essential elements remain: the Homa fire ritual, the presence of honored guests including local officials, and the joyful participation of the community. This foundation, built by "Bapan" (the elder/predecessor), continues to serve as a beacon for spiritual seekers who come to experience the living presence of the Divine Mother.</p>
