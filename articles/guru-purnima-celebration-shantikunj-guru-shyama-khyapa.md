<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Yes, it has been busy since 7:00 AM. I tried to stay away for a bit, but the situation is the same everywhere—there is a huge crowd. People are coming from far and wide to offer their worship and touch my feet. What else can I say? This is a very auspicious day. The full moon of the month of Ashadh is known as Guru Purnima. It is a day dedicated to the Guru. Everyone comes to show their respect in their own way. We have made arrangements for food here; we expect to serve around 500 people.

I also have two very close disciples here—Snehasis and Subhasis. They are brothers. They have immense devotion and respect for me.</span>

They are among Gurudeb's most beloved disciples.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">(to the brothers) Brothers, you write such beautiful letters to Gurudeb. Where do you get this inspiration and this deep-seated devotion?</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">Snehasis/Subhasis:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Look, everything is a gift from Gurudev. It is something that exists within, and it just needs to be awakened. Those who walk the right path and practice mindfulness receive this strength naturally from within. It doesn't require extra effort. It is a Sanskar (latent impression) from previous births. That Sanskar remains in a subtle form and gradually becomes manifest through the Guru's grace. This potential is within everyone; some awaken it, and some don't.

Furthermore, everyone is at a different stage of spiritual development. We respect Gurudev in this way, while others might show even greater devotion—they might be many steps ahead of us. It is a journey that spans many lifetimes. Ultimately, everyone will attain Mukti (liberation). As Swami Vivekananda said, everyone will reach liberation, but it happens stage by stage.</span>
</div>
