<strong>Om. May the Divine protect us both. May He nourish us both. May we work together with great energy. May our study be enlightening and not give rise to hostility. Om, Peace, Peace, Peace.</strong>

Today’s session is dedicated to a discussion of a song by the devotee Ramprasad.

The core message of the song is: do not be swayed by the whims of the mind; do not follow false paths.

If heaven exists within the mind, why search for it outside? We are truly deluded. We haven't learned to love our inner soul. That is why we suffer from the duality of good and evil, sin and virtue, and better or worse.

Ramprasad says, "O mind, why do you wander about in confusion? The Supreme Soul resides right within you."

The language of the song is simple, but its essence is profound. Ramprasad was primarily a devotee of Mother Kali. His songs reflect the inseparable bond between a mother and her child.

He says that the Mother is right beside us; we only need to open our eyes and see.

In the sweetness of Ramprasadi songs, we find peace of mind. Let us now listen to this song and try to understand its inner meaning.</span>
