The ancient saying, "Vidya dadati vinayam" (Knowledge brings humility), is forgotten because parents themselves aren't humble.</span>

In our day, we respected our teachers; today, children just offer a casual "Hi" or "Hello."</span>

We are moving toward a directionless society composed of people without emotions or respect.</span>
