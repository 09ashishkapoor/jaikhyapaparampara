<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Pranam, baba. Pranam.</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">DISCIPLE:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Gurudev, we have discussed many gods and various people over a long period. But there is one place that a person can reach in their physical body—and that is Shibalok (the realm of Shiva). We consider Mount Kailash to be the abode of Shiva. Could you tell us something about Kailash today?</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Certainly. You see, the word "Shiva" means "auspicious" (mangalmay). He who brings about well-being is Shiva. Plain and simple—He is the only one bringing auspiciousness to this world. No other deity serves the world’s well-being in quite this way.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Now, as you may know, there are fourteen realms (Bhubans). There is Bhulok, Dyulok, Shibalok, Vaikuntha-lok, Brahmaloka, and so on. What we understand as Shibalok is located beyond Mansarovar—the snow-covered peaks of Kailash. We consider that the original abode of Mahadev. And He never leaves that place. Mahadev remains there in deep meditation (dhyanastha). If you manage to see Him there, you will find Him in a meditative state, looking nowhere else, constantly chanting. Who is He chanting for? That is a profound mystery.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">We know of three main deities: Brahma, Vishnu, and Maheshwar (Shiva). Brahma’s presiding goddess is Saraswati; Narayan’s is Lakshmi; and Shiva’s presiding goddess is Maa Durga. Shiva and Durga reside together in Kailash.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Kailash is covered entirely in ice. People do attempt to climb it, but many cannot go very far. They fall, they die—the snowstorms are fierce. It is extremely difficult terrain and at a very high altitude. At those heights, humans can hardly speak, let alone breathe properly. Yet, people still go. Hundreds have died there.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">There is a concern now that Kailash is becoming "polluted" because so many bodies have remained there over the years, buried under the ice. There is no way to clear them. Recently, someone was recovered from the ice after 70 years—bodies remain perfectly preserved in that cold.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Lately, many people have been traveling there, sitting in meditation and chanting. A constant "Omkar" sound can be heard in the wind. Whether it is the wind passing through the mountains or something else, the sound of "Ooooom" resonates through the peaks. Many pilgrims have heard this.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">There are places to sit and meditate, even small dwellings. But the core essence is that Shiva is "Kaal" (Time), and He is also beyond Time. If He blesses someone, no one else has the power to interfere.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Consider the story of Kuber. When Ravana gained blessings from Brahma and became powerful, he began seizing property. Kuber, who was Ravana’s half-brother—Ravana was a Rakshasa and Kuber a Yaksha—went to their father, Vishrava Muni. He said, "Father, I am in trouble. Ravana has gained Brahma's blessing and might take my wealth." His father advised him, "Go to the Himalayas and pray to Mahadev. When He appears, tell Him your troubles."</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Today, we see many Lamas in that region. There are Lamas who are 400 years old, and some say there are those who are 5,000 years old—sages who claim to have witnessed the Kurukshetra war. This is the creation of Mahadev; Time cannot enter there. It is said to be a different dimension where "Time" as we know it doesn't function.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">DISCIPLE:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">It is said that in Kailash, the dimension of time doesn't work.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Exactly. Because of that, people's hair and nails grow much faster than normal. No one can accurately tell their age once they enter that space. Sages of 300, 400, or 500 years live in the crevices of those mountains.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Look at the Naga Sadhus who appear at the Kumbh Mela. Where do they come from and where do they go? No one really knows. They come from the regions around Kailash. There are various Akhadas (orders), like the Niranjani Akhada and the Juna Akhada. In the Kumbh, the Niranjani Akhada bathes first, followed by the Juna Akhada, and so on through the thirteen orders. Among them, you find sadhus who are hundreds of years old. Some even claim to be 5,000 years old. How is this possible? It’s because death does not work there. There is a saying: "Come one, come all, to the place where there is neither death nor old age." By Mahadev's grace, those things do not exist there—though people still die from the extreme cold, sometimes -70 degrees.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">DISCIPLE:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Gurudev, there is also a belief that there are secret gates in Kailash that can transport a person to another place instantly.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Yes, they use Asana Vidya (the science of the meditative seat). They can travel from one cave to another, or one peak to another, by floating on their asana.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">DISCIPLE:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Like we see in the stories of Aladdin and the magic carpet?</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Exactly like that. It is Asana travel. They sit on it, and it takes them where they need to go. There is also a place called "Gyan Ganj" hidden within Kailash.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">DISCIPLE:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Gyan Ganj?</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Yes, there are actually five Gyan Ganjes. The most famous resident is "Khepai Ma," who is said to be between 2,500 to 3,000 years old. There are two women there of that age. I know Khepai Ma—she looks remarkably like Maa Sarada (the consort of Ramakrishna Paramhansa). There is even a Sarada Math there.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Accessing these places is difficult. These days, there is a registration process in Delhi, medical checkups, and a journey that takes about a month. For common people, it's a long trek. But if you go via Gapa by train, it's a two-month walk.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">People like Arun Sharma and Shankar Maharaj have visited Gyan Ganj (or Shangri-La). Arun Sharma was traveling through Arunachal Pradesh; as evening fell, he feared being eaten by wild animals. He looked up and saw a dome. He hurried toward it and found himself in Shangri-La/Gyan Ganj. He stayed there for a long time.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">There are hundreds of Paramhansas and Dandi Swamis meditating in those mountains. You might have heard of "Gandha Baba" (the Fragrant Baba)—his body naturally emitted a beautiful scent. Or "Borfani Baba." They live on "Pawan" (air) alone. They don't eat because they want to avoid the need for bodily functions in that sacred, frozen space.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">DISCIPLE:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Gurudev, some say that China attempted to research the magnetic fields of Kailash.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">China, and even the Mughal emperors, tried to uncover the secrets of Kailash because they were lured by the idea of people living for thousands of years. But they couldn't find anything. Many Mughal expeditions were sent, but they found no trace. China sent planes, but the planes were destroyed. There is a strange magnetic field there. Our clocks and compasses work on magnetism, but in Kailash, that magnetism is so powerful that normal time doesn't apply. You come out in the same state you went in, often losing track of how many years have passed. Time stands still. That is why Shiva is called Kaalatit (Beyond Time).</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">We might talk about many realms—the 14 Bhubans—but the only one we can truly "feel" or reach is Shiva’s. We feel He is close to us. While Narayan is said to be millions of miles away in the Garuda Purana, Shiva is right here in the Himalayas.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">When a person dies, it is said they must pass through 16 difficult realms, including the river Vaitarani. This is described in the Garuda Purana for those who have lived wickedly. But for the righteous, the path is different.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">In Kailash, there is a place called "Yam-dwara" (the Gate of Death) near Mansarovar. Pilgrims must perform a ritual there, seeking permission from Yam (the God of Death) before they can proceed to Shibalok or bathe in Mansarovar. The air there is incredibly strong—blowing at 70-80 km/h.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">
<span class="speaker-name" style="font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-bright);margin-right:0.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;font-size:1rem;">GURUDEV:</span>
<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">In Mansarovar, people often see two swans—a male and a female—who are believed to be Shiva and Durga in form. If you are fortunate, you might see them. There is also the "Brahma Kamal," a magnificent flower that grows in the snow. Kailash is truly a place of wonders.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Gurudev, thank you for this profound discussion on Kailash.</span>

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Be careful, be mindful. "Walk carefully, my mind, stay alert. The day fades, and darkness approaches. The Creator will judge you, and there, no trickery will work."</span>
