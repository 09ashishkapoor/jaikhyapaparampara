<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">Yes, the annual function that we hold every year will be on the 13th this time, due to the Solar Eclipse. I don't see this in a positive light, which is why we moved it to the day before. We will have the night before the New Moon, and even if it falls on that night, we will be able to manage.</span>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">So, considering all that, we are holding it a day early. The following day is not good; various calamities could occur at any time. It's Saturday, there's an eclipse, and then the New Moon. Nowadays, the New Moon and Full Moon periods are becoming very intense. We don't know what might happen, so after thinking it over, we decided it's better to perform the puja on that night since we'll have the New Moon then anyway.</span>
</div>

<div class="speaker-block" style="margin-bottom:2rem;border-left:3px solid var(--accent-color);padding-left:1.5rem;">

<span class="speech-text" style="display:block;margin-top:0.5rem;">I think it will be much better. The deity's ornamentation (Angarag) is also finished; everything was completed today. I've seen this in the almanacs, and many great scholars of today are saying the same—that the day is not good. After considering everything, we will finish the Mother's puja on Friday.</span>
</div>
