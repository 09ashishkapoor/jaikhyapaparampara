<h2>The Command of the Mother</h2>

I stay with 'Ma' (the Goddess). To be honest, I didn't like meeting people, and I still don't. But I am doing this now because it is Ma's order. It is her instruction that I must do this to help others.

Even my Guru, Bama Khyapa, used to get very annoyed when he saw people. Ma told him his job was to look after people, and he was forced to agree, even though he often expressed his frustration to her. Similarly, Ma kept me hidden in meditation in a 6x6 room and the nearby cremation ground (Shmashan) until I was ready.

<h2>Staying Hidden in Plain Sight</h2>

I spent over two decades in a closed room. Only a few people knew. After I gave initiation to a student named Joy Chakraborty and his problems were resolved, he suggested sharing this knowledge on YouTube so others could benefit. Initially, I refused, but Ma said, "Yes, you have to give this."

As soon as I started, people from all over the world began to come. Now, it is overwhelming. I often scold Ma because handling so many demands is not possible for a person like me. But she does 80% of the work. I give the initial push, and then I tell the seekers to go to Ma.

<h2>The Temple Without Money</h2>

I don't take money here, nor do I run a business. These two things are strictly forbidden. It is written at the temple: **"Do not give a single rupee in this temple."** I don't want to pollute Ma's sacred abode with money. 

I sacrificed many temptations to remain hidden. For years, I worshiped without clothes ('ulanga') because that was my way of serving Ma. Now that people come, I wear simple clothes so I don't stand out. I discarded red clothes and rings long ago because they bring ego and 'ripu' (internal enemies).

<h2>Leaving a Vibration</h2>

Sadhaks leave behind a vibration that lasts for centuries. Even if the saint is no longer physically there—like at Dakshineswar or Tarapith—the vibration continues to heal people. I am creating such a vibration here so that people get better even when I am no longer around.

Truth is God. You must always be honest, for God stays with the honest. My time is also coming to an end. Now it is time for the final 'giving and taking' with Ma—a spiritual calculation of what I gave her and what she gave me. This is a private matter between a Sadhak and the Mother.
