<h2>The Disruption of Isostasy</h2>

In scientific terms, we call the Earth's natural balance "isostasy." When we level mountains for ore or build massive concrete structures in cities like Kolkata, we disturb this pressure balance. The Earth responds to this instability with earthquakes as it attempts to regain its equilibrium. 

Furthermore, the expansion of concrete surfaces creates "Urban Heat Islands." These concentrated zones of heat are so intense they can even deflect major cyclones, but at a terrible long-term cost to the ecosystem. Our "cosmetic" modern life is lived on a world of dreams where we have no right to survive if we continue to destroy the foundation.

<h2>The Impending Kalpanta (Cosmic End)</h2>

We have entered a transition period between cosmic cycles. Scripturally, we are moving through the age of the Manus. The eighth Manu has ended, and we are in the ninth, the age of the sun. This shift is mirrored by the environmental collapse. 

As global warming melts the glaciers, it will eventually lead us toward a perpetual ice age. Nature will simply evict humanity first, as we are the most disruptive force. Life will have to start over from the simplest cell.

<h2>The Final Notice</h2>

Nature is giving us its final notice. With all our technology, we still rely on green plants for every bite of food. Our only hope is to return to a state where we respect nature as something sacred. If we cannot stop being "modern" and start being truly "human," nature will settle the account itself.
