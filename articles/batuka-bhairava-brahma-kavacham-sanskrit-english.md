The **Shri Batuka Bhairava Brahma Kavacham** is a powerful protective armor (*kavacham*) from the **Shri Rudra Yamala Tantra**. Batuka Bhairava — "the young Bhairava" — is the fierce yet benevolent child-form of Lord Bhairava, remover of misfortunes and destroyer of all obstacles. This kavacham was revealed by Lord Shiva himself to the Goddess Parvati, and its 22-syllable root mantra is:

  <strong>oṃ hrīṃ baṭukāya āpaduddhāraṇāya kuru kuru baṭukāya hrīṃ</strong>
  <div style="font-size:0.9rem;color:var(--text-muted,#bbb);margin-top:0.75rem;font-style:italic;">Chant 11 or 21 times before and after reciting the Kavacham</div>

<h3>The Prologue — Parvati Asks, Shiva Answers</h3>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 1</div>
bhagavansarvavettā tvaṃ devānāṃ prītidāyakam .<br/>
bhairavaṃ kavacaṃ brūhi yadi cāsti kṛpā mayi ..
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
O Lord, you are the knower of all things. You are the one who gives joy to the gods. If you have mercy on me, please tell me the Kavacham of Bhairava.

</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 2</div>

</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
If you do not tell me, I shall give up my very life. This is the truth, the truth, and again the truth — there is no doubt whatsoever.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 3</div>

uvāca vacanaṃ tatra devadevo maheśvaraḥ ..
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Hearing these words of the Goddess, the great Lord Maheshvara — God among gods — smiled tenderly and began to speak.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 4</div>
bāṭukaṃ kavacaṃ divyaṃ śṛṇu matprāṇavallabhe .<br/>

</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
O beloved of my life, listen now to the divine Batuka Kavacham. It is the very essence of the Chandika Tantra, especially dedicated to Batuka.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 5</div>

śaṅkhavarṇadvayo brahmā baṭukaścandraśekharaḥ ..
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
The first syllable of its mantra is the very form of Vishnu. Brahma is pure white like a conch shell. Batuka wears the crescent moon upon his head.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 6</div>
āpaduddhāraṇo devo bhairavaḥ parikīrtitaḥ .<br/>
pravakṣyāmi samāsena caturvargaprasiddhaye ..
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Bhairava is celebrated as the god who lifts us out of all misfortune and danger. I shall now teach it briefly so that you may attain the four goals of human life.
</div>
</div>

</div>

<div class="kavach-section">
<h3>The Root Mantra Explained (Verses 7–9)</h3>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 7</div>
praṇavaḥ kāmadaṃ vidyā lajjābījaṃ ca siddhidam .<br/>
baṭukāyeti vijñeyaṃ mahāpātakanāśanam ..
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
<em>Oṃ</em> fulfills desires. <em>Hrīṃ</em> (the Lajja-bija) grants perfection and success. The word <em>Batukaya</em> is known to destroy even the greatest sins.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 8</div>
āpaduddhāraṇāyeti tvāpaduddhāraṇaṃ nṛṇām .<br/>
kurudvayaṃ maheśāni mohane parikīrtitam ..
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
The phrase <em>Apaduddharanaya</em> literally removes misfortune from human beings. The double <em>Kuru Kuru</em> is specifically used for enchantment and attracting beings toward the divine.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 9</div>
baṭukāya maheśāni stambhane parikīrtitam .<br/>
lajjābījaṃ tathā vidyānmuktidaṃ parikīrtitam ..
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
The final <em>Batukaya</em> is used for paralyzing and stopping obstacles in their path. And <em>Hrīṃ</em> at the end is said to grant liberation itself.
</div>
</div>

</div>

<h3>The Kavacham — The Divine Armor (Verses 10–21)</h3>

<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
May <em>Batukaya</em> protect the navel and the lower regions. May the double <em>Kuru Kuru</em> and Batukaya protect the root base of life.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 12</div>

ṣaḍaṅgasahito devo nityaṃ rakṣatu bhairavaḥ ..

<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
May the seed <em>Hrīṃ</em> always protect both arms without fail. And may Lord Bhairava — complete with his six limbs — protect me every day and night.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 13</div>
oṃ hrīṃ baṭukāya satataṃ sarvāṅgaṃ mama sarvadā ..<br/>

</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
May the full mantra protect every limb of my body always. May Mahakala protect my feet; may Virasana protect my heart.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 14</div>

</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
May Kala protect my head; may Bhairava guard my throat. May Ganarat protect my tongue, together with the eight Shaktis who surround him.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 15</div>

</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
May Dandapani, along with Bhairavi, guard my private parts. May Vishvanatha protect every single part of my body at all times and in all ways.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">

<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 16</div>

āsitāṅgaḥ śiraḥ pātu lalāṭaṃ rurubhairavaḥ ..
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
May Annapurna always protect me; may Chandika guard my shoulders. May Asitanga protect my head; may Ruru Bhairava guard my forehead.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 17</div>

</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
May Chanda Bhairava protect my face; may Krodha Bhairava guard my throat. May Unmatta Bhairava protect my heart at all times.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 18</div>

</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
May Kapali protect the navel region; may Bhishana Bhairava guard the reproductive center. May Samhara Bhairava always protect the root base — the Muladhara.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">

</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
May Bhairava alone always protect both my arms. May the Hamsa seed syllable protect the heart; may the great <em>So'ham</em> — "I am That" — protect my feet.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">

<div class="verse-english">

May he protect my five vital breaths — prana, apana, samana, udana, and vyana — and may he guard all ten directions around me completely.

</div>

</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
May <em>Oṃ</em> protect my whole body; may <em>Hrīṃ</em> protect me in states of great fear. Thus ends the celebrated Brahma Kavacham of Bhairava.
</div>
</div>

<h3>Phalashruti — The Fruits of Recitation (Verses 22–32)</h3>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 22</div>
catuvargapradaṃ nityaṃ svayaṃ devaprakāśitam .<br/>
yaḥ paṭhecchṛṇuyānnityaṃ dhārayetkavacottamam ..
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
This kavacham bestows all four goals of life daily. It was revealed by the Lord himself. One should read it, hear it, or keep it on one's body.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 23</div>
sadānandamayo bhūtvā labhate paramaṃ padam .<br/>
yaḥ idaṃ kavacaṃ devi cintayenmanmukhoditam ..
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Becoming filled with eternal bliss, the devotee attains the supreme state. O Goddess, one who meditates on this kavacham — spoken from my own lips — reaches the highest.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 24</div>
koṭijanmārjitaṃ pāpaṃ tasya naśyati tatkṣaṇāt .<br/>
jalamadhye'gnimadhye vā durgrahe śatrusaṅkaṭe ..
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
The sins accumulated over millions of births are destroyed in an instant. It protects in the midst of floods, fires, the influence of malefic planets, and danger from enemies.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 25</div>
kavacasmaraṇāddevi sarvatra vijayī bhavet .<br/>
bhaktiyuktena manasā kavacaṃ pūjayedyadi ..
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
O Goddess, one who remembers this kavacham becomes victorious in all undertakings. And if one worships it with a mind full of true devotion, the results are even greater.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 26</div>
kāmatulyastu nārīṇāṃ ripūṇāṃ ca yamopamaḥ .<br/>
tasya pādāmbujadvandaṃ rājñāṃ mukuṭabhūṣaṇam ..
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
He becomes like the god of love to those who love him, and like the god of death to his enemies. Even kings bow their crowned heads at his lotus feet.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 27</div>
tasya bhūtiṃ vilokyaiva kubero'pi tiraskṛtaḥ .<br/>
yasya vijñānamātreṇa mantrasiddhirna saṃśayaḥ ..
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
Seeing his prosperity, even Kubera — the god of wealth — is put to shame. By merely knowing this kavacham, the attainment of mantra-siddhi (perfection) is certain without doubt.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 28</div>
idaṃ kavacamajñātvā yo japedbaṭukaṃ naraḥ .<br/>
na cāpnoti phalaṃ tasya paraṃ narakamāpnuyāt ..
</div>
<div class="verse-english">
<div class="verse-label">English Meaning</div>
One who chants the Batuka mantra without first knowing this kavacham gets no fruit at all from that practice, and may face a downward path in future lives.
</div>
</div>

<div class="verse-grid">
<div class="verse-sanskrit">
<div class="verse-label">Sanskrit · Verse 29</div>
