Not knowing what else to do, Satyavrata picked up the fish, brought it home, and put it in a pot. 

But the fish continuously kept growing larger. It grew so big that it again said, "Save me!" So, Satyavrata placed it in a pond. But its growth was so massive that he eventually had to move it to a river, and then from the river to the ocean. Yet, nothing changed; it just kept growing. 

Finally, Lord Vishnu revealed Himself in His avatar form. He told the King, "In seven days, a great calamity (a massive flood) will strike the Earth. Gather all your belongings, collect the seeds of all plants and herbs, take them onto a ship, and stay there." 

King Satyavrata obeyed Narayana's command. He gathered all necessary resources, from crops to seeds, and placed them on the ship. Seven days later, a true cataclysmic flood (*Pralaya*) occurred.
