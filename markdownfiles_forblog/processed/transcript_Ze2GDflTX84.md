Speaker 1: From Rajpur Mahashashan Shantunj, we extend our heartfelt love and greetings to every visitor. Pranam (greetings), Gurudev.

Speaker 2: And it will create a terrible sound.

Gurudev: Naturally. That is what I believe is happening, though they have their own interpretation. One thing is certain—an explosion will occur wherever that object lands. Now, among the four Dhams (sacred abodes), the Dham of the Kali Yuga is Jagannath Dham. Prophecies suggest that Jagannath Dham will be hit hard; there will be a terrible surge of water, and the temple will be submerged. It is said that fish will swim over the temple.

Speaker 2: Six districts will be flooded. They will be washed away.

Gurudev: If this large celestial object falls into the sea near Puri today, as predicted, there will be a massive upheaval. Forget the sound; the physical impact and the resulting tsunami will be enormous.

Speaker 2: In the Satya Yuga, it was Badrinath. In the Treta Yuga, it was Rameshwaram. In the Dwapara Yuga, it was Dwarka. And in our Kali Yuga, it is Jagannath Puri. These are the four Dhams.

Gurudev: And prophecies say all four will vanish. They will no longer exist. Even the four "smaller" Dhams in Uttarakhand—Kedarnath, Badrinath, Gangotri, and Yamunotri—will disappear.

Speaker 1: Gangotri and Yamunotri too?

Gurudev: Yes. It is said that the Ganga will return to heaven. The Saraswati has already vanished; now it is the Ganga's turn. The glacier at Gomukh, from which the Ganga flows, has already melted significantly. As the Ganga dries up, it will cease to flow, and eventually, it will be gone.

Speaker 2: The Ganga was brought from heaven by Bhagirath through the grace of Lord Shiva.

Gurudev: Yes. When Bhagirath saw that his ancestors had been destroyed and needed the holy water of the Ganga for salvation, he performed penance. Lord Shiva appeared and asked what he wanted. Bhagirath asked to bring the Ganga to Earth (the mortal world). At that time, the ocean water was salty and unfit for agriculture. They believed that if "sweet" water (the Ganga) reached the sea, farming would flourish.

Speaker 2: And so, through Shiva’s matted hair, the Ganga descended.

Gurudev: Shiva initially held her captive in his hair because she was proud. Eventually, he released her for the sake of humanity. He blessed her, saying that anyone who bathes in her waters—even those who have committed the gravest sins—would be purified.

Speaker 1: But if the Ganga dries up, the salt water from the sea will push further inland.

Gurudev: Exactly. Agriculture will fail. In fact, it has already begun. Near Dakshineswar, scientists are finding oak trees, which typically only grow in salty soil. This suggests that seawater is already encroaching. If the sweet water of the Ganga stops flowing, the salt water will take over completely.

Speaker 2: This is a grave concern. We don't know exactly when, but the Bhavishya Malika (ancient book of prophecies) mentions all of this.

Speaker 1: So, will we see this destruction within the next 24 hours?

Gurudev: Look, this is the plan of the Creator. The prophecies do not say the entire Earth will be destroyed, but they do mention the coming of Lord Kalki. The Muslim scriptures also suggest that the world's time is limited. They believe this is the final Ramadan.

Speaker 2: They say after Ramadan, there will be a loud sound.

Gurudev: I interpret that sound as the impact of the celestial object falling into the sea. I have been listening closely, but I haven't heard it yet. They say it will happen after evening or during the night. We will remain watchful.

Speaker 1: Gurudev, please pray to God for everyone's well-being so that everyone remains safe and healthy.

Gurudev: I cannot do that now. This is not the time for such prayers. Right now, it is the time of God’s destruction. God has appeared in a destructive form. It is not right for me or anyone to pray against His will. The prophecies state that out of the entire world's population, only 630 million people will survive. The rest will perish. In India, only 330 million will remain out of 1.4 billion. Imagine where all those bodies will go. This is what is written; I am only repeating it.

Speaker 1: It is frightening because these were the words of great mahatmas (sages).
