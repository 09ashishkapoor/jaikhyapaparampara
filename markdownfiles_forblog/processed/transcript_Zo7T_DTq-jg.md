Disciple: Greetings, Gurudev.

Gurudev: Greetings, my son.

Disciple: Gurudev, yesterday evening around 7:30 PM, a saint from Odisha called you. He mentioned that although you have made several episodes on the Bhavishya Malika (ancient book of prophecies), West Bengal is still asleep. He said the people of West Bengal need to be awakened and that you must lead this effort. What specifically did he say?

Gurudev: Yes, what he told me was that West Bengal is still dormant. There hasn’t been a shift in consciousness there yet. They have deployed fifteen people globally to promote the message of Bhavishya Malika in various countries. I have been given the responsibility for West Bengal.

Upon looking into West Bengal, I realized that the general public is indeed still asleep. Their consciousness hasn't been stirred. I am saying this because the time is drawing very near. If people do not call upon God now—specifically Lord Krishna—it will be too late. The whole world is beginning to call out to Him; even people of other faiths have started. If the people here don’t act now, calling out at the final moment won't help.

Disciple: So, awareness is needed immediately?

Gurudev: Absolutely. Awareness is vital because a very difficult time is approaching. He emphasized this repeatedly. He mentioned that he is a holder of the Bhavishya Malika and has appointed me as a promoter for West Bengal. He assured me of his support whenever needed. He gave me his name as well.

Disciple: Do you know his name?

Gurudev: Yes, his name is Pravin Bhaiyaji. He said there is no time left and requested me to make one specific appeal to the people of West Bengal. Whether they listen or not is up to them, but the request is this: Give up fish, meat, eggs, and alcohol.

Disciple: So, he is advising everyone to become vegetarian?

Gurudev: Yes, he is urging people to stop consuming these things. I asked him why, especially since Bengal is a land where fish and rice are staples. People will eat them regardless of what I say. It is the favorite food of Bengalis. But he insisted that fish, meat, and eggs must be avoided. He said consuming these will lead to great difficulties in the future.

He asked me to make this request because the people of West Bengal know and trust me. He wants me to tell them to wake up.

Disciple: So, through your outreach, you want people to become aware?

Gurudev: Yes, and the core message is to abstain from alcohol, meat, fish, and eggs. People in West Bengal think they can continue as they are, but a terrifying time is coming. Tell them that when that time arrives, calling upon God won’t change the outcome. They must try to call upon Him now—specifically Sri Krishna.

Chant the sixteen-word, thirty-two-name Maha Mantra. Do it with devotion and sincerity. If not, there will be consequences. Those who continue to consume meat, fish, and eggs are described in the Bhavishya Malika as "Yavanas" (barbarians/outcasts), and Sri Krishna will not spare them.

I am not adding any words of my own; this is what is written in the prophecies. I am trying to make people understand that they should give up these habits. In West Bengal, people seem to be in the dark, almost in a trance-like state of ignorance.

Pravin Bhaiyaji mentioned he was pleased with my episodes and believes I am an honest person, which is why he reached out. He wants the message to spread through me.

The Bhavishya Malika is essentially the "Jagannath Bhagavat." It was written 500 years ago by five saints. Everything they predicted has come true—not a single word has failed. And the text clearly states that during this time, fish, meat, and alcohol must be abandoned.

There should be no more indulgence. If you do not change, you will be labeled a "Yavana." You will have to face severe struggles. I am requesting everyone to give up these foods.

Disciple: West Bengal is being singled out?

Gurudev: Yes, because other states have apparently already begun to change and take a stand. Only West Bengal remains behind. I am speaking on behalf of the Bhavishya Malika and Lord Jagannath: please wake up and remember God. Calling out at the very end will be futile.

According to the prophecies, the time has arrived. There is no time left. Please try now so that your lives may be saved or at least you may be spared from extreme misery. Otherwise, the suffering will be intense.

I am spreading this message as requested. People of West Bengal, if you listen, it is for your own good. If you don't, you will suffer. The prophecies say that those consuming meat and eggs are eating the food of "Rakshasas" (demons). This is the food of the "Yavanas." If you eat it, you will regret it, and Sri Krishna will punish you.

I am requesting everyone once again: abandon these things and remember Lord Krishna. Chant "Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare / Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare." You don't need elaborate rituals or to worship various deities right now. Just chant this name morning and evening. That is the only requirement mentioned in the Bhavishya Malika for this time. I have said this before, and I am saying it again. Give up meat, fish, eggs, and alcohol.

Pravin Bhaiyaji noted that while these are consumed elsewhere, the scale in West Bengal is unique. He asked for my help because of my platform. I am fulfilling that request. Live a simple, virtuous, and "Sadhu-like" life. Otherwise, the coming destruction will be unavoidable.

Disciple: Gurudev has shared the warnings from the prophecies. Please stay alert, listen to his words, and follow them for your own well-being. Do not remain in a state of sin, as it will only lead to harm.

Gurudev: Jai Madhav. Respect to all.
