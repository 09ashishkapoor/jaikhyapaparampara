What has weakened is that the Gurukulas and Sampradayas are no longer as strong as they were before. There are some who might still be holding onto the values of a few thousand years back, but most have been infiltrated by people not of that caliber. What happens is that people of caliber have gone into the materialistic world, started running after materialistic goals, and are succeeding really well. By the time they get back to the fold of spirituality, it just becomes "retirement spirituality." They do not have time to climb up the ladders of the Sampradayas.

Therefore, we have to assume that there is a chance that whoever is actually in the position of Gurus may not be as great as we hoped. But yes, there are great Gurus too. If you have such a Guru in your life, then you are blessed. There is nothing this video can give you that the Guru hasn’t already given you.

The Role of the Guru and Self-Initiation Coming back to "Part 2" of Before the Basics: As I said, everything is there on the internet. You do not need guidance on how to recite a mantra because, unlike before where only a certain section of society knew how to read Sanskrit and the rest were illiterate, today everybody can read English, Hindi, and regional languages. They are able to read the translated languages of Sanskrit itself. Sanskrit in English allows you to get quite close to the pronunciation. It is just that certain Beeja Mantras or Beeja Aksharas might need someone to just recite it once for you, but once you get it, you can read it in English itself.

All of this is not to be looked down upon. You cannot say that just because there are no ashrams that seat students under the trees and give them mantras, the mantras have lost their power. Nor can you say that just because you have picked up a mantra from the internet, you are not worthy of doing it. No. These are all very arrogant statements that are hypocritical in themselves.

You also should realize that the deities are not that weak. Just because somebody randomly picked up a mantra—but puts his heart, soul, time, and effort into it, and bleeds for that mantra—if you think the deities are reacting to him solely based on "procedural collapse" (meaning, where there is a collapse in written niyamas, Bhairava will not be there), that is incorrect.

If somebody is putting their heart, blood, and soul into reciting a random mantra without guidance, please understand that one way or the other, if Bhairava likes you, he will pick you out. He will take you right out of that place, make you sit in front of him, and he will teach you. So, never assume a sense of superiority because you have a Guru. Someone who does not have a Guru will probably progress slowly, but in fact, there is also a chance that he might be on par or even go beyond you. This level of understanding you should have because this is the Kali Yuga, and things are not as they were.

If you have a Guru, hold onto them. If it is truly a worthy Guru, grow with them. If you do not have a Guru, do not panic. Do not get into a mode of dejection. Because again, this is Bhairava. This is someone who does not look at the entire process of invocation of deities the way other deities do. If he likes you, he will pick you. He will guide you. That is the kind of force that he is.

Do Not Mock Others As much as possible, do not get into a "mock-fest." If you are blessed with a very good Guru, be happy for it. But if you find somebody walking around with a "walking stick" but has the intent, be very careful—especially when it comes to Bhairava Upasana—that you do not mock him. You have no clue about the abilities of Bhairava. He resides in "procedural collapse." Where there is a collapse in the written niyamas, where there is a collapse in the way things are supposed to be, Bhairava will be there.

If you mock someone progressing on that path who is doing really well, you will get it immediately. In the next 30-40 days, you will see your life spiraling out of control.

How to Start: Batuka Bhairava Start off with Batuka Bhairava. That is the right way to start a Bhairava Upasana. Even if you are specifically not inclined towards going to Batuka, please understand why you should: He has all the Shaktis of Kala Bhairava, but in a child form. He is easier for you to digest, no matter your history in Sadhana or how many other deities you have been propitiating.

This is the deity for almost anybody who has a little bit of Ganesha Upasana in their blood. Even if you have just been going to a few temples, you can start Batuka Bhairava. This is the form that will be the least risky and the highest rewarding, because he is a child.

The Procedure:

The Image: Just take out a photo of Batuka Bhairava from the internet. Make sure it is very Saumya (very calm and pleasing). Print it out.

Timing: Choose a timing where you sit down every day. Ensure it is a time you can keep to the best of your abilities. Sunset is mostly the best (around 6:00 PM to 7:00 PM), or sunrise. It is up to you.

Setup: Keep the photo in a dark room. Light a lamp.

Offering: Offer him something simple. Jaggery water is an excellent offering for Bhairava. You can offer fruits.

The Mantra: Just go about his very basic mantra. Do not get into any of the Beeja Mantras you find online; that is not where you start. Start with a basic mantra like:

Om Bhairavaya Namah

(While visualizing Batuka Bhairava).

This alone you can do for a year, and you will find tremendous progress. Or, if you want to get a little more into it, you can go with:

Om Batuka Bhairavaya Namah

(Adding the form to the generic mantra).

Continuously chant this. Take an Anushtana (vow). Say you will do 10 malas (rosaries), or do one. It doesn’t matter. Do not think of it as a competition. It is not about the number of mantras you do. He is not a deity for you to be counting. He will come to everybody who is innocently going towards him.

Bhairava is the kind of Guru Tattva within Shiva that is so loving. Your very first experience with Bhairava once you get on that journey will tell you how much love and compassion he has.

Approaching with Innocence When you go to him, go innocently. Don’t go saying, "I am from this Sampradaya," or "I am from this Guru." Even if you are from a great Sampradaya, do not go to Bhairava with a righteous chest-thumping. Go to him innocently, and he will come to you. This is the simple fact of Bhairava Upasana.

Go to him innocently, and he will choose you. He will make you sit in front of him, and you will start getting experiences really fast. For some who are able to really focus, it could be as fast as three months. You will see things happening around you, you will be able to make connections work, and realize why it is happening. You will get signs. It will be like a treasure hunt. That is a very fun-filled stage of Bhairava Upasana.

The Ritual Structure

Start with the mantra.

Make the offering in front of the photo.

Sit down every day.

Do the 108 names.

Do the Ashtakam (optional).

Ensure you do a Kavacham (Batuka Bhairava Kavacham).

Say a prayer of Kshama (forgiveness stotra), apologizing in case you made any mistakes, while mentally telling Bhairava: "I am trying to come to you. I don't know how. You guide me. If I am making any mistakes, it is only out of love for you."

This is Bholenath—the Guru Tattva within Bholenath. Do not overcomplicate it.

Feeding the Dogs While you sit down for your Puja, ensure that you keep some kind of biscuit or offering specifically meant for dogs aside. Keep his Naivedyam (food offering), and on the side, keep these biscuits.

After you have finished your Kavacham and your entire prayer for the session:

Get up.

Don't talk to anybody. Be very silent.
