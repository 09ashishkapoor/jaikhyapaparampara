**Disciple:** Hearing Gurudev’s words gives me goosebumps. Truly, God belongs to the devotee.

**Disciple:** Gurudev, I have a question. When the *Maha Mrityunjay Yagya* was being performed here, you went to Puri for a month. Could you tell us about that?

I went and stood inside the Jagannath temple. A gentleman came up to me and said, "What are you doing wandering here while the Yagya is stopped? Go back today and restart it." So I did. 

God is very merciful to His devotees. He shows Himself to everyone—the poor, the rich—He doesn't discriminate. Anyone who goes to His "Raj Darbar" (Royal Court) with a sincere prayer receives His blessings.
