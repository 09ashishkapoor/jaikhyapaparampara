Do You Need a Guru for Bhairava Sadhana?

Do you need a Guru for Bhairava Sadhana? What happens if you do not have a Guru, and what happens if you do? Let us look at two parts.

Scenario 1: Without a Guru You picked up a Mantra from the net—a basic Nama Mantra—and started doing it. You saw it in a video or somebody told it to you, and you went ahead. Nobody initiated it; you are just going.

Understand this: There is absolutely nothing wrong that can happen to you. You will be fine. You will be absolutely fine. No Papa (sin), nothing is going to hit you. You will do very well in life; you will find progress happening; you will find experiences happening.

The only thing you need to understand is that you need to keep going. Do not worry, thinking, "I am not getting any experiences, so am I progressing?" You need to remove this self-doubt. You need to tell yourself: "Until I get experiences, I will keep coming to You. I am a fool; I know nothing. I need to come to Bhairava, and I am going to come to You. I will keep reciting this Mantra until You give me Your Darshan, Your guidance, or show me the Guru who is going to teach me the path."

Trust me, He is very fast in reacting. By the first 48 days or so, you will generally start getting experiences. Keep going. Do not doubt yourself. If there are a thousand people talking about Bhairava, there will be one or two who are very genuine. If you are genuine and really want to attain Bhairava, keep going. Talk to Him and simply keep going with the Nama Mantra.

You can go with this Nama Mantra for a year, two years, or the entire 25 or 100 years of life that you have left. He is beyond all this. You cannot restrict Him by saying, "He did only the Nama Mantra, so I am not going to have any influence on his life." He is not like that. He will literally come and stand in front of you, and you will start experiencing things you cannot even describe to people because they will call you mad.

Scenario 2: With a Guru / Advanced Sadhak Now, let's come to the other part. You got the Mantra from a Guru or a very advanced Sadhak (practitioner) sitting in a corner making a video, and you took that video seriously.

Acceleration: If you have taken the Mantra from him—even if it is the same Mantra from the internet—when it is given by somebody who has put a considerable amount of years, time, and blood into that particular basic Nama Mantra, it will directly project you at a pace that will be really fast. There are no two ways about it. It will be faster because he is now putting his Prana Shakti (life force) into that. The Mantra is now empowered.

The Responsibility: However, I do not suggest you search for such a person and take guidance unless you are 100% sure that you will follow the Upadesha to the letter without a moment of self-doubt. Because, unlike taking this Mantra from the internet, you have now empowered yourself and are almost already in touch with Bhairava's energy through this person.

The Danger of Disrespecting Upadesha

If you take that Upadesha, take it into your head, and then walk off—you don't do it, you are not respectful to the Mantras, you zone out, you start and stop, some days you sit at 7:00, some days at 10:00, some days not at all—this has two implications:

Impact on the Guru: The Papa Karma will hit the person who has given you the Upadesha. There is no escaping that; it will hit him. But Bhairava will be very kind to that person because how much can he control you? He gave it to you with the proper intention so that you would progress.

Impact on You: If you took it from him and do not do it, it will majorly affect your eligibility for Bhairava Sadhana for the rest of that life. This will absolutely destroy your progress. Even in the next life, you probably will not be able to come into Bhairava Sadhana. There might be a fear of specific things to do with Bhairava or Mahakali, or the Shakti path itself might be very difficult for you to enter.

So, if you take a Mantra or Upadesha (in writing or by phone), do it properly. If the person is genuine and you are respectful, you will fly. If you are not respectful, you will create a huge hurdle.

Don't Chase the Guru, Chase Bhairava

Do not seek a Guru. Do not be in search of a human being. Go in search of Bhairava. Bhairava will send you to the human when you are ready.

Do not search saying, "This is a very famous guy, I want to run after him," or "I see a new video, amazing, I think he knows his stuff, I'll run after him." Go after Bhairava. If that video came into your life because Bhairava wants you to interact with this guy, then yes, it will go through smoothly.

But if you start your Sadhana looking for a human being to guide you, that is not the way. Full-fledged, jump into Bhairava Upasana. Make mistakes. He is a Guru. Don't get into these commercialized ideas that He will destroy anybody who makes a mistake. If you make a mistake, tell Him: "I am a fool. I have no clue what I am doing. If this is wrong, take me to somebody who will guide me right."

Do you really think a Guru like Bhairava will punish you for that? He will not. He will probably give you a rap on the head and take you to a person who can actually guide you.

Correcting Mistakes with Anusthan

If you have taken an Upadesha and feel you cannot follow it, take an Anusthan (a specific period of disciplined practice) and apologize to Bhairava. Take an Anusthan for 15, 20, or 48 days and apologize: "I took this Upadesha. I don't think I am fit for it. I don't think I can follow it. Please forgive me."

The person who gave you the Upadesha also has to apologize: "I got this opportunity. I did not realize the gravity of it. I gave the Upadesha to somebody who has not taken it seriously. Please do not attach any Papa Karma to me." Don't be casual when asking for Upadesha.

Ground Yourself: Become a Stone

I am noticing that many of you are getting into human worship. Be very careful. Be grateful to whoever is guiding you, be grateful to your Gurus and mentors, but go to Bhairava. If the human is taking you to himself and not to Bhairava, he is not a genuine Guru.

Do not get swayed. Connect to Bhairava all the time. Spend time, make friends in the Sadhana path, but do not let go of Sadhana.

If you are doing a proper Anusthan and you get bored—boredom, or the need for another path, another answer, or excitement—this is a bad sign. Do not look for excitement, at least for the first two or three years. Just the basic Nama Mantra can keep you going for 30 years.

Ground yourself. Become a stone.

Why do we pray to deities in stone? That is your biggest lesson. The stones don't move. Don't flutter around like a bee going to all the flowers; you will never enjoy one single flower. Be the flower. Don't try to go to 100 people.

Become monotonously boring for the first year or so. I assure you, it is when you are monotonously boring that the deities will become extremely interactive. Do not be overly explorative. If you find yourself fluttering, remove your slippers, go to a Goshala (cow shelter) or a nice piece of ground, and stand on the earth. Ground yourself.

It is like walking on the edge of a blade. If you walk properly, you are fine. If you start dancing around, you will cut your entire body and lose the ability to progress.

Conclusion

If you have taken any Upadesha, sincerely finish it. The Mantra is the body of the deity itself. The Bijaksharas (seed syllables) are the deities encoded in specific forms. Do not disrespect that.

Don't be adventurous. If you are meant for it, it will come to you. Calm down. This is not a cinema ticket counter where only the first 100 people get in. Become a stone, and Bhairava will come to you.

We will eventually talk about Mahakali and her Sadhana, but before that, you have to put a stage of Bhairava in your life, in your Atman. He will have to lie down here. If He lies down here, that is where Mahakali will come and stand.

Go slow. Be a stone. Enjoy the path.
