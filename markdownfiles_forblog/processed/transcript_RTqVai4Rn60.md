Firstly, Shukla Chaturdashi is ideal for any kind of worship of the Mother Goddess, and it is also ideal for the worship of Lord Shiva. Additionally, Pushya Nakshatra is the Mother’s own star. So, it occurred at a very unique juncture.

Gurudev: Not only that, it is mentioned in the Vedas regarding Pushya Nakshatra that any auspicious work can be performed during this time. There are three such Nakshatras (lunar mansions) we consider: Rohini, Pushya, and Revati. If we get any of these three, we can perform many tasks. Among them, Pushya is the best. You can do any benevolent work, and it will be successful.

Sujit Babu: And on top of that, today is Tuesday.

Gurudev: Yes, Tuesday. Anyway, everything has aligned perfectly.

Sujit Babu: And our Homa is performed for world peace. Naturally, we didn't want to miss such a juncture. That is why we brought Gurudev here to perform the ritual.

Gurudev: We finished the Homa before 1:32 PM. That is a very good sign—that you completed the ritual before then. Also, tomorrow is our most important day, Maghi Purnima (the full moon day of the month of Magh).

Today, Sujit asked, "Gurudev, will you come?" I said, "Yes, I will definitely go." Homa is something I love dearly. It makes me feel very good. Regarding tomorrow afternoon... tomorrow is indeed Purnima (full moon), and Maghi Purnima is a very, very auspicious day. But unfortunately, I might not be able to make it.
