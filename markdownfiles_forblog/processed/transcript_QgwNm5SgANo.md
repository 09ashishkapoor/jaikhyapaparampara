End of Kali Yuga! || by Shyama Khyapa YouTube Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QgwNm5SgANo

Here is a clean and well-formatted transcript of the audio, translated into English:

Disciple: Pranam, Gurudev.

Gurudev: Pranam.

Disciple: Gurudev, regarding the book Bhabisya Malika, some parts have been translated into Bengali and sent for printing. Prabhin Kumar Choubey from Asansol, West Bardhaman, and Pandit Kasinath Mishra from Odisha—who is the main person behind this—are working on it. They are translating the Bhabisya Malika, which was originally written in Odia, into Bengali and sending it to me. I have compiled the first part into a book format. If anyone wishes to read it, they can come here and read it, or they can take a copy by paying a nominal fee of 20 to 50 rupees. It is an invaluable treasure. Gurudev, could you please show the book?

Gurudev: Here it is. (Shows the book to the camera) Is it visible?

Disciple: Yes, it is. You can put it down now.

Gurudev: This is an invaluable asset. We are very fortunate. These gentlemen chose me to promote the message of Lord Jagannath. I have no objection; I have my YouTube channel where I can share these things. They are happy about it. They are sending me translated sections. Reading this will make people aware. Everyone should read it to know what will happen in the next seven years—not just in India, but across the world. It contains many prophecies. Anyone who reads this will be blessed. This is something you won't find anywhere else.

Disciple: This book was written 500 to 600 years ago, Gurudev.

Gurudev: It was written back then, but it is only coming to light now. It was kept secret for a long time. It was never released before. Now it is being revealed because a "Mandal" (organization) has been formed. Given the rate at which sins are increasing, a group called "Kalika Mandal" has emerged. It's all related to Krishna. There is a dialogue where Krishna speaks with Kali. That has also come to light. This is the authentic version. It predicts what will happen to India and the world, when the war will start, what the outcome will be, and which countries will take which sides. People must read this to be cautious. If they aren't careful, there will be great danger. It says that 700 crore (7 billion) people will perish in these seven years. I don't know exactly how, but one part mentions people dying while vomiting blood. Interestingly, a virus has recently been found in Africa where children are dying within an hour. This makes one wonder if such a disease is coming.

Disciple: It's not just diseases, Gurudev. There will be various calamities—earthquakes, and even a nuclear war is about to begin.

Gurudev: Yes, it has almost started. Taiwan and China are at odds, and the Russia-Ukraine conflict could lead to World War III.

Disciple: Then there are pandemics, famines, and droughts.

Gurudev: Yes, everything. There will be extreme rain in some places making farming impossible, and droughts in others. There will be a massive shortage of food. People will die of hunger. Many children will die without food. All of this is written there.

Disciple: It was all written beforehand, 600 years ago.

Gurudev: And many events have already occurred as predicted. In this Kali Yuga, our ultimate pilgrimage site is Puri Jagannath Dham. There are four Dhams for four eras: Badrinath for Satya Yuga, Rameswaram for Treta Yuga, Dwarka for Dvapara Yuga, and now Puri for Kali Yuga. The prophecies say all these Dhams will be destroyed. Even the smaller Dhams like Kedarnath, Amarnath, Gangotri, and Yamunotri—Gangotri and Yamunotri have already started drying up. There is no water. Whatever remains of the Ganges might be drained away soon. It is predicted that the Ganges will become a path of sand.

Disciple: Meaning there will be no more water in the Ganges?

Gurudev: No more water. Just as King Bhagirath brought the freshwater of the Ganges, now the seawater will push in. The river will be filled with saltwater. Many such things are mentioned. I request everyone: either come here to read the book or take a copy for 20-25 rupees, which is just the cost of the xerox. Read it and share it with others. This is for the purpose of spreading awareness. People are currently in the dark; they aren't aware. They think they are living in great joy, but if they read this, they will become conscious and perhaps turn toward God. We want people to know. I never had this opportunity before, but by the grace of Lord Jagannath, I have it now.

Disciple: Swami Achyutananda Das says he is the reincarnation of Vyasadeva.

Gurudev: Yes. The main point is the Battle of Kurukshetra. During that war, many great warriors couldn't even use their weapons because Lord Krishna's Sudarshan Chakra was the only weapon that truly mattered. Other weapons like the Pashupatastra or the Brahmastra were used and lost. Now, those same souls—the Pandavas and the Kauravas—have been reborn because they want to fight again. They feel their true heroism was never fully displayed.

Disciple: You mean at the end of the Mahabharata war, a part was left incomplete?

Gurudev: Exactly. Bhabisya Malika says their desires were not fulfilled. They were great warriors but couldn't fight to their full potential because the Sudarshan Chakra did everything. They have a grudge in their hearts. That is why they have been reborn in human form for this Third World War. Even Krishna has been born as the Kalki Avatar. His companions from the previous eras have also taken birth. They will manifest very soon. They will participate in this war and defeat Britain. They will go to Britain and defeat those who looted India, including the Kohinoor diamond. Everything that was stolen from India will be returned.

Disciple: Gurudev, Bhabisya Malika says that out of 145 crore Indians, only 33 crore will survive. And out of 800 crore people worldwide, 750 crore will perish.

Gurudev: Yes, only about 63 to 65 crore people will remain on Earth. Out of those, 33 crore will be in India and the remaining 30 crore will be spread across the rest of the world. European countries will become like deserts. Many places will be completely wiped out. Pakistan will be totally destroyed.

Disciple: It's amazing, Gurudev. This was written 600 years ago. When the authors wrote this, Pakistan didn't even exist, yet Pakistan is mentioned by name.

Gurudev: Yes, many things that didn't exist then are mentioned and have now come into being. This is why I request everyone to read Bhabisya Malika. It's a small expense for a great wealth of knowledge for the next seven years.

Disciple: What is the way to be saved from these sins, Gurudev?

Gurudev: For those who have sinned, death is inevitable within these seven years. But those who take refuge in God, or have already surrendered themselves to the Lord, will be saved. Those who are wicked, who mock these warnings as "nonsense" or "fake"—the atheists—they will face great suffering.

Disciple: They have already started suffering. The consequences of their sins are beginning to show.

Gurudev: Yes, we can see it now. People who commit theft, fraud, and other crimes cannot sustain themselves for long. I repeat my request to everyone: read this book. It is a treasure given to us by the grace of Lord Jagannath. It contains the prophecies for the next seven years.

Disciple: We will end here, Gurudev.

Gurudev: Yes.
