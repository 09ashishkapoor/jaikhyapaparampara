[00:00 - 00:08] (Sound of a conch shell blowing)

Disciple: Gurudev?

Gurudev: Yes, tell me.

Disciple: You have already explained the rituals for Saptami, Ashtami, and the Sandhi Puja. But one small thing was left out—perhaps you forgot. Could you please recite the Anjali Mantra for Mahashtami? It would be very helpful for everyone.

Gurudev: Oh, that! It is quite a long mantra, you know. It’s a great mantra, but listen, I will recite it for you.

For those who will offer Pushpanjali (floral offerings) to the Mother, go to her with a pure heart. That is all that is required—a pure heart. Whether you have bathed or not, or whatever else may be, does not matter. If the mind is pure, everything is pure.

“Apavitrah Pavitro Va Sarvavastham Gato'pi Va...” Whether one is impure or pure, or in whatever state one may be, he who remembers the Lotus-Eyed Lord (Pundarikaksham) becomes pure both externally and internally.

So, there is no such thing as being "impure." If you remember God once, even in your old clothes, it works. This is the main thing: you must go to the Mother with a pure heart. You cannot go with a mind full of tangles and malice—thinking of hurting someone, catching someone, or worrying about money. These things won't do.

If you can clear your mind of these, then offer Anjali to the Mother. Otherwise, there is no need. Don’t go to the Mother with poison in your heart.

Disciple: The heart must be pure.

Gurudev: Absolutely. Only that. Purify your heart, and you will see how much joy you feel while giving Anjali.

I will recite the Anjali Mantra now. It is long, but I will say it. Everyone, first bow down to the Mother. Stand before her, clear your mind, and let your soul become one with the Mother. Are we ready? Now, repeat after me:

[The Mantra Recitation]

Gurudev: Namoh Apavitrah Pavitro Va Sarvavastham Gato'pi Va Yah Smaret Pundarikaksham Sa Bahyabhyantarah Shuchih Namoh Vishnu, Namoh Vishnu, Namoh Vishnu!

Namoh Madhavo Madhavo Vachi Namoh Madhavo Madhavo Hridi Smaranti Sadhavah Sarve Sarva Karyeshu Madhavam Namoh Madhavam, Namoh Madhavam, Namoh Madhavam!

Namoh Sarva-Mangala-Varanyam Varadam Shubham Narayanam Namaskritya Sarva Karmani Karayet.

Now, take a flower in your hand. Take a wood apple leaf (Bel Pata) and a basil leaf (Tulsi Pata). Hold them together in your folded hands before the Mother and say:

Namoh Ayurdehi Yashodehi Bhagyanti Bhagavati Dehi Me Putrang Dehi Dhanang Dehi Sarvan Kamanshcha Dehi Me.

Namoh Bhagavati Bhayachhede Bhavabhavini Kamade Shankari Kaushiki Tvam Hi Katyayani Namostute.

Esha Sachandana Gandhapushpanjali Sayudhaya S-vahanaya Sa-parivaraya Shri Shri Bhagavati Durgayai Namoh.

This means we are offering Anjali not just to the Mother, but to the weapons in her hands, to her lion (the carrier), and to her entire family. Everyone receives this offering.

(He directs the followers to place the flowers at the Mother's feet and take fresh ones for the second round.)

Gurudev: Now, for the second time, take a flower and leaves and say:

Hara Papam Hara Klesham Hara Shokam Harasokham Hara Rogam Hara Khobham Hara Maring Harapriye.

Namoh Kali Kali Mahakali Kalike Papaharini Dharma Artha Mokshade Devi Narayani Namostute.

Esha Sachandana Gandhapushpanjali Sayudhaya S-vahanaya Sa-parivaraya Shri Shri Bhagavati Durgayai Namoh.

(Again, he directs them to offer the flowers and take fresh ones for the third and final time.)

Gurudev: Namoh Kayena Manasavacha Karmana Yatkritang Maya Gyanagyatama Kritang Papam Durge Devi Hara-durgati.

Namoh Mahishagni Mahamaye Chamunde Mundamalini Ayu Arogyam Vijayam Dehi Narayani Namostute.

Esha Sachandana Gandhapushpanjali Sayudhaya S-vahanaya Sa-parivaraya Shri Shri Bhagavati Durgayai Namoh.

The Anjali Mantras are complete. Now it is time for the final salutation (Pranam) to the Mother:

Om Aing Namoh Durgam Shivan Shantikarin Brahmanin Brahmanapriyam Sarvaloka Pranetrinca Pranamami Sadambikam.

Mangalam Shobhanam Shuddham Nishkalam Paramangkalam Vishveshvarim Vishvamatam Chandikam Pranamamyaham.

Ishana Mataro Devi Vishvari Ishvaripriyam Pranatosmi Sada Durga Sangsara-taranim.

Namoh Ayurdadatu Me Putrang Dehi Sada Shive Dhanang Dehi Mahamaye Narasinghi Yasho Mama.

Andhang Kushtancha Rogancha Shokancha Darunah Bandhu-Svajana Vairagyam Durge Me Hara-durgati.

Namoh Rajyatashcha Pratishthacha Lakshmitashcha Sadashthira Prabhutvam Jashya Samarthakam...

Dhanya Aham Kritakula Aham Saphalang Jivitam Mama Agatasi Jato Durge Maheshwari Mamalayam.

Argham Naivedyam Malyam Malayavasinim Grihana Varade Devi Kalyanam Kuru Sarvada.

Rajadware Mahaghore Devadware Cha Parvate Sarvato Rakshaman Devi Durge Devi Namostute.

Namoh Sarva Mangala Mangalye Shive Sarvartha Sadhike Sharanye Tryambake Gauri Narayani Namostute.

Namoh Srishti-Sthiti-Vinashanang Shaktibhute Sanatani Gunasraye Gunamaye Narayani Namostute.

This completes the offering at the feet of Shri Shri Maha Durga. Namoh Sri Hari, Namoh Sri Hari, Namoh Sri Hari!

Now, we should perform a little Hari-Naam Sankirtan.

Hare Krishna Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna Hare Hare Hare Rama Hare Rama, Rama Rama Hare Hare.

In this age of Kali, there is no other way but the chanting of the Holy Name. No other worship is as necessary—just chant the name. At the end of our Durga Puja, you should all chant this yourself. This 16-word, 32-letter mantra will be your salvation. This completes the Anjali.

Disciple: You all just heard the Mahashtami Anjali Mantra. Those who are staying at home can offer their Anjali by listening to Gurudev’s recitation.

Gurudev: Yes, those who cannot go to the pandals due to illness or other reasons can simply play this and listen, and their Anjali will be accepted.

Disciple: Gurudev, regarding Anjali, there is a tradition of bathing and fasting before the ritual. Is that necessary?

Gurudev: There is no such hard rule. I have never seen anywhere in the scriptures where God says, "Worship me by starving yourself." One should not cause pain to the body to perform worship. Eat something—just don't eat a massive, heavy meal that gives you indigestion or discomfort during the prayer. Eat something light and then perform your puja. This idea of strict fasting is a misconception. I do not agree with it.

Disciple: Thank you, Gurudev. You’ve heard it all, everyone. Enjoy the festival, stay healthy, and stay well.

Gurudev: Yes, indeed.

Disciple: Respects to you, Gurudev.

Gurudev: Blessings to you.
