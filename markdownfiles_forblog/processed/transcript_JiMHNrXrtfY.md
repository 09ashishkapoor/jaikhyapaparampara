(00:54) Speaker 3 (Guru): Yes, they are saying one thing: initiation is done, now give us the instruction. What do I mean by instruction? Instruction means living honestly and purely. To live honestly and purely—that is instruction. Initiation was given in the name of the Mother, and I say instruction is to live honestly and purely. Behave as saints do, live as they do. Do not deceive people, do not mislead them, and do not behave dishonestly toward others—this is instruction. Maintain these principles properly. Work hard honestly and maintain a virtuous mindset; I say this repeatedly—this is instruction. Initiation is a matter of God, and it has already happened. (To the woman) You say something.

(01:51) Speaker 4 (Woman): Namaskar. I had a great desire to receive the initiation mantra from Baba, and today that desire has been fulfilled. Having received the mantra from Baba, I am very, very happy. I feel very good. I want to live honestly throughout my life and never go down the wrong path. My only request to the Guru is that he blesses me so that I never wander onto the wrong path. May I live a happy domestic life with my husband and children, and remain a servant at the feet of God and the Guru for the rest of my life. This is all I want. Domestic life is a great virtue.

(02:33) Speaker 3 (Guru): Yes, do that. Do that. There is no point just staying at the Guru’s feet. The real matter is to live honestly and purely, to stay well, always remember God, and keep God close to you—that is enough; there is no need for anything more. You are householders; what can householders do? Can you live in the cremation grounds like us? Can you perform sacrificial rituals (hom-yagna) in the cremation grounds? No, you cannot. What you can do as householders is always remember God. Call upon God. I can tell you this much—God exists. If you call Him properly, you will find Him. You might not see Him, but He will walk beside you as a friend. He will stay by your side as a friend and help you without you even knowing it. He will stay by your side and do much for you, but you won't realize it. This is the law. Common people do not understand this; they ask, "Why didn't it happen to me?" That is not the point. Actually, after taking initiation, one must also take the instruction. He told me, "Give me the instruction. You gave initiation, now give the instruction." It is true; he said a good thing—simply taking initiation is not enough; one must also take the instruction. And instruction is nothing else but living honestly and purely. Do not deceive people, work hard honestly, earn money honestly, and live honestly—this is what we say repeatedly. This is our instruction. Is that clear? There is nothing above this.
