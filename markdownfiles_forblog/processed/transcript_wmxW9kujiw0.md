Once, King Bali initiated a massive *Yajna* (fire sacrifice). Narayana saw this as the perfect opportunity. He arrived in the form of a Vamana (a dwarf Brahmin) and asked King Bali for a few things. The demons' priest tried to stop Bali, saying, "No, there is no need to give anything right now. A Yajna is going on." But Bali did not listen to the priest, his wife, or his relatives. He got up straight away and went to the Brahmin, believing he couldn't proceed with the ritual without offering what a guest asked for.

Bali went to Narayana and asked, "What do you want?" 
Narayana replied, "I want three paces of land. Just three steps of space on the ground."

King Bali laughed it off and said, "Alright, that’s not a big deal. It shall be given."

Then, to crush King Bali's pride, the Vamana-form Narayana revealed His true cosmic power. What happened with the first step? Narayana assumed a gigantic, universal form. With His first step, He covered the entire Earth (*Martya*). With His second step, He covered Heaven (*Swarga*). Then, He produced a third foot from His navel and asked Bali, "Where should I place this foot?"

Bali humbly bowed his head and said, "Place it on my head, right on my crown." King Bali then started chanting Narayana's name intensely. Narayana was highly pleased.

At that moment, Prahlada arrived and requested Narayana to spare Bali. Narayana replied, "Yes, certainly. I will spare him; there is no problem. By placing my foot on his head, I will push him beneath the earth into the underworld, and he shall remain a *Chiranjeevi* (immortal). He will not die."

Accordingly, King Bali is still beneath the earth, alive by Narayana's blessing. Vishnu gifted him *Rasatala* (the underworld) for his residence and use.
