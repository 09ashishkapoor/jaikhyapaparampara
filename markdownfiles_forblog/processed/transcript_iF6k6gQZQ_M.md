Disciple: That covers the natural side. What about the general situation for people? Many people come to you concerned about their jobs and businesses.

Guru: You already know how many people lost their jobs and businesses in 2025. In 2026, whatever is left will also go. It will be impossible to save. Young people are rising, and the old "bosses" will lose their influence.

Disciple: That brings up an old question: Is science a curse or a blessing?

Disciple: What about the talk of "aliens" or a new species?

Disciple: What about biological or atomic warfare?

Guru: Yes, there is hope. I tell the young people: study hard, stay on the right path, and don't lose your way. Better days will eventually come for you. Don't be impulsive. Keep God in your heart. Focus on God and your work will progress. If you keep God with you, you will not face loss; you will find gain.

When the financial crunch begins, there will be no escape. Jobs as we know them will vanish. AI will take over every single job. This will be a massive disaster, and repairing the damage will cost everything.

My final word is this: keep God with you. Only God has the power to make things right. No human has that power.
