**Interviewer:** So, one progresses bit by bit through various births?

If I ask you your age, you might say fifty or fifty-five. But can you tell the age of your soul? You cannot. A person’s current life is a result of their past actions—from the previous birth, the birth before that, and so on. We look back through seven lifetimes.
