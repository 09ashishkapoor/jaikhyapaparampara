Are the four Dhams on the verge of destruction? || What is the signal again? || by Shyama Khyapa

**Host:** Welcome, viewers, to the Gupta Sadhak YouTube channel. Today’s video is being filmed at Gurudev’s residence. Given the current weather conditions, it isn't advisable for Gurudev to be outside, as it could affect his health. Therefore, we have been filming our recent videos here. However, he will be visiting the temple soon, and we will resume filming in that natural setting then. 

To our new viewers, welcome to the Gupta Sadhak Shamakapa YouTube channel, and a heartfelt thank you to our long-time friends. Greetings, Gurudev.

**Gurudev:** Greetings, my son.

**Host:** Gurudev, there is something I’ve been wanting to ask. Given the current global instability we’ve witnessed over the last three or four years—the conflicts, the struggles—what should we be doing right now to find a way out or seek deliverance from these situations?

**Gurudev:** Well, listen. I don’t know if you’ve read the *Bhabishya Malika*. Much is detailed there regarding what could happen in the future and the current state of the world. As you can see, there is widespread disease, suffering, and conflict. In West Bengal, there is strife in every household—envy, malice, and greed have escalated tremendously. 

Consider our education system, which is the backbone of the nation. Those who were supposed to oversee and control it are now in jail. It’s hard to even imagine. The very people entrusted with the education system are behind bars. This hasn't happened without reason; it’s the result of dishonest actions. I have never seen or heard of such a state of affairs in education in my life. It’s deeply saddening. The backbone of our nation has been broken. The current leaders don't seem to care; they are indifferent. Many are in jail, and more are heading there. It’s tragic, as they are people from our own society, but their actions have led to these consequences.

I’m reminded of a story. Would you like to hear it?

**Host:** Of course, Gurudev.

**Gurudev:** There was a boy named Gopal. After his mother passed away, he was raised by his aunt. When Gopal grew up a bit, he was sent to school. Gopal developed a habit of stealing pencils and pens from his classmates and giving them to his aunt. Instead of correcting him, his aunt was delighted and said nothing. As Gopal grew older, he began stealing from the homes of friends and neighbors. He continued giving his loot to his aunt, who would joyfully feed him and encourage him. 

One day, he was caught by the police and sent to jail. While sitting in his cell, he thought, "If my aunt had stopped me back then, I wouldn't be here today. She supported my actions." He decided to call for his aunt. When she arrived, he told her he wanted to whisper something in her ear. As she leaned in, he bit her ear off. When she cried out in pain, he said, "If you had warned me when I was young, I wouldn't be rotting in jail today. You made me a monster, and this is your punishment."

The same is happening now. If our leaders had stepped in and stopped the corruption when it started, they wouldn't be in jail today. Their honor would be intact. This is what saddens me.

**Host:** Returning to our main topic—the global situation of the last few years—is there a way to find relief?

**Gurudev:** There is no easy way out. Lord Mahadev himself has said that rituals and traditional worship (*puja-path*) will not suffice now. The only path forward is *Harinam Sankirtan*. Chant the names constantly—the 16 names and 32 syllables. Do not worry about worshipping various deities; they will not accept it right now. They have departed from this world of corruption and sin for their respective abodes like Vishnuloka or Shivaloka. God cannot reside amidst such deceit. 

I had someone come to me recently—a known thief. He asked me to perform a ritual at Tarapith and offered to pay whatever it cost. I told him, "I will not support you. You can rot in jail for all I care; you are a thief." He left in anger. There is so much sin that even God chooses to stay away. God is pure and transparent; he cannot remain where there is so much evil. That is why Lord Shiva said that for now, set aside the rituals. This is not the time for them. The only way is the *Maha-mantra*: 

*Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare*
*Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare*

In this Kali Yuga, there is no other way. The end of the age is near. We are standing at a crossroads, transitioning from Kali Yuga to a new era. When the influence of Kali reaches its peak, it will affect the minds of all people. Kali does not just destroy with weapons; he influences the mind. It is said that Kalki will be born and will be mentored by Parashurama, an avatar of Lord Narayan himself. He will be taught the use of weapons and the principles of ethics. 

You might know of the great wars—the Ramayana and the Mahabharata. Where did those divine weapons go? Where is Krishna’s Sudarshan Chakra? These weapons, like the Brahmastra, are still here on Earth, buried within the soil. They are beginning to resurface. The Sudarshan Chakra has been found, as have ancient swords. These are so powerful that even a touch can be fatal. They will all emerge from the Earth on their own; no one needs to dig them up. The Sudarshan Chakra, the Panchajanya conch—everything belonging to Narayan will reappear and be reclaimed by the Kalki avatar. Kali is coming with immense, terrifying power.

**Host:** You mentioned that the influence on people's minds will lead to great changes. Are we already seeing this?

**Gurudev:** Yes, it has already begun. You see people going to jail—that’s just the beginning. You have no idea what is coming. We hear about the pandemic—how it started and how it’s returning. I’ve heard that in China, ten million people are affected, and the death toll is beyond counting. The same people who created COVID-19 are now suffering because of it. China will face immense devastation. Another variant, BF.7, is emerging, for which doctors have no cure.

**Host:** Will this affect the entire world?

**Gurudev:** It is predicted in the *Bhabishya Malika* that 600 million people will perish. This is not my word; it is written by the five saints who authored the prophecies. Everything they predicted has come true. Recently, lightning struck the famous Bagala temple, a center of ancient wisdom. This is an omen. 600 million people will die from this disease. It has already started in China. I’ve seen images of bodies lined up with no one to count them. 

Kali has begun his work. Ancient weapons will resurface. These are not ordinary; a Brahmastra can devastate everything within a thousand-kilometer radius. People say I’m just trying to scare them—especially the younger generation who don't believe.

**Host:** We received comments a few months ago saying you were just spreading fear, but events are already unfolding.

**Gurudev:** Those who criticize will be the most affected. I am not trying to frighten anyone; I am warning them. It is my duty, as someone in my position, to alert people. I cannot stop the destruction; that is Kali’s work. 

The *Bhabishya Malika* also mentions that India will be united. The current political structures will change, and the RSS (Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh) will play a significant role. A "Tyagi" (renunciant) leader will emerge to protect India and create an undivided India (*Akhand Bharat*). Nations like Pakistan will cease to exist as separate entities; thirteen nations will merge with India. There will be a war. It is even written that there will be a missile attack on Kalighat.

**Host:** I’ve heard that when Kalighat is attacked, India will take decisive action against those thirteen nations.

**Gurudev:** It will be a time of great upheaval. The deities will not remain silent. Pakistan is already impoverished, and many other nations will follow. Regarding this new variant, BF.7, we have been dealing with the pandemic since 2019. While our doctors are more prepared now, the situation in China shows how overwhelmed a nation can become. We must remain vigilant, or the consequences will be dire. 

In the end, only *Harinam Sankirtan* is the way. Lord Mahadev has explicitly stated this. Chanting the 16 names and 32 syllables should be on everyone’s lips and in their hearts at all times. He also suggested worshipping Adyashakti Mahamaya. 

A great dissolution (*pralaya*) is coming. I don't know exactly how it will manifest, but we are at a crossroads. The end of Kali Yuga will lead to a new age, but not before significant loss. The four great holy sites—Jagannath Puri, Badrinath, Tirupati, and Dwarka—will face destruction or be lost. We are receiving signs from Amarnath and Kedarnath as well. The end is near.

**Host:** Gurudev, your words are quite intense today.

**Gurudev:** I am a bit stirred up because of the current situation. Every person is living in fear, and I spend my days and nights thinking about this and wanting to warn people. If they choose to take it differently, that is their choice.

**Host:** Thank you, Gurudev. Pranam.

**Gurudev:** Pranam.
