These five elements work within you as long as you are alive, and they consume you from within. Ramprasad's realization was that the five elements are eating within the stomach. 'Shashan' is where the body merges with the five elements and the six enemies are destroyed. A person, no matter how great, turns into 1.5 kg of ash. I've lived in the cremation ground for many years. People talk about ghosts, but there are no ghosts or spirits. But when you can make your body a 'Shashan', destroying the six enemies, that's true 'Shashan'. But they don't die easily. Ramprasad said, "Mother, how can I manage the six enemies? I feel like killing them all with poison." But if you kill them, you die. The five elements will remain and consume you. We say, he merged with the five elements. These are the six enemies: lust, anger, greed, attachment, pride, and envy. They cause people to do various things, good and bad. They need to be controlled. Like electricity, it's 33,000 volts at the source, but we use 220 volts at home. We use a transformer to control it. If it increases, our appliances will burn out. Control is key. If one can control these six enemies and live with truth, they can see everything. One should never cheat, harm, or lie. If someone living with truth says something, it comes true.
