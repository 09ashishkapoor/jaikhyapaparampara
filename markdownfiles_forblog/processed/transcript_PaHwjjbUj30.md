Part 5: The Story of the "One Eye"

Gurudev: People think no one is watching, but one Eye is always watching. There is no escape. I once told a story about two friends who went to a great sage for initiation. The sage gave them each a pigeon and said, "Go kill this where no one—not a human, bird, or animal—can see you. If you do this, I will initiate you."

One boy was clever. He went to a secluded spot, covered himself and the pigeon with a thick shawl, and wrung its neck. He thought, "No one saw me."

The other boy traveled far, but every time he tried, he felt an eye watching him. The pigeon itself looked at him as if to say, "Why are you killing me just for initiation?" He couldn't do it.

They both returned. The first boy said, "Master, I killed it where no one could see." The Master asked the second boy why his pigeon was still alive. The boy replied, "Master, every time I tried, I saw an Eye watching me. I couldn't find a single place where I was truly alone."

The Master said to the second boy, "You are the one who is truly ready for initiation."

We think we are hidden, but the Divine Eye follows us everywhere. There is no escape from the results of our actions.
