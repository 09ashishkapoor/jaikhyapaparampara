Introduction: Vairagya as Preparation

We are back on yet another video, and the subject for today is Vairagya. Why is it important in the sadhana of Bhairava? Why is it important in the sadhana of Ma Kali? Why is it not merely a state of mind, but actually a preparation?

Vairagya is a preparation for the final journey to the Smashana (cremation ground). That is what it is. Vairagya is that state wherein the body burns before it is laid down on the funeral pyre.

If you were to ask anybody, they would say that it is just detachment. You are no longer identifying with your egos. You are just calmer. You are no longer attached to goals or desires. It is as if you are just at a rather relaxed state. That is not Vairagya.

Vairagya, in its true essence, is that you begin to burn before you enter into the Smashana. You are no longer identifying with the body because your Jiva (the individual soul) has now come to a state where it is in control of the ego.

"Ego" means your name, address, your passions, your hobbies, where you work, your email address, how somebody reaches you, what do they call you, what does your close friend call you, what does your wife call you, what does your mother call you. All this—the entire set of ideas together that the Jiva is now born as in this body—is ego. So, Vairagya is not merely detaching from all this. Vairagya is the realization that you are now beginning to burn this ego that is attached to the physical body before you step into the Smashana itself.

The Journey of the Jiva

This is the most important thing. This state is achievable in every path—be it a sadhaka of Sri Krishna, Sri Rama, or any deity. Vairagya has to set in; otherwise, any sadhana of any deity will not bear fruit.

But in the state of Bhairava, what happens is this: How does a human body, after its passing, get carried to the Smashana? It is usually a bit of an affair. People all gather. They cry for you. Some of them rejoice, thinking of the great memories. If you pass off as a very old man or woman, they think of your memories and smile about it as they walk towards the Smashana. That is how the general process happens. Then they lay you onto the funeral pyre. Then they all leave as your body burns.

Once everybody has left, that is when the Jiva—the confused Jiva that is floating above the dead body—now begins to realize the presence of Bhairava in the Smashana. This is the general understanding of the journey of the human body as it moves into the path of getting destroyed, and the Jiva moves on to its next life or purpose.

Vairagya is that state where you are able to go through this process, but before you are even dead. You are preparing for the death. You are preparing for it in such a way that when it comes, there is no discomfort, there is no shock. Vairagya is that state where the Jiva is now running the ego. So, ego is here, Jiva is here; as they come closer, there is a realization. And then, it is the Jiva that controls the ego. The Jiva is in the driver’s seat. That is the state of Vairagya.

The State of the Vairagi

What happens in the Smashana after everybody leaves? The body is alone. The confused Jiva who has just left the body, who is still tied to the body, has no understanding of what is happening. As the body burns off, it is suddenly free.

That state, you decide to achieve while you are alive. That is Vairagya.

And Vairagya is attained through the realization that the physical world around you can only give you "X" amount of knowledge about life. Your degree has only "X" amount of value. Your degree is no longer valid as soon as you step into your job itself. Then how is your degree going to be valid to understand the Jiva? Your degree is part of the ego. It is merely a few letters behind your name. It has absolutely no value to understand the purpose of the Jiva.

The Three Steps to Vairagya

Realization of Knowledge: The first step of Vairagya is realizing that what is around you physically is not the right knowledge. Right knowledge is the knowledge of the Jiva: to understand what and why you are born, what is Karma, why are you here, why are you born to this family, and where should you go.

Playing the Part: The second step is when you realize that you are born into specific situations and placed into specific conditions which you need to play. Giving up on those will lead to further karma and further rebirth. So you play the part. The Jiva now controls the ego. The ego runs, and the Jiva follows? No longer. Now, the Jiva is in the driver’s seat and the ego is just the passenger.

Sadhana: The third and most important one is the Sadhana. The sadhana is what gives both step one and step two. It is almost impossible to attain Vairagya without sadhana. Without sadhana, it is merely being disinterested in life itself. When you say, "I don't care about the outcome of anything, I am just living my life," that is because you are careless. When you say that you don't care about the outcome because you are not attached to the outcome, nor the process, nor anything to do with the entire process—that is Vairagya. You will not be happy when the result comes in your favor, nor will you be sad when the result is no longer in your favor.

The Purpose of Bhairava Sadhana

If anybody who doesn't go through the process of Vairagya lives a very egoistic, materialistic life, that person dies only after he is actually dead. Till then, he has lived as per his ego—his name, his family, his income, his property. This is all that he identified with; he never realized his Jiva. Such a person will only burn in the Smashana.

But once you attain Vairagya, you start burning your body while being alive itself. You are in the Smashana before you enter the Smashana. You realize that in the Smashana, when your body burns, you will be alone. The Jiva will be watching from above.

But for somebody who is already in the state of Vairagya and who started burning his body physically, realizing he is no longer attached to this... do you think the Jiva of this person will be confused when the body burns? No. Because the Jiva stopped identifying with the body long back. So in that dark, empty Smashana when all your relatives are leaving, that Jiva will not be confused. That is the purpose of Bhairava Sadhana.

The purpose of Bhairava Sadhana is to prepare you for your death. Not that you should die now, or look at your death in the next year. It is to prepare your Jiva for a state where it will not float above your body in a confused state, wondering why it cannot go back into the body ("I am so attached with my name, my job, my career, my wife, my house!"). That panic, that state of utter chaos, will not exist in the Jiva that has realized and come into the path of sadhana. That Jiva is ready. It was ready long before the body’s expiry date reached. It is absolutely waiting for that moment when the body burns and is then liberated.

Living vs. Dying

If someone was to ask me, "Are you living or dying?" I will tell you what I will choose. I would say I am dying. I won't say I am living.

Because ultimately, every single one of us watching this video—aren't we going towards the Smashana? Are you going towards the Smashana or are you going back towards your mother's womb? You are not reverse-aging. You are not born at 100 and becoming a baby and going back. No, you left your mother's womb. Life that was created at that point is now heading towards your death.

Vairagya is a state when you realize it is not about living right alone, but also dying right.

At that state of death, when your Jiva leaves your body, it is when you realize: "I lived well. I lived right. Now I am free. I am waiting for Bhairava. Where are you?" Everybody has left. Now you come to me. That preparation for that state is Vairagya.

Vairagya is not merely a two-day exercise. It is a permanent alteration between the relationship of the Jiva and the ego. The Jiva realizes that the ego is merely a physical existence and starts getting rid of the ego (which is the body) long before it enters into the Smashana for burning itself.
