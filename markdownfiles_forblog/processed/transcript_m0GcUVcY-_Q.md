At that moment, the Sun god (Surya) and the King of gods (Indra) were passing by. They are friends, you see. Their eyes fell upon this woman, and they were stunned by her beauty. They had never seen anyone so exquisite. Both gods felt a surge of energy, often referred to as a "Mani" or "Jyoti"—not a physical jewel, but a divine ray or essence.

Indra’s energy fell upon her hair, and Surya’s energy fell upon her neck. From the energy on her hair, Bali was born, and from the energy on her neck, Sugriva was born. So, Bali and Sugriva were essentially brothers. Bali grew to be exceptionally powerful.
