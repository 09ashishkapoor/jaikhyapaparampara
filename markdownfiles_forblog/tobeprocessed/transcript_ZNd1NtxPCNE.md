It’s a strange thing, but it’s all written in the Bhavishya Malika. It will happen; there is no doubt.

Follower: Even the farmers won't be able to farm.

Gurudev: Think about the potato farmers. They are crying in the fields. There is political interference there too, but they are crying. I don’t know what will happen next. The prices of all crops and grains will skyrocket because farmers won't cultivate anymore since they don't get a fair price.

The people of Puri know this, the priests know that the Rath Yatra won't happen for the next six years. These six years are critical.
