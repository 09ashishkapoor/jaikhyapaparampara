If they come from a cat's form, they will exhibit cat-like behavior. Ordinary people might not try to understand this or feel the need to, but it is a significant factor. When we are born, the form we come from is mentioned. It could be any animal.

Look at Ramakrishna Dev; he also had a dog. That dog would drink Ganges water and eat fruits and sweets—whatever was given to it. As far as I know, Ramakrishna said the same thing—that the dog was human.

Interviewer: So, because of the conduct and habits of the past life—which were good—the animal behaved that way when it was born?

Gurudev: Exactly. But I have observed that it’s not just "bad karma" that leads to an animal birth like a dog or a tiger. It’s a cycle. A person can be born as a human and then, due to bad karma, return to an animal form. It’s not guaranteed that because I am a seeker today, I won't be born as a dog tomorrow. People say good karma leads to a human birth, but that’s not always the case. I could be born as a dog, a tiger, a deer, or a snake.

When we prepare the astrological chart for a newborn, we state which form they have come from. It’s a cycle through the 8.4 million species. Even if I do good work now, it doesn't mean I will definitely be born human again. I could be a dog, a snake, a tiger, or a mongoose.

Look at the deeds of the saints. Someone once gave Bamdeb Baba a pair of wooden sandals (Kharam) to wear. Baba refused. When asked why, he said, “There is life within this wood. It was once a tree with a soul. How can I wear this?” He believed trees have life and even “blood” (sap). He never wore them. Trees have souls and can communicate like humans; there is no doubt about that.
