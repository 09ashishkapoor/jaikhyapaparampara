[0:11 – 0:46] Sanskrit Invocation (Mantra)

Om purnamadah purnamidam purnat purnamudachyate Purnasya purnamadaya purnamevavashishyate Om Shanti, Shanti, Shanti

Om sarve bhavantu sukhinah Sarve santu niramayah Sarve bhadrani pashyantu Ma kashchit duhkha bhagbhavet

[0:48 – 1:38] Spoken Prayer

"O Lord of Lords, Mahadev, I am making just one prayer to you. I am praying for one thing: that this disease is cured. May people be freed from this illness. I am remembering you every moment. You are there in the heavens; surely, you can hear my words. May this disease of the people be cured. This is my wish—that everyone stays happy.

As long as I am alive, I never want to see anyone die. That is why I am praying to 'Mrityunjay' (the Conqueror of Death). O Mrityunjay, free us from this disease! Eradicate this disease from the entire world. Let the world live in peace once again."

[1:39 – 1:53] Sanskrit Invocation (Reprise)

Om purnamadah purnamidam purnat purnamudachyate Purnasya purnamadaya purnamevavashishyate

[1:54 – 2:20] Spoken Prayer (Continued)

"This is it, nothing else. I am constantly praying for this to God. All the time. Every single moment, just this one thing. O Lord of Lords, Mahadev, you are the Creator, the Sustainer, and the Destroyer. I am praying to you; surely, you are in the heavens and listening. Please cure this disease, and may people find peace."

[2:21 – End] Chanting

Om Namo Narayan, Om Namo Narayan... (Repeated rhythmically)
