Here is the clean, punctuated, and formatted English transcript of the audio:

Disciple: Pronam (salutations), Gurudev.

Gurudev: Pronam, pronam.

Disciple: Gurudev, in today's society, the line between truth and untruth has blurred. Many people use the name of God to commit many wrong and untruthful acts. This is confusing people and leading individuals toward sin. Gurudev, what happens to those who harm others?

Gurudev: Look, God is established in Truth. Truth is God; God is Truth. Therefore, one cannot reach God through falsehood. If I try to reach Him by force, that is a different matter, but one can never reach God through deceit. I might lie, then go to God and offer a garland of 108 flowers or donate two thousand rupees—none of that will work. God does not look at these things. God never tolerates sinful behavior (Papachar).

God desires a person’s mind to be like that of a six-month-old child—one who understands nothing and is free from the 'Six Enemies' (Soro Ripu: lust, anger, greed, etc.), the five senses, and the five elements. God knows them, and it is them that He blesses and draws near. If you are possessed by the 'Six Enemies,' it is very difficult. That is why we say: go to God as a six-month-old child, and He will embrace you. But if you go to Him after being deceitful or behaving badly with others, He will not accept it.

Disciple: Today, people are running toward these untruthful people. For example, yesterday you received a phone call from someone asking you to perform a 'Ban Mara' (a harmful black magic spell). People are constantly trying to harm one another. Only they know what they hope to gain from such harm.

Gurudev: Look, God never harms anyone. When you use God as a tool to harm others, the consequences are terrifying. If not today, it will happen tomorrow morning. You are using God’s name as a shield to harm people, and the result is devastating.

You see this trend everywhere now—'Kala Jadu' (Black Magic). You won’t even know it’s happening, but black magic is being performed. The consequences are extremely dire. Some people do this for a bit of money, but the end result is horrific. One should not do these things. You cannot utilize God for sinful work. God is not present in sin or evil acts. If you try to involve Him in such things, you are inviting your own peril. God won't do anything; you will simply become powerless. Whatever spiritual strength you had earned will be destroyed. God will take it away because He never wants you to harm others.

God wants people to prosper and be well. 'Sarva mangala mangalye, shive sarvartha sadhike...'—this means we must pray for the welfare of all. If you pray for someone to get a car, you might at least get a bicycle! That is how it works. You should be for everyone's welfare, just as God is for everyone's welfare. We chant these mantras repeatedly, but we don't apply them in practice. Instead, we call upon God while committing all sorts of wrongs.

Look at the 'Hom' (sacrificial fire ritual) happening today. It is for the welfare of people; no evil is intended. If I try to do harm through a ritual, harm will surely follow—but to the one performing it. You have no right to harm anyone. And if you involve God in it, He will not spare you.

Disciple: But Gurudev, the tendency to do evil is so high in society today.

Gurudev: What good is coming of it? Nothing. It’s only getting worse. Malice has grown so much among people.

Disciple: People try to harm others through small tricks, making them eat things, making them go mad, or using spells.

Gurudev: What can these tricks really do? Do I have the power to harm you?

Disciple: Well, they don't do it themselves; they get others to do it—people dressed in red robes who do it for money.

Gurudev: I know there are people in red robes who do this for money. But go to their houses and see—they are never well. Their children might be disabled, or their wives might have left them. None of those who deceive people are well. I’m talking about those fraudulent astrologers who cheat people. One came to me yesterday and admitted it was all a scam to eat. They have no other way to survive, so they cheat. But you cannot cheat people in the name of God.

Trying to reach God by deceiving others is futile; it only accumulates sin. They are dying from their own actions. None of them are happy. If they were, I would say, 'Okay, they are doing well.' But they aren't. If you harm others, the harm will return to you. If you visit the homes of those who do these things, you will see the misery—illness, family strife, and pain. It's a 'profession' for them, but a miserable one.

Disciple: It has become a huge business now, with banners and festoons everywhere.

Gurudev: Let them do their business. We don’t want that. We don't do business with God. We keep God close to our hearts. Is God a commodity for business? People can do what they want, but that money will just go down the drain. I have seen many of these people come to me after losing everything.

Disciple: What should ordinary people do then?

Gurudev: Ordinary people should just call upon God. Who can truly harm whom? The person trying to harm you is already being harmed by God. Ordinary people should just pray. God listens. I am a very ordinary person, but when I call upon Him or the Mother or my Guru, He stands by me and removes the danger. I know this for a fact.

Disciple: People are very greedy and full of expectations.

Gurudev: Greed leads to sin, and sin leads to death. Those who are greedy today will perish tomorrow. Don’t blame God for it. People do it for money, but the end is terrifying.

Disciple: But we often end up going to these dishonest people out of greed ourselves.

Gurudev: You go there, so what can be done? God didn't tell you to go to a dishonest person. This is what's happening most often, and these frauds take advantage of it. But that shouldn't affect you. You must be honest.

Disciple: Gurudev, how can one distinguish between a holy person and a dishonest one?

Gurudev: You don't need to distinguish. You are only trying to know because of your own greed. Just call upon God. Why do you need to meet anyone? You only seek them out when you want something.

Disciple: Gurudev, could you explain the 'Eightfold Path' for ordinary people?

Gurudev: The Eightfold Path (Ashtamarga) is from Gautam Buddha. We use it in his name.

Right Action (Sat-karma): Whatever work you do must be honest. In the Gita, it says "Om Tat Sat"—be truthful. Every chapter of the Gita reminds us to be honest.

Right Vision (Sat-drishti): The way we look at the world must be pure. We should look at women as mothers or sisters. Our gaze should not be tainted by evil thoughts.

Right Resolve (Sat-sankalpa): Whatever we resolve to do should be for a good cause. We must make a vow to be truthful.

Right Speech (Sat-vakya): We use the same mouth to curse and to chant God’s name. Our speech should be used carefully, without hurting others.

Right Livelihood (Sat-jivika): Our profession, the way we earn our living, must be honest and not based on deceit.

Right Effort (Sat-prajatna): We must maintain our path with care and mindfulness. One who is honest is a saint, equal to God.

Right Mindfulness (Sat-smriti): The thoughts and memories in our minds should not be chaotic or negative. We should have positive thoughts.

Right Samadhi (Sat-samadhi): People often pretend to meditate for show. True Samadhi in the name of God is profound.

If a person follows these eight paths, they need nothing else. That is why I say: do good work, walk the path of truth, and do not cheat or harm others. The harm you intend for others is already waiting for you. People think they are getting away with harming others, but it becomes an addiction for them. They find joy in it. But go to their houses and see the lameness, the sickness, and the tears. Their 'Guru' taught them wrong, so they do wrong. But a good Guru gives good advice. We follow the path of a good Guru. There is no problem for anyone then.

Disciple: Pronam, Gurudev. Let’s end here for today.

Gurudev: Yes, let's end here.
