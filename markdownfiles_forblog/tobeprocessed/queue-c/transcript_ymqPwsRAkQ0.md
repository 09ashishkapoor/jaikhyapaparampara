A Tithi (lunar day) is formed within every 12-degree difference between the positions of the Sun and the Moon. In our birth charts, we find two types of Tithis: starting from the first day of the Shukla Paksha (waxing moon) until the 14th day followed by the Full Moon (Purnima); then the Krishna Paksha (waning moon) begins from the first day until the 14th day followed by the New Moon (Amavasya). First, we must see if the energy of the Tithi we were born under matches the energy of our Karana. If the energy of the Karana doesn't match, we will face many obstacles and difficulties in our life's journey and progress.
