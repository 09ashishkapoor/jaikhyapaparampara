We have performed the Maha Mrityunjaya Yagna for a long time—specifically for 1,000 consecutive days. It has benefited many people; we have saved many lives.

Host: Was that right before the COVID-19 pandemic?

Gurudev: Yes, just before Corona. We helped people suffering from terminal illnesses like cancer and AIDS through the Maha Mrityunjaya rituals. Many were saved and are still alive today.

Host: By performing the Hom and providing the Kabach in their names?

Gurudev: Yes. Preparing the Kabach is exceptionally difficult. It must be drawn after midnight in the presence of Lord Mahadev. One must draw the Yantra (mystical diagram).

Host: A Yantra?

Gurudev: Yes, drawing the Yantra and placing it in an amulet. There is a specific process. If someone wears it, death can be averted. Many people ask me for a Mrityunjaya Kabach as if it is as simple as buying sweets. But it is a serious matter. It must be drawn after midnight, following strict rituals, in the presence of Lord Shiva. It is then submerged in specially prepared water. Meanwhile, the Maha Mrityunjaya chant must continue.

Host: How many times must it be chanted, Gurudev?

Gurudev: At least 1,008 times, often more. The mantra goes:

"Om Jung Sah, Om Bhur Bhuvah Svah, Om Trayambakam Yajamahe, Sugandhim Pushti-Vardhanam, Urvarukam-iva Bandhanan, Mrityor Mukshiya Ma-amritat. Om Namo Maha Mrityunjaya Bhairavaya Shivaya Swaha Namah. Om Girijapataye Swaha, Om Ambikapataye Swaha."

We chant it this way. "Girijapataye" and "Ambikapataye" refer to the Lord of Goddess Durga. We have performed these rituals many times; we did it just yesterday. It is very soothing to hear, and if a terminally ill person listens to it constantly—perhaps a recording near their head—I have heard they can survive.

I once saw a young boy, about three or four years old, who was hit by a falling iron beam. I was called to perform the Maha Mrityunjaya chant for him every day. This was a long time ago, but that boy is still alive. It was a miracle. If you perform the Maha Mrityunjaya rituals regularly, following the correct lunar phases and planetary positions, you will see results. When you call upon God with devotion, He responds. In my personal experience, many people have been saved.

Host: Gurudev, is this Yantra drawn on Bhojpatra (birch bark)?

Gurudev: Yes, it is drawn on Bhojpatra. The ink is prepared with various special ingredients. The pen nib is sometimes a peacock feather or even gold. Once used, it must be discarded and cannot be used for anyone else—much like how a judge breaks the nib after signing a death warrant. It requires immense patience and devotion. The person performing it must follow a strict diet—no fish, meat, or eggs—for the duration of the ritual.

Host: So, did the Maha Mrityunjaya mantra originate from Lord Mahadev himself?

Gurudev: Yes, I agree with that. It is extremely powerful if done with sincerity.
