Usually, high-level saints can sense these things. Not only that, but they also get extremely anxious, as Bama Deb did. He feared that since Ma was unwell, she might even pass away. That was his sentiment. They treat Ma in that way and look at her in that light. For him, if Ma Tara passed away, it would be the end of him too. This happens with high-level saints. You might have seen that in the Jagannath Temple in Puri, when the Lord falls ill, he is given pots of herbal tonic. But that happens on a specific day. In Ma’s case, it wasn't like that. Gurudev suddenly saw through his yogic power that Ma was very sick and everything was becoming chaotic. So, he collected those leaves and vines, made a tonic, and gave it to Ma, saying she would get better. These might sound like stories now, and no one knows for sure if they are true or false, but it is a fact that Ma falls ill. And I am telling you, as a Tantric, it is possible to make Ma unwell. Gurudev: Perhaps some other Tantric came here—I didn't know. He might have asked, "Gurudev, can I sit in front of the holy pit?" and so on. In my simplicity, I said, "Yes, please sit." I couldn't explain to them what Bama Deb had done, nor did I feel the need to. I understood that Ma had fallen ill. So, we made an amulet and put it around Ma’s neck. We kept it there for a few days, and once she was better, we removed it.

Interviewer: Gurudev, I want to know how you realized Ma was unwell.

Gurudev: It can be felt through yoga.

Interviewer: Does her physical form show any changes?

Gurudev: Yes, it certainly does. There is no doubt about it.

Interviewer: What kind of changes did you see in our Ma Tara?

Gurudev: Ma fell ill. She wasn't moving, and so on. I have done this many times. I put an amulet on her, which caused a lot of commotion about why Ma would need one. Without even entering my house, I told my wife I was going back to the cremation ground (temple). I came back and saw a large lump on Ma's foot. It wasn't there before; I hadn't seen it when I left. Ma seemed to be in a lot of pain. So, I gathered some Dhutra and Akondo leaves, which grow here. I heated them over a fire and tied them to Ma's foot with a piece of cloth, like a bandage. In the morning, when I came and removed the bandage, the lump was gone. Her foot was perfectly fine. I was amazed. This is a true story. Many such incidents have happened or continue to happen, where Ma gives us a sign beforehand.

Interviewer: Gurudev, my question is, did anyone else see what you saw?

Gurudev: As I said, who would see Ma at midnight? I had finished my prayers and gone home. Only after reaching home did I feel that Ma was in pain. It was a feeling in my heart. I didn't know what kind of pain it was, so I rushed back to the temple. That’s when I saw the lump on her foot. I was worried. I took those leaves, heated them, and tied them to her foot. By morning, she was better. You can't explain these things, but they are absolutely true. I don't lie. A few days ago, a young man came to me and said his Guru in Kalighat told him to sit near Ma. A gentleman named Debjyoti Banerjee asked this question—whether Ma Tara falls ill.

People like us then say, "Ma, I am here, what is there to fear?"
