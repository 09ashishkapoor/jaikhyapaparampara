Summary of the Content

The Ratri Suktam: This hymn is a prayer by Lord Brahma to the Divine Mother (Yoga Nidra) to wake Lord Vishnu so he may defeat the demons Madhu and Kaitabha. It praises the Goddess as the supreme power behind creation, preservation, and destruction.

The Shanti Mantra: The concluding part is a famous Upanishadic peace prayer signifying that the "Whole" (the Divine) remains whole even when the "Whole" (the universe) emerges from it.
