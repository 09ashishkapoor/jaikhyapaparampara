Guruma's last journey

English Translation

0:00 - 0:15
We are informing you with great sadness that our most revered Guruma is no longer among us.

0:16 - 0:29
Today, at 7:15 in the evening, she passed away to the heavenly abode.

0:30 - 1:19
To those who wish to pay their last respects to Guruma, we inform you that tomorrow morning at around 9 o'clock, for a short period of about 30 to 40 minutes, Guruma's mortal remains will be laid out in front of the Mother's Temple at Gurudeb's spiritual grounds, Santikunja, Rajpur Great Crematorium. You may pay your last respects to Guruma there.

1:20 - 1:37
We are deeply saddened by this untimely passing of Guruma.
