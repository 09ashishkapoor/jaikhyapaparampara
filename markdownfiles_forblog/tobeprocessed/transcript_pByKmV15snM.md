Happy New Year 1432 || What did Gurudev say? || by Guptasadhak Shyamakyapa YouTube Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pByKmV15snM

Here is a clean, punctuated, and well-formatted transcript of the audio in English:

Interviewer: Greetings, Gurudev.

Gurudev: Greetings to you, my son. Sit down.

Interviewer: Since we are recording this video a day in advance—because it takes some time to edit and upload to social media—I wanted to ask you about the upcoming Bengali New Year.

Gurudev: Yes, the Bengali New Year is approaching.

Interviewer: We’ve heard so many things about what might happen. If I were to ask you in one word: how will the new Bengali year be?

Gurudev: Look, we are Bengalis. Why should we follow the English calendar? We will follow our own. But our "Bengali-ness" is fading. In the old days, people wore dhoti-panjabis, took baths with neem water in the morning, and visited shops for Halkhata (new ledger books). Those traditions are mostly gone now.

Interviewer: It is the year 1432 coming up.

Gurudev: Yes, 1432. If you add the digits—1, 4, 3, and 2—it totals 10. I don’t see this number as very favorable. The year starts on a Tuesday, and it is a year governed by Mars. It will be a year of fire and blood. Not just fire, but significant bloodshed. You can already see fire burning everywhere without reason—slums burning, houses burning.

Interviewer: Everything feels so restless.

Gurudev: Exactly. Look at what is happening in Murshidabad and other places—unforeseen events. They’ve even shut down the internet, so people don’t even know the full extent of what’s happening. The military and BSF are being deployed. Such incidents will continue to happen. This year will be defined by fire and blood. It has started even before the year officially began.

Interviewer: Gurudev, could you say something positive? Will people have to live in this state of terror all year?

Gurudev: If there was something good to say, I would. But 1432 doesn't look good. We are currently in the "Pishach Yog" (Age of Demons). We just had a lunar eclipse which added to this ill omen. This is how the year is beginning for India. You can't expect much good from it.

Interviewer: You mentioned "Pishach Yog"—and it’s true, people seem to be behaving more inhumanly lately.

Gurudev: That’s what I’m saying. There is a "demonic" presence in every household. I am scared, even as a spiritual person. I am worried about what is going to happen. We won't be able to stop it. I tell everyone who comes to me: this is a bad time. Even our spiritual power has been restricted by God because this is a Mahapralaya (Great Cataclysm).

Interviewer: A cataclysm?

Gurudev: Yes. Even Brahma, Vishnu, and Maheshwar might not stop this. Great saints and figures from the Treta Yuga—like Hanuman, Ashwatthama, and Parashurama—have reappeared to assist in this transition. This is a time of immense conflict. Preparation for World War III has already begun. Even Trump is pointing toward it.

Interviewer: A Third World War?

Gurudev: It will involve nuclear weapons. It won’t be a conventional war. People will grow weak; there will be respiratory issues as oxygen levels deplete. It has already started. I saw a heartbreaking video from Gaza—a small child was praying to God to take him to Paradise (Behesht) because he heard there is plenty of food there. Imagine that! He wanted to leave this world just to eat.

Interviewer: That is truly tragic.

Gurudev: There are signs everywhere. People are seeing figures on horses in the sky. These supernatural signs suggest the time of the end is near.

Interviewer: If this cataclysm is inevitable, and you say your powers are restricted, what can we do?

Gurudev: God has told us: "Do not interfere. Just watch." We aren't supposed to rescue anyone. Everyone will face their own fate. If you try to interfere with destiny now, you will be the one who suffers. I see it every day—people come to me with stories of unrest between brothers, fathers, and sons. It’s everywhere.

Interviewer: What else do you foresee for 1432?

Gurudev: There will likely be a massive tsunami. Underwater volcanoes are waking up. In India, military preparations are visible. In Bengal, things are gradually moving toward military oversight.

Interviewer: That is a very grim outlook for the future.

Gurudev: It is what it is. I don't want to scare people, but the more we discuss it, the scarier it gets.

Interviewer: Despite all this, I hope people can find some peace in the New Year.

Gurudev: We all want people to be well. But it’s out of our hands. At our temple, we are performing rituals—Homa, Yajna, and Narayan Puja—to seek balance. We are doing everything we can for the welfare of humanity.

Interviewer: So, the only way forward is through prayer?

Gurudev: Yes. Call upon God. That is the only path left. God will return in the form of Kalki, joined by the immortals like Ashwatthama and the Saptarishi, to settle things.

Interviewer: I won’t make this video any longer. We will end it here.

Gurudev: Yes, there’s no use in spreading more fear. I pray to the Almighty, to the Fire, and to the Universe for everyone’s well-being, though I do not know what lies ahead.

Interviewer: Thank you, Gurudev. I wish everyone a peaceful New Year despite the challenges. May there be prosperity and health for all. Greetings, Gurudev.

Gurudev: Blessings to you.
