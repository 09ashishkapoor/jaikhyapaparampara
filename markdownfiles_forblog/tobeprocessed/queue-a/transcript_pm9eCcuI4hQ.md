This is a transcript of the audio conversation between a disciple and a spiritual teacher (Gurudev), discussing current global conflicts, geopolitical tensions, and local religious observances.

Disciple: Greetings, Gurudev.

Gurudev: Yes, greetings. Tell me.

Disciple: Gurudev, currently, if we think on a macro level, or a large scale, the entire world is heated up. It is as if World War III is almost upon us.

Gurudev: It is like an iron pan. Just as an iron pan becomes red hot when placed on a fire, that is the current state of the world. We have been hearing about Russia-Ukraine for a long time. Now, the Middle East…

Disciple: Yes.

Gurudev: You are making a mistake; it has been repeatedly said that the war will start in the Middle East. The third World War will originate from the Middle East. What has been happening between Russia and Russia-Ukraine was their internal conflict; they fought, they bombed, whatever. But it has actually been predicted that at the end of 2024—around October, November, or December—the war drums will sound from the Middle East. And it has already begun.

Disciple: Currently, Israel is fighting on two fronts—on one side Lebanon, and on the other, Iran. We are hearing about the heartbreaking situation and the loss of life in this war. What is your opinion on this situation?

Gurudev: My opinion is that the Third World War will happen just like the Second World War did; there is no doubt about it. Slowly, everyone will take sides—one group here, another there. This is how it will be.

Disciple: Gurudev, the Russia-Ukraine war has been going on for almost two years. The world is already divided. The current situation with Israel has made that division even more obvious. Even in the UN Security Council meeting, Israel is explicitly stating who their enemies are and who their allies are—who is a blessing and who is a curse. In this context, India will eventually have to take a side.

Gurudev: It has been said that India will get involved in this war. India will be drawn in, and there will be an attack. Now, it is just a matter of time.

Disciple: Another thing, Gurudev—we saw a small example of this in Bangladesh recently. Currently, they seem even more anti-India than Pakistan. They are making open comments in the media and in the US.

Gurudev: The main issue is that Durga Puja is the highest festival of our Hindu Sanatan religion. There, they have issued a "fatwa" that Durga Puja cannot be performed, and anyone who does so will be fined or killed. That is what has been said. They even talked about changing the flag of Bangladesh. Actually, they have no real leader; Yunus is the main figure, but there is no one else. There are one or two Chief Justices, but there is no clear leadership.

Disciple: Many say that behind this—as it is said of every war, whether the First or Second World War—the US, directly or indirectly, lights the first spark. In the case of Bangladesh, many are suspicious of this, though no one is commenting properly yet. After the current power shift, when the leader went to the US, he was given many awards and was seen embracing Pakistan and passing comments.

Gurudev: It was demonstrated that if those who were hostile to India befriend each other, why wouldn't Pakistan befriend Bangladesh? Everyone betrayed India, starting with the British, yet they are called "friends of India." If this is the case, we have to accept that Bangladesh and Pakistan are shaking hands. There is nothing more to say.

Disciple: Recently, France was the first to support India's inclusion as a permanent member of the UN Security Council. Now, the UK is also supporting it. It is being said that the UK is finally bowing down to India.

Gurudev: I tell you, they have to bow down to India; India must be given extreme value. Not only that, it is said there will be a bombing, a blast, in which 4.5 million people will die. This is unofficial; I heard it from a source. I don’t know if it’s true or false, as there is no proof, but it has been said.

Disciple: Gurudev, regarding the situation with neighboring countries like Bangladesh—they are in need of beef. For them, beef is the main food staple. Now they are suggesting an exchange: "We will give you jute, you give us cows." This is the kind of exchange being proposed.

Gurudev: A conflict is brewing, and it will eventually lead to trouble over cows.

Disciple: And recently, there was the issue where they blamed India for the floods. These things are happening continuously.

Gurudev: This has been going on for a long time. It happened during Sheikh Hasina’s time and before that with Khaleda Zia. Anti-India sentiment has always been there, but they cannot survive without India.

Disciple: We talked about global and national issues. Now, within our state (West Bengal), many things are happening, and Durga Puja is approaching. Will people be able to handle all of this?

Gurudev: It has already been said that this time, Durga Puja will not happen properly. This is because on the day of Ashtami, the Sandhi Puja falls at 5:00 AM. Usually, Ashtami lasts until Sandhi Puja, after which Nabami begins. So, can Durga Puja happen at 5:00 AM? People are saying to do it "mentally" or with limited rituals. But if the sun hasn't risen, the puja isn't fully possible. There is a special ritual called Ardharatri Bihita Puja which we perform at midnight by waking the Goddess, but other rituals will be hindered. The Ashtami Anjali cannot be given; people will have to give the Nabami Anjali.

Disciple: People who don't observe anything else usually give the Ashtami Anjali. Now even that is hindered. It seems like a divine plan. Also, there is fear regarding the second wave of Monkeypox in Kerala.

Gurudev: It will take a massive form. It is highly contagious. So far, it hasn't reached Kolkata, but two cases have been found in India. This could turn into another pandemic.

Disciple: There was also news about a Chinese submarine sinking, and if it had nuclear weapons, the hazard would be immense. China hasn't officially disclosed anything yet.

Gurudev: If there is a blast, all marine life will be destroyed. You haven't seen anything yet. Your hair will stand on end when you see what is coming. We can't even sleep at night thinking about these things. The nuclear war drums are beating. Nine countries are nuclear-armed.

Disciple: And Iran might join them, and Bangladesh might join Pakistan as a nuclear-allied country. If even one blast occurs, the whole world could be turned to dust. When someone is at their end, they might do anything. Like Israel or Russia.

Gurudev: "If I die, everyone dies"—that is the mentality. There will be massive loss of life. It cannot be stopped. Humans will do it to themselves.

Disciple: You said so many people would die. How many will remain? And what will be the role of Kalki?

Gurudev: According to the Bhavishya Malika, the world's population will drop from 7 billion to 670 million. India’s population of 1.1 billion will drop to 300–330 million. That is how it will end. Many people say Kali Yuga is ending, but Kali Yuga is supposed to last 432,000 years. Only 5,000 years have passed. You will be born at least 50 more times in this Kali Yuga.

Disciple: There were calculations about how much the age of Kali Yuga has decreased due to sins.

Gurudev: I don't agree with those calculations. Kali Yuga will take its time. This destruction you see will be temporary. It won't last for ages.

Disciple: So this isn't the final end of Kali Yuga?

Gurudev: People gossip about it on YouTube, which makes me laugh. We look at the Bhavishya Malika and understand what it says. But we aren't skipping 5,000 years to reach the end immediately. The war will end, but it will be devastating while it lasts. The Middle East will suffer the most. Everyone will have to take a side because the countries there are land-locked and not as geographically secure as India with its mountains and seas.

Disciple: Also, there is news of 900 Kuki militants entering. There will be more deaths. Since COVID, death has become a very natural topic for people.

Gurudev: World wars never lasted forever. They lasted a year or two and then ended. The current situation during the Puja season is also bad. The Goddess is arriving in a "Ghot" (pot), which signifies destruction. Idols are being stopped from being taken out in processions.

Disciple: You have repeatedly mentioned the Goddess coming in a "Ghot."

Gurudev: The consequences will be felt shortly after she leaves. I have one request for everyone for the upcoming Puja: please do not bring me any clothes or gifts. I already have enough to distribute. I want people to come empty-handed. If anyone brings clothes, I will throw them into the pond. I am being very clear about this. We have enough clothes to last two or three years. Let’s use what we have first.

Disciple: Whatever you think is right, Gurudev.

Gurudev: I hope people follow this. If not, the pond is right there.

Disciple: That is all for today, Gurudev. My respects.

Gurudev: Yes, respects.
