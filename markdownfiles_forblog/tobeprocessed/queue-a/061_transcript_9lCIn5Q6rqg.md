Today's keyword is Para Sanatani. Somebody says, "Are you Shaiva?" Say, "I am not Shaiva." Somebody asks you, "Are you a Vaishnava?" "I am not a Vaishnava." "Are you a Shakta?" "I am not even a Shakta. I am above all that. I am the Para Sanatani." That is Ma Adya Kali. Vaishnava, Shaiva, Shakta—these exist in the four heads of Brahma. And for Kalika, Adya Kalika, Adya Maha Kali... you are Para Sanatani. Nothing exists on planet Earth that is not her path. Even from Hip-Hop, she is there.

Jai Ma.
