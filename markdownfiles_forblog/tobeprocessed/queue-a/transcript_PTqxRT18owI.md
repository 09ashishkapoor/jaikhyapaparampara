He said that in this age of Kali (Kaliyuga), elaborate ritualistic worship is not strictly necessary. Don't worry about complex rituals; they won't be as effective. Just chant this one mantra loudly—perform "Nama Sankirtana." If possible, "Nagar Sankirtana" (chanting in the streets) is very good. We used to see Nagar Sankirtana on the day of Holi, where many people would walk through the streets together chanting the holy name. Now, it's almost extinct. She said, "I love Tamala because it's dark like Krishna." Then they worried that crows or vultures would eat her body. She said, "Let the crows eat every part of my body, but let them leave my two eyes so I can still catch a glimpse of Krishna."

This intense devotion, this "Arati" of Krishna's vision... she was desperate to see him.
