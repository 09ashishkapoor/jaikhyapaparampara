Many people have been coming to me, saying that the disturbances in their families are not subsiding. They say there is unrest every day, and they can no longer live at home like this. For them, I have a simple and direct suggestion: find out where a white Lajjabati (Sensitive Plant/Mimosa) is. It must be a Shwet Lajjabati (white variety); no other color will work. It usually grows in the wild, but ensure it's white.

Pull out the root of this plant—be careful, as it has many thorns—and soak it in a special mixture of water. This mixture should include Gochona (cow urine), Gomoy (cow dung), Ghrito (ghee), Dadhi (yogurt), Modhu (honey), and Kushodok (water infused with Kusha grass). Once the root is soaked, place it on the head of an idol of Lord Mahadev (Shiva).

After this, tie the root with a white thread. Boys should wear it on their right hand, and girls on their left. This will significantly reduce family unrest and bring peace to the household. I repeat, it must be the root of a white Lajjabati tree. There are many varieties, but only the white one will work.

The process should be done carefully. You must bathe early in the morning, as you shouldn't touch these things without bathing. Go to the tree, offer your respects, and then pull it out. After soaking it in the holy water mixture and offering it to Shiva, you can wear it. There are no dietary restrictions or other rules to follow.

While anyone can pull the root, it is generally better if a boy does it. It can be done on any day, but you must do it with a Sankalpa (a firm intention or vow). You must clearly state why you are doing this ritual. After the offering and the prayer, wear it, and you will see the benefits. That is all.
