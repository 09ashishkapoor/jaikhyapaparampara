This transcript provides a translation and well-formatted version of the dialogue between the disciple and the spiritual teacher ("Gurudeb") from the provided audio.

Disciple: Salutations, Gurudeb.

Gurudeb: Salutations, son. Please, sit down.

Disciple: Gurudeb, our world is currently bearing the marks of unimaginable loss and damage in the name of COVID-19. Furthermore, you have provided various forecasts of disasters before. But what way is left for us to be delivered from this impending catastrophe?

Gurudeb: Even I don't feel like I have a solution for this deliverance. I am in deep thought regarding this because one calamity just passed in Uttarakhand, and now two consecutive meteors have fallen. There will be more meteor falls. According to the scriptures, these are very bad signs.

In my 70 years of life, I haven't seen such large meteors. We’ve seen meteors and meteor showers before, but they didn't cause damage. However, these two recent meteor falls were terrifying. Perhaps it is God's infinite mercy that people are being saved repeatedly. But more meteors will come; they haven't stopped yet. A massive one is approaching, and it will fall on Earth. Two have already landed, but the big one is yet to come.

According to our scriptures, these falls will happen. Another concern is "Solar Storms." If a solar storm begins—and my third prediction is precisely that—it will be a terrible storm from the Sun. It could cause massive damage to satellites and everything we have in space.

Furthermore, humans are meddling with the Moon and Mars. This is not a good thing. We are seeing images of Mars' soil and atmosphere thanks to science, and we hear that the USA is trying to set up a nuclear plant on the Moon by 2030. I don't know what the result will be. These planets have power. If they exert their power in return, the effects will fall on Earth and its people.

Mars is responsible for the well-being of the Earth. In astrology, if Mars is poorly positioned, we say the person won't have a stable family, children, or business. Now, science is "hitting" Mars and the Moon. They won't let it go; nature will take revenge on Earth.

Disciple: Regarding COVID-19, many think it has gone away, but the numbers are rising again in Bangladesh, India, and other countries.

Gurudeb: Yes, people think it's gone, but it hasn't. People have become careless—they don't wear masks or use sanitizer. They've lost their fear. But one person's negligence will cause five others to die from the virus. This is very worrying.

Disciple: The current temperature and heat we are seeing are also unusual, aren't they?

Gurudeb: Absolutely. Nature has become very volatile. The Sun is the source of everything, but its heat is becoming fierce. In this month of Chaitra, it feels like a furnace. I don't know what will happen in the coming months of Baishakh and Jyaistha. The temperature is already 38–39 degrees Celsius.

Disciple: Because of this global warming, an iceberg is moving toward the UK.

Gurudeb: Yes, that is even more dangerous. It might even submerge parts of Britain. Last time there was such heat, people in the UK stayed underwater to cool off. This time will be worse. Everything I’ve predicted is coming true.

Disciple: And that iceberg is thousands of kilometers long; it could sink the UK.

Gurudeb: I can't even imagine what will happen. I am surprised by what is happening to the Earth. The Uttarakhand disaster was heartbreaking. And the meteors have started. One fell in America and another near Australia. The one that is coming next is even bigger. I don't know how it will react or what the chemical impact will be. It has shaken the world.

Nostradamus said that in 2021, there would be a massive decline in humanity. His words have never failed. COVID is increasing in such a way that it could wipe out entire villages. There is no room in hospitals in Bangladesh or India. Even graveyards are running out of space. Many of my young friends and acquaintances have passed away. I am fearful of what is coming.

Disciple: Is there any way out of this?

Gurudeb: I am confused myself. However, I have a firm belief that the Great Souls and Sages in "Gyan Gunj" (a mythical spiritual abode) are still controlling things. Because they exist, there is still hope. In ancient times, thousands of sages meditated in Naimisharanya. Now, the focus has shifted to Gyan Gunj. Perhaps by Shiva's mercy, people haven't found the path there yet, as it moves and remains hidden. But the Great Sages there, who have lived for 2,000–3,000 years, are controlling the world. That is my only hope.

The world has never been this chaotic. We know of past world wars and disasters, but science is failing here. Science can go to the Moon and Mars, but it can't find a cure for COVID. People go to great hospitals in Vellore or abroad, and when they can't be cured, they come to me as a last resort. I wonder, has science reached its limit?

I tell them, "Call upon God." Only He can save you. As it says in the Gita, God appears to protect the virtuous and destroy the wicked. The Earth is currently shedding its weight because the chaos has increased too much. The "Satya Yuga" (Age of Truth) has begun. God will restore balance, perhaps by removing those who are sinful.

There is a battle between Truth and Falsehood. In the end, Truth will win. There is so much sin and falsehood everywhere—in education, in law, in every sector. A famous Bengali poem says, "Is there no thunder in your hard hand? End this violence. Give your protection to those who are helpless in this world."

Disciple: So, we should follow the path of solution you've indicated.

Gurudeb: There is still time. There are still good people in the world, which is why it continues to function. But if people don't rectify themselves, they will die like ants. It has already begun. I am not at peace; I feel unwell seeing all this. But call upon the Almighty, the One who is full of nectar and light. That is the only refuge left for humanity.

Disciple: Pronam (Salutations), Gurudeb.

Gurudeb: Pronam, son. Stay well.
