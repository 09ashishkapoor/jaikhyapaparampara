This is a transcript of the audio, translated into English and formatted for clarity.

Setting: A conversation between a spiritual leader (Gurudev) and his disciples regarding the Bhabisya Malika (an ancient book of prophecies).

Disciple: Gurudev, my respects (Pranam) to you.

Gurudev: Blessings to you, son.

Disciple: Gurudev, you have obtained the Bhabisya Malika that was sent from the Puri Temple. You are providing Bengali printouts of it here for the devotees who want them.

Gurudev: No, they sent it to us. It was sent to our YouTube channel and via WhatsApp. We are simply printing it and distributing it to everyone. We are only charging for the printing and the cost of the paper. However, many people do not want to believe in it.

Disciple: If you could tell us something about the Bhabisya Malika. Looking at the various natural disasters occurring now—like the terrifying storm we had just yesterday—were these mentioned in the Malika? Are these future events really going to happen?

Gurudev: Yes, they will happen, word for word. But you know, those who are atheists do not care for these things; they won’t believe it. Only one in ten million people will truly understand this. It is not possible for common people to grasp it. They will dismiss it as gossip or fantasy. I don’t deny that they will react that way.

Disciple: But it seems that much of what is written there has already started to manifest. The process has begun. Could you explain this in more detail?

Gurudev: Primarily, we had four holy sites (Dhams). In the Satya Yuga, it was Badrinath. In the Treta Yuga, it was Rameshwaram. Then came Dwarka. Due to the curse of Sage Durvasa, Dwarka eventually submerged under the sea. And now, we have Puri Dham. This is our current concern.

We knew a long time ago that Puri would eventually go under the sea. I have visited Puri many times—about 30 times—but I have never heard the roar of the sea quite like this before. Furthermore, I have never seen eagles, vultures, or crows flying over the temple spire (the Jagannath Temple) the way they are now. This was explicitly mentioned in the Bhabisya Malika.

Swami Achyutananda Das prophesied that these things would happen. He said vultures would perch on the temple and that meat-eating birds like eagles and vultures would sit on the holy flag. This has already happened. They are circling over the Lord’s head, which is a terrifying sign.

Following this, there will be a massive tsunami. Not only will the Jagannath Temple be submerged, but it is said that six surrounding districts will also go underwater. It has already begun; yesterday’s Cyclone Mocha was the start.

Look at Bangladesh—places like Cox’s Bazar and Chittagong. You cannot imagine how people are crying out to God, saying, "Allah, save me." Saint Martin's Island has been devastated and is submerged. It was a large island, and now many such islands are being swallowed by the sea. About 8,000 people lived there; only God knows what has happened to them. I saw reports of bodies floating like water hyacinths.

We believe that the Kalki Avatar has arrived. He is currently "imprisoned" within the Jagannath Temple, and until the temple is submerged by the rising waters, he will not be released. Even Lord Hanuman is said to be stationed there. Once they are released, the Kali Yuga will reach its end.

According to what we have studied, for six years, Lord Jagannath will not ride his chariot. These will be six devastating years. The chariot will not move. Within these six years, a total apocalypse will occur. What is happening in Bangladesh will happen everywhere. In places where there are great saints and holy men, they will try to hold it back, but otherwise, the world will face a helpless situation.

Disciple: Gurudev, a question arises: seeing how humanity has become so undisciplined and has crossed all limits, will the sages actually step forward to save them?

Gurudev: That is a different matter. The perspective of a sage is different from that of an ordinary person. Why would they intervene now? This is the time of the apocalypse. It began in December 2019 with COVID-19. That was the start. Now, the "Great Apocalypse" (Maha Pralay) is approaching. This will happen across the entire world. Countless humans and animals will perish.

The Bhabisya Malika states that only 630 million people will remain in the entire world. In India, only 330 million will survive. The rest will be gone. This will happen within these six years. Anyone could be among those who pass, including me. While the great sages will try to save the Earth, the world will be left scarred.

Disciple: Gurudev, is there any way to be saved?

Gurudev: The Bhabisya Malika says there is no other way but to chant the name of Shri Madhav (Krishna). Worship Krishna. That is the only path left open. If you seek salvation from the sins humanity has committed, this is the way. In the past, you might have been punished for a sin 10 or 20 years later. Now, you sin today and receive the punishment by tomorrow morning.

Disciple: Gurudev, humans are largely responsible for inviting this disaster. For instance, the Russia-Ukraine war, which some say was instigated by America.

Gurudev: America may have started it, but America will have to suffer the consequences. Every country is falling into a financial crisis. You see Pakistan before your eyes. Many countries will go bankrupt. Even West Bengal may not take long. We are hearing it will be like Sri Lanka or Pakistan.

Europe will be finished. Half of America will be underwater. There will be no crops in Europe next year—there will be no money and no food. By 2026, everyone will be embroiled in war.

Disciple: It seems this year there will be agricultural problems in India too, Gurudev.

Gurudev: Definitely. The weather is changing. There is drought. We will suffer as well. A financial crisis and a food shortage will hit simultaneously. I see very bad omens ahead.

Disciple: Aside from natural disasters, man-made conflicts are destroying the environment.

Gurudev: It is said that certain "bombs" will be released that contain germs or viruses beyond our imagination. Look at how the glaciers are melting because of these wars...
