### **Invocation and Benefits**
**Thuthipporku... Thanai**
For those who chant this, heavy karma and sorrow will vanish.
For those who hold this in their heart, wealth and courage will flourish.
Meditation and wisdom will be attained.
This is the Armor of Lord Skanda, bestowed by the Graceful One.

### **Visualization of the Lord**
**Amarar Idar... Mayil Vahananaar**
Focus your heart on the feet of the Lord who fought to end the suffering of the Angels (Devas).
Oh Lord Saravanabava, look upon us during this Sashti day.
Oh Wielder of the Red Spear who helps the righteous.
With anklets ringing on Your holy feet and bells chiming.
You arrive riding the peacock that dances to the music.

### **Calling the Lord for Protection**
**Kaiyil Velaal... Varuha Varuha**
Come with the Spear (Vel) in your hand to protect me.
Come, come, Oh Lord of the Spear.
Come, come, Oh Rider of the Peacock.
Praised by Indra and the guardians of the eight directions.
Come, come, Oh Lord with the mystic, powerful Vel.

**Vaasavan... Seekkiram Varuha**
Come, nephew of Vishnu, come.
Come, you who is loved by the daughter of the hunter clan (Valli).
Come, Oh Lord with six faces.
Come daily, Oh Lord who wears the holy ash.
Come quickly, Oh Lord of the Silagiri hill.

### **The Mystic Mantras**
**Saravana Bavanaar... Nirena**
Oh Lord Saravanabava, come instantly.
*(Chanting of the mystic seed syllables 'Ra', 'Ri', 'Du' invoking the Lord's vibration).*
Salutations to the brave warrior, Saravanabava.

### **The Armor (Protecting the Body)**
**Vasara... Ilanga**
Come, destroyer of the demons (Asuras).
In the hands of the young Lord who rules me...
Shine the twelve weapons and the goad.
Shine the twelve wide, graceful eyes.

**Virainthu... Olirum**
Come quickly to save me, Oh Lord of the Vel.
*(Chanting of mystic syllables 'Sau', 'Kili' for life protection).*
Let me be established in Your light forever.

**Shanmuhan... Chevvaayum**
Oh Six-Faced Lord, come daily as the Kundalini energy.
With your six faces and six crowns.
With holy ash on your forehead and long eyebrows.
With twelve eyes and coral-red lips.

**Nanneri... Maalaiyum**
With gem-studded ornaments on your forehead.
With earrings dangling from your twelve ears.
With garlands and medals on your twelve strong shoulders.
Wearing a chain of nine precious gems.

**Muppuri... Muzhanthaalum**
With the sacred thread and pearl necklaces on your chest.
With a beautiful stomach and navel.
With a silk belt shining on your waist.
With beautiful thighs and knees.

**Thiruvadi... Diguna**
With anklets sounding on your holy feet.
*(Chanting of rhythmic dance syllables describing the peacock's dance).*

### **Direct Plea for Protection**
**Munthu Munthu... Vinothanendru**
Rush, rush to the front, Oh Lord Muruga.
Oh Lord of the hills who rules me.
Grant your son the boons I desire with happiness.
You who enjoys the playful divine forms.

**Unthiru... Kaaka**
I hold your holy feet as my only certainty.
Place your feet on my head to bless me.
Protect the life within my life, Oh Lord.
Protect this child with your twelve eyes.

Let the Good Vel protect my nostrils.

Let the Red Vel protect my tongue.
Let the Ray-like Vel protect my cheeks.
Let the Sweet Vel protect my neck.
Let the Gem-studded Vel protect my chest.

**Serila... Kaaka**
Let the Holy Vel protect my chest area.
Let the Strength-giving Vel protect my shoulders.
Let the Great Vel protect my neck.
Let the Graceful Vel protect my back.
Let the Wide Vel protect my sixteen ribs.

**Vetrivel... Kaaka**

Let the Sharp Vel protect my reproductive organs.
Let the Great Vel protect my buttocks.

**Vattak... Kaaka**

**Munkai... Kaaka**

### **Protection in Time and Space**
**Yeppozhuthum... Kaaka**
Let the Vel always be in front to protect me.
Whenever I speak or move.
Come instantly, Oh Golden Vel, to protect me.
Protect me during the day, Oh Diamond Vel.
Protect me in the darkness of night, Oh Fire-like Vel.

**Yemathil... Thaakka**
Protect me against the God of Death and during all watches of the day.
Remove delays and protect me, Oh Clever Vel.
Protect, protect, Oh Golden Vel.
Look, look, and protect me in a second.
Attack, attack, and remove my obstacles without fail.

### **Destruction of Evil and Black Magic**
**Paarka... Ahala**
As you look at them, let my sins turn to dust.
Let witchcraft and great enemies vanish.
Let powerful demons and spirits of the sky...
Let the trouble-causing spirits and graveyard ghouls...

**Kollivaay... Oruvazhi Pokum**
Let fire-breathing ghosts and deceiving spirits...
Let all evil spells, mantras, and black magic buried in the house...
Let wealth, money, and offerings meant for evil...
Let them all fade away and disappear upon seeing me (your devotee).

**Adiyanaik... Kalangida**
Let enemies tremble and retreat when they see me.
Let deceivers come and bow down to me.
Let the messengers of Death be terrified if they see me.

**Anji... Sokku**
Let them shake in fear and run away screaming.
Tie them up with the rope of love.
Smash the obstacles, roll them away.
Let the enemies be stunned and confused.

### **Protection from Wild Animals**
**Kuthu... Oda**
Pierce them with your sharp Vel.
Let the Vel burn like the sun's fire and chase them.
Let the Vel fly and scare them away.
Let tigers, foxes, wolves, rats, and bears run away in fear.

### **Protection from Poison and Disease**
**Thelum... Iranga**
Let scorpions, snakes, and centipedes flee.
Let their severe poisons come down and leave the body immediately.

**Polippum... Arulvaay**
Let swelling, joint pain, headaches, paralysis, and bile disorders...
Let ulcers, colic, skin diseases, and abscesses...
Let all diseases vanish the moment they see me.
Grant me the grace that they do not stay.

### **Social Harmony and Relationships**
**Eerezhula... Mahizhnthura Vaahavum**
Let the fourteen worlds be my kin.
Let men and women be favorable to me.
Let the kings and rulers be pleased with me.

### **Phala Sruti (Conclusion and Benefits)**
**Unnai... Viduthaay**
I praise your holy name, Oh Saravanabava.
You who released the Devas from their hard imprisonment.
Oh Kanda, Oh Guha, Oh Lord of the Ray-like Vel.
You who destroyed the demon Idumban, Oh Sweet Vel Muruga.

**Kathirkaa... Tharase**
Oh Lord of Kathirkamam and Palani.
Oh Lord of Thiruchendur and Chengalpet.
Oh King of the Six Faces who lives in battle.

**Kaarar... Poothiyey**
While the Goddess of Knowledge dwells on my tongue, I sing of you.

**Nesamudan... Rakshi**
Wearing the ash with love, my karmic attachments are removed.
To attain your feet, grant me your grace.
Protect me with love and grant me food and wealth.
Grant me great prosperity, Oh Lord of the Vel.

**Sithi... Unkadan**
Let your devotee attain success and live with honor.
Long live the Peacock, Long live the Vel.
Long live the devotee, Long live the Guru.
It is your duty as a Guru/Father to forgive my mistakes.

**Petraval... Arulvar**
Like a mother forgives, and a father loves his son.
Accept me and grant that your devotees flourish.
Those who meditate on this Kanda Sasti Kavacam without doubt...

The rulers of the directions will grant them favors.

**Maatrala... Thadiyai**
Enemies will come and bow down.

This Armor of the Vel is the Truth.

**Vizhiyaal... Sankaa Rathadi**
Ghosts will flee just by seeing the devotee.

**Arintha... Potri**
Understanding this, I praise the feet of the Lord at Palani.

**Thiramihu... Potri**
Praise to the Divine Body.
Praise to the Great Hero.
Praise to Kanda, Praise to the Vel.
Praise to the King of the Golden Hall on the High Hill.

**Mayilnada... Saranam**
I surrender to the flower feet of the One whose peacock dances.
I surrender, I surrender, Oh Saravanabava Om.
I surrender, I surrender, Oh Six-Faced Lord.
