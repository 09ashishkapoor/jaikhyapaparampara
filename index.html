Frequently Asked Questions

What is Adya Mahakali Sahasranama?

Adya Mahakali Sahasranama contains 1000+ sacred names of Goddess Adya Mahakali, the primordial form of the Divine Mother. This free ebook compilation includes English translations, pronunciation guides, and spiritual insights for devotional practice.

What is Kalabhairava Sahasranama?

Kalabhairava Sahasranama contains 1000 sacred names of Lord Kalabhairava, the fierce manifestation of Lord Shiva who destroys fear and obstacles. Available as a free ebook with English translations and chanting guidance.

Are these spiritual ebooks free to download?

Yes, all ebooks from Jai Khyapa Parampara Temple of Knowledge are forever free. Knowledge is the birthright of the soul, and these sacred texts are offered as seva (spiritual service). No registration or payment required.

What is Khyapa Parampara?

Khyapa Parampara is a spiritual lineage originating from Tarapith, West Bengal, India. It includes revered saints like Guru Bamakhepa and Guru Shyamakhepa who embodied ecstatic devotion (khyapa means "mad with divine love") to Goddess Tara and Kali.

How do I use a Sahasranama for chanting?

Each ebook includes a chanting guide. Generally, you can recite the names daily as a devotional practice, ideally during morning or evening worship. The translations help you understand the meaning behind each sacred name, deepening your connection.

Will more ebooks be added?

Yes! This temple of knowledge is a living Sankalpa (sacred vow). More sacred works are being compiled and will be shared freely in service of Ma Adya Mahakali and the Khyapa Parampara.
