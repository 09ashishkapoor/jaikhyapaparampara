Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the texts, the lineage, and the way of chanting.

Who is Ma Adya Mahakali?

Ma Adya Mahakali is the primordial source, the power behind creation, dissolution, and transformation.

She is worshipped as the power beyond fear and limitation, the one who destroys ignorance, ego, and inner darkness. For the devotee, she draws the seeker toward truth and oneness.

Benefits of Chanting Adya Mahakali Sahasranama (1000 names)

Spiritual Protection

These names are chanted as a practice of protection, purification, and surrender.

Destruction of Internal Enemies

Devotees turn to the Sahasranama to work against ego, fear, anger, greed, attachment, and ignorance.

Removal of Fear

This practice is often taken up to steady the mind and strengthen the heart in times of fear and inner struggle.

Oneness with Maa Adya Mahakali (Moksha)

Through steady chanting and reverence, the seeker grows closer to Ma Adya Mahakali and moves toward truth and liberation.

Divine Grace and Blessings

For the devotee, the fruit of this practice is inner change, grace, and a deeper remembrance of her.

Who is Kalabhairava?

Kalabhairava

Kala: He stands beyond time and fear.

Bhairava: His form may appear terrible, but to the devotee he is a protector, guide, and Guru.

He is remembered here as fierce in form and compassionate in nature.

Roles and Qualities

  • He is remembered here as the Guru of Shakti Vidya.
  • He is a guardian of the path.
  • He cuts through fear, ego, and confusion.
  • He grants detachment and steadiness.
  • He supports the seeker on the way to Ma Kali.

Jai Kalabhairav Baba

Benefits of Chanting Kalabhairava Sahasranama (1000 names)
  • These names are chanted to awaken courage.
  • They deepen the Guru-tattva within.
  • They strengthen one's walk on the path of Dharma.

For the devotee, they offer support in fear, confusion, and inner struggle, and become a steady companion on the path of Dharma.

What is Khyapa Parampara?

Khyapa Parampara is a spiritual lineage rooted in Tarapith, West Bengal. It is associated with saints such as Guru Bamakhepa and Guru Shyamakhepa, whose lives were marked by intense devotion to Bhairava Baba, Ma Tara and Ma Kali.

What is Adya Mahakali Sahasranama?

Adya Mahakali Sahasranama is a collection of 1000+ sacred names of Ma Adya Mahakali. On this site it is offered through free ebooks, audiobooks, and articles, along with guidance for reading, pronunciation, and devotional use.

What is Kalabhairava Sahasranama?

Kalabhairava Sahasranama is a collection of 1000 sacred names of Kalabhairava. It is offered here through free ebooks, audiobooks, and articles for study and chanting.

How do I use a Sahasranama for chanting?

Each ebook includes simple guidance for chanting.

  • You may read the names slowly, imperfectly, aloud, silently, with Om, or without it.
  • Pronunciation deepens with repetition.
  • You may chant a few names, eleven names, or the full thousand in one sitting.
  • Understanding grows over time, but practice can begin at once.
  • What matters most is sincerity and repetition.
