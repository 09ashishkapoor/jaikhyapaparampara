Shri Kalabhairava Ashtottarashata Namavali
1. kalabhairavadevaya: The Divine Lord Kalabhairava, Revered Deity of Time and Transcendence.
2. kalakalaya: The Destroyer of Destruction, Lord Who Conquers Time Itself.
3. kaladandadhrite: Wielder of the Rod of Time, Upholder of Divine Judgment.
4. kalatmane: The Soul of Time, Pervading and Animating All Existence.
5. kamamantratmane: Embodiment of the Desire-Inciting Mantra (Kama Bija), the Essence of Creative Will.
6. kasikapuranayakaya: The Protector and Lord of the Sacred City of Kashi (Varanasi).
7. karunavaridhaye: Ocean of Compassion for His Devotees.
8. kantamilitaya: The Beloved, Ever-Residing in the Hearts of His Devotees.
9. kalikatanave: The Destroyer of all Strife and Calamities.
10. kalajaya: The Victorious Over Time, Transcending all Temporal Limitations.
11. kukkutarudhaya: Mounted upon a Rooster, Symbolizing Vigilance and Victory over Ignorance.
12. kapaline: Bearing the Skull, Symbol of Detachment from Worldly Existence.
13. kalanemighne: Destroyer of the demon Kalanemi, symbolizing the vanquisher of obstacles and ignorance.
14. kalakanthaya: One with a Dark Blue Throat, bearing the poison of the cosmic ocean.
15. kataksa'nugrahita'khilasevakaya: Whose Graceful Glance Bestows Blessings upon All His Devotees.
16. kapalakarparotkristabhiksapatradharaya: Holding aloft a skull-bowl as a sacred begging-bowl, symbolizing detachment and renunciation.
17. kavaye: The Supreme Poet and Knower of All Knowledge.
18. kalpantadahanakaya: Whose Form Consists of the Conflagration at the End of a Cosmic Cycle.
19. kalanidhikaladharaya: Adorned with the Moon, the Repository of All Arts and Knowledge.
20. kapalamalikaabhuosaya: Adorned with a Garland of Skulls, signifying victory over ego and mortality.
21. kalikulavarapradaya: Bestower of Boons upon the Family of Kala (Time), the Lineage of Destinies.
22. kali-kalavatii-diiksa-samskaaropasanapriyaya: Delighting in the initiation and purifying rites for those who comprehend the mysteries of Kali.
23. kalikadaksaparsvasthaya: Seated by the right side of the Goddess Kali, embodying fierce protection.
24. kalividyasvarupavate: Embodiment of the Knowledge of the Kali Age.
25. kaliikurcasamayuktabhuvanakutabhasuraya: Resplendent amidst "kaliikurca," the cosmic seed syllables that embody the essence of the universe.
26. kaliidhyanajapaasaktahrdaakaranavasakaya: Whose heart is filled with devotion, constantly chanting the sacred mantras of Kali.
27. kalikavarivastyaadipradhanakalpapadapaya: The primordial wish-fulfilling tree of immense growth, rooted in the very essence of Kali (Time and Sakti).
28. kalyugravasava braahmii pramukhacaryanaayakaya: The Leader of the host of Brahmi and other Matrikas, who is the Supreme Wielder of Power in the Kali Yuga.
29. kankalamalikadharine: Adorned with a Garland of Skulls, Symbolizing Victory Over Death.
30. kaamaniyajatadharaya: Adorned with matted locks charmingly intertwined with divine attributes.
31. konarekhastabhadrasthapradesabindupithakaya: The One Whose Sacred Seat (Pitha) is a mystical point on the intersection of the corner-lines of the auspicious quarter.
32. kadaliikaraviirakakanjhomarcanapriyaya: Delighting in the worship with Kadali (banana) flowers, heroic Karavira (oleander) blossoms, and Kanjha (lotus) flowers.
33. kurmapithadi saktisaya: The Divine Abode resting upon the cosmic tortoise and primordial energies.
34. kalakastha'dhipalakaya: The Guardian of the Farthest Extent of Time's Boundaries.
35. kathabhrave: The one who removes all obstacles and impurities through His terrifying roar.
36. kamasancarane: One who moves and acts according to His own will and desire.
37. kamaraye: The Enemy of Kama, the Destroyer of Cupid's Illusions.
38. kamarupavate: Possessing the Power to Assume any Form at Will.
39. kanthadisarvacakrasthaya: Residing in all the chakras, from the throat center upwards.
40. kriyadikotidiipakaya: Illuminator of the Innumerable Paths of Action.
41. karnahiinopavitapaya: Adorned with the Sacred Thread of the Earlobe, signifying Supreme Renunciation.
42. kanakacaladehavate: Adorned with a Body Resplendent like a Golden Mountain.
43. kandharakaradahaaragasabhaasura muurtimate: Possessing a splendid form, radiant with a necklace of skulls, an abode of ultimate bliss.
44. kapalamocanantaya: The Liberator from the Sin of Brahmahatya, cleansing the skull of delusion.
45. kalarajaya: The King of Time, who governs its very dominion.
46. kriyapradaya: Bestower of Actions and the Fruits Thereof.
47. karanadhipataye: Lord of All Causes, the Primal Source of Creation.
48. karmakarakaya: The Agent of All Action, Orchestrator of Karma.
49. kartrinayakaya: The Supreme Leader and Doer, Controller of All Actions.
50. kanthadyakhiladesahibhusanadhyaya: Adorned with all the directions, from the throat upwards, as His ornaments.
51. kalatmakaya: The Embodiment of Time, the Soul of All Ages.
52. karmakhandadhipaya: The Overlord of the Realms of Action and Destiny.
53. kilbisamocine: Remover of Sins and Liberator from Faults.
54. kamakostakaya: Dweller in the Womb of Desire, Grantor of All Wishes.
55. kalakantharavanandine: Rejoicing in the sweet melody of the nightingale.
56. karmasraddhavarapradaya: Bestower of the Boon of Faith in Action.
57. gunabhakirnagandharasancarane: Residing within the subtle essences of attributes and fragrances, pervading all.
58. gaumatismitaya: Possessing a Gentle Smile, radiating serene wisdom.
59. kinkinimannjunirvana-katisutaravirajitaya: Adorned with a Waistband of Tinkling Bells, Resonating with the Sound of Liberation.
60. kalyanakrtkalidhvamsine: The Benefactor who Destroys the Ills of the Kali Yuga.
61. karmasaksine: The Witness to All Actions, Observing the Deeds of All Beings.
62. kritajnapaya: Who is Known and Honored for His Deeds.
63. karaladamstraya: Possessing Frightful Fangs, Striking Terror into Evil Forces.
64. kandarpadarpaghnaya: Shatterer of the Pride of Kama, the God of Love and Desire.
65. kamabhedanaya: The Piercer of Desire and Remover of Lust.
66. kalaguruvilioptangaya: Whose Limbs are Adorned with a Garland of Dark-Hued Flowers, signifying His Majestic and Fierce Form.
67. kadaraartha'bhayapradaya: The Granter of Fearlessness, dispeller of all anxieties and dangers.
68. kalantikabharadaya: Bearing the End of Time, Symbolizing Ultimate Dissolution and Re-Creation.
69. kalibhaktalokaavarapradaya: Bestower of Boons upon Devotees of Kali.
70. kaminiikancanaabhaktamocakaya: The Liberator of Devotees from Attachment to Women and Gold.
71. kamaleksanaya: Whose Eyes are as Beautiful as a Lotus Blossom.
72. kadambarirasasvaadalolupaya: Rejoicing in the sweet taste of Kadamba flowers (or celestial liquor), an intimation of divine intoxication.
73. kanksitarthadaya: Bestower of Desired Boons and Fulfillment.
74. kabandhanavaaya: One Who Rids Devotees of Spiritual Obstacles and Bondage.
75. kamakhyakancyadiksetrapaalakaya: Guardian of the Sacred Places like Kamakhya and Kanchi.
76. kaivalyapradamandaaraya: Bestower of Absolute Freedom and Bliss, The One Who Grants Salvation.
77. kotisuryasamaprabhaya: Radiant like Ten Million Suns, Illumining All Existence.
78. kriyecchaajnanasaktipradiipakanalalocanaya: Whose fiery eyes illuminate the powers of action, will, and knowledge.
79. kamyadikarmmasarvasvaphaladaya: The Bestower of the Fruits of All Desired Actions and Deeds.
80. karmaposakaya: Nourisher and Sustainer of All Actions and Their Fruits.
81. karyakarananirmatre: The Creator and Regulator of Cause and Effect.
82. karagrihavimocakaya: The Liberator from the Prison of Existence, Releasing Souls from Bondage.
83. kalaparyayamulasthaya: The Root of the Cycles of Time and Transformation.
84. karyasiddhipradayakaya: The Bestower of Success in All Undertakings and Endeavors.
85. kalanurupakarmangamoksanaabhrantinasanaya: Dispelling the Illusion of Actions Corresponding to Time and the Fruits Thereof.
86. kalacakraprabhedine: Breaker of the Wheel of Time, Liberator from Cycles of Existence.
87. kalimmanyayoginiipriyaya: Cherished by Yoginis Who Reside in the Sacred Kali Mandala.
88. kahalaadimahavaadyaatalataandavalaalasaya: Delights in the Cosmic Dance of Dissolution, accompanied by the grand sounds of the Kahal instruments.
89. kulakundaliniisaktayogasiddhipradayakaya: Bestower of the Siddhis of Yoga through the Awakened Kundalini Shakti within the Family (Kula).
90. kalaratri maharatri sivaratryaadi karakaya: The Bestower of Kalaratri, Maharatri, Shivaratri, and other Auspicious Nights.
91. kolahaladhvanaye: The Lord Whose Roar Fills the Universe, Creating a Great Uproar.
92. kopine: The Wrathful One, Dispenser of Justice and Destroyer of Ignorance.
93. kaulaamargapravartakaya: The Originator and Propounder of the Kaula Path, the Esoteric Tradition.
94. karmakausalyasantosine: The One Delighting in the Skillful Execution of Action, the Perfection of Karma.
95. kelihasanalaalasaya: Radiant with the Effulgence of Playful Laughter.
96. kritsnapravrttivisvandapancakrityavidaayakaya: The Granter of All Fivefold Cosmic Functions of Creation, Preservation, Dissolution, Concealment, and Grace.
97. kalanathaparaya: Supreme Lord, Beyond the Bounds of Time.
98. kalaya: The Controller of Time and Destroyer of Illusions.
99. kaladharmapravartakaya: Establisher of the Laws of Time and Cosmic Order.
100. kulacaryaya: The Revered Teacher of the Clan, Upholder of Lineage Traditions.
101. kulacarataya: The Upholder of Family Traditions and Lineage.
102. guhvastamiipriyaya: Fond of the eighth day of the dark fortnight, particularly significant for His worship.
103. karmabandhakhilacchedine: The Severer of All Bonds of Karma.
104. gostasthabhairavaagrnye: Foremost among the Ganas, the Divine Chieftain of Hosts.
105. katoraujasyabhismaajnapaalakinkaarasevitaya: Served by Fearful and Mighty Messengers, Upholding His Stern Commands.
106. kalarudraya: The Fierce Rudra of Time, Whose Wrath Governs the Cosmic Cycle.
107. kalavelahoramsamurtimate: The Embodiment of All Time Divisions, from Kalas to Ahoras (Day and Night).
108. karaya: The Doer of All Deeds and Actions in the Universe.
